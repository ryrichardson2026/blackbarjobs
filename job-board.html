Frequently Asked Questions

What security officer jobs are listed on this board?

Most listings on this board are security officer roles: armed, unarmed, overnight, event, mobile patrol, loss prevention, hospital, and TSA positions, plus warehouse and logistics roles now live in select markets. Filter by vertical, role, shift, pay, and city. Jobs are pulled directly from employer postings across every market we cover and updated continuously. No spam listings.

Which markets does BlackBarJobs cover?

Active markets include Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, San Antonio, and Chicago, with more metros added as we expand. DFW is the largest market with 100+ dedicated job pages covering every role type and submarket. Use the location filter at the top of the board to jump straight to any city.

Do I need a license to apply for jobs on this board?

Most positions require a valid state security officer license, and requirements vary by state. Some entry-level and training-provided roles accept applicants who are actively pursuing their license. Each listing specifies the credential requirement. Working in Texas? See the Texas License Guide if you're starting from zero.

How is BlackBarJobs different from Indeed or ZipRecruiter?

BlackBarJobs is built exclusively for security officers. Every page, every filter, and every listing is specific to this industry, so there are no irrelevant results to sort through. The network is also candidate-first: we surface who's hiring and what they pay, so you can apply with context instead of blind.
