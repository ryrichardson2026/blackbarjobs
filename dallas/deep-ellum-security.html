What security jobs are hiring in Deep Ellum right now? +

Is Deep Ellum security dangerous? +
Deep Ellum has an active nightlife environment with the associated risks — alcohol-involved incidents, occasional fights, and high crowd density on weekend nights. It is not considered a high-crime assignment compared to hospital emergency departments or overnight industrial patrol, but it requires more active conflict management than most other Dallas security environments. Officers who are comfortable de-escalating confrontations and working in a fast-paced nightlife setting handle it well.
Do I need experience for Deep Ellum security jobs? +
Smaller Deep Ellum bars and venues are among the more accessible security employers in Dallas — they frequently hire candidates without prior security experience as long as they have a guard card and present professionally. Larger music venues and established clubs prefer candidates with prior nightlife or event security experience for their primary floor positions.
What hours do Deep Ellum security jobs run? +
Most Deep Ellum security shifts run from approximately 8pm to 2:30am on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday nights. Some venues start earlier for happy hour coverage on Fridays. Shifts are typically 6–7 hours with a guaranteed minimum pay period regardless of actual close time.
How do I find security work in Deep Ellum? +
Two paths work well: apply directly to the larger Deep Ellum venues that maintain their own security staff, and apply to guard firms that hold contracts with district venues. Signing up for job alerts here captures both direct-hire and contractor-managed Deep Ellum security openings as they're posted. The district's security community is relatively tight — getting one good venue on your resume opens doors to others.
What does Deep Ellum venue security pay? +
Entry-level door and floor security in Deep Ellum runs $15–19/hr. Experienced officers in lead floor or VIP positions earn $19–25/hr. Head of security and armed posts at larger venues reach $25–35/hr. Many venues pay a flat flexible rate for a guaranteed shift minimum rather than strict hourly.
Deep Ellum District

Security Demand in Deep Ellum

Deep Ellum is Dallas's primary live music and nightlife district — a concentrated stretch of Elm Street, Main Street, and Commerce Street east of downtown that hosts hundreds of bars, music venues, restaurants, breweries, and event spaces. On any given Thursday through Saturday night, the district draws tens of thousands of visitors and requires significant security staffing across its dozens of active venues.

Security in Deep Ellum is fundamentally different from commercial or corporate security work. The environment is high-energy, the crowds are dense, and the hours are late — most shifts don't end until 2am or later on weekends. Officers work entrance control, crowd management, and active conflict de-escalation in an environment where alcohol is present and situations can escalate quickly. The work is demanding, but it pays well and creates flexible scheduling flexibility that few other security categories offer.

Deep Ellum venue security is one of the most accessible entry points in the Dallas security market. Many smaller venues and bars hire security officers directly rather than through guard firms, and the requirements are often more flexible than corporate or healthcare security. A valid guard card, professional demeanor, and comfort in a nightlife environment are the primary criteria. Active hiring zip codes include 75226 and 75201.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available in Deep Ellum?

Deep Ellum security spans several role types depending on the venue. Larger music venues and concert halls hire officers for entrance control, bag checks, and floor monitoring during ticketed events. Bars and clubs hire door security to manage capacity, verify IDs, and handle ejections. Breweries and rooftop venues need crowd management and perimeter security on busy weekend nights.

Most Deep Ellum security work is flexible and event-driven — you work specific nights rather than fixed shifts. Thursday through Saturday nights are the primary demand window. Friday and Saturday nights at the district's major music venues can require 10–20 officers working simultaneously across entrances, floors, and VIP areas. Sunday through Wednesday demand exists but is significantly lower.

Officers who build a reputation in Deep Ellum's security community often work multiple venues across the same week — one venue's network connects to another's, and reliable officers get called consistently. For candidates who want flexible, above-average-paying weekend work in a lively environment, Deep Ellum is one of the best options in Dallas.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff venue entrances, checking IDs, verifying tickets, and managing capacity
  • Conduct bag checks and wand screenings per venue security protocols
  • Monitor crowd behavior on the floor, in the pit, and in general admission areas
  • Manage and de-escalate conflicts between guests before they escalate
  • Conduct ejections in accordance with venue policy and Texas law
  • Staff VIP and backstage areas at music venues requiring restricted access control
  • Conduct post-event property sweep before clearing the venue
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD officers assigned to the Deep Ellum district on escalated incidents

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required for all paid Deep Ellum venue security
  • Comfort working in loud, crowded nightlife environments — the Deep Ellum environment is genuinely demanding
  • Strong de-escalation skills — alcohol-involved conflicts are a regular part of the job in the district
  • Weekend (Thursday–Saturday) availability is the primary qualification — most Deep Ellum security work falls in this window
  • Prior nightlife, event, or venue security experience preferred at larger Deep Ellum venues
  • Physical fitness — officers may need to manage physical confrontations and stand for 6–8+ hours per shift
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Thursday through Saturday nights are the core demand window — most Deep Ellum venues are at capacity these nights
  • Shifts typically start 1–2 hours before venue opens and run until 30–60 minutes after last call
  • On weekends most Deep Ellum shifts run from approximately 8pm to 2:30am
  • Some venues also need early evening and happy hour coverage on Fridays
  • flexible structure typical — you get called for specific nights rather than set on a fixed schedule
  • Officers who build relationships across multiple venues can work 3–4 nights per week consistently during busy seasons
