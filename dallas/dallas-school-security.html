Do I need special training for school security in Dallas?+
A valid Texas Level 2 Security License is the baseline requirement. Some DISD and charter school positions require additional school safety or campus security certification. De-escalation training is strongly preferred across all Dallas school security employers. Bilingual English/Spanish proficiency is a significant competitive advantage given the demographics of DISD's student population.
What does school security pay in Dallas?+
DISD school safety officer pay runs $15–18/hr at entry level with a structured district pay scale. Experienced officers with tenure at the district earn $18–23/hr. Charter school and private school rates vary by operator. DISD positions include district employee benefits — health insurance, paid time off, and retirement contributions — which adds meaningful total compensation value above the hourly rate.
Is school security a good job in Dallas?+
Yes — particularly for candidates who value predictable daytime hours, summers off, and a community-oriented work environment. School security officers at DISD are district employees with structured pay scales and benefits. The role is one of the most lifestyle-compatible security positions in Dallas for candidates with families or other daytime commitments that make overnight and weekend security work impractical.
Does Dallas ISD hire security officers directly?+
Yes. DISD hires school safety officers as district employees — not through contracted guard firms. This means DISD school security officers receive district employee benefits including health insurance, retirement contributions through TRS, and the district pay scale. Applications are submitted directly through the DISD HR portal.
Can I work school security in Dallas with no prior security experience?+
DISD and most Dallas charter school security programs prefer candidates with prior security, law enforcement, military, or youth services experience. The combination of campus safety responsibilities and daily interaction with students makes school security more demanding than many entry-level security roles. However, candidates with strong youth-oriented backgrounds — coaching, youth ministry, teaching assistant — are often competitive even without a formal security resume.
Dallas School Security

School Security Demand in Dallas

Dallas Independent School District is one of the largest school districts in Texas — operating over 230 campuses serving roughly 145,000 students across Dallas. DISD's campus security program employs hundreds of school safety officers, and the district consistently maintains active hiring for security positions across its elementary, middle, and high school campuses. This makes DISD the single largest employer of school security personnel in the Dallas area.

Beyond DISD, Dallas supports a large charter school network — including Uplift Education, KIPP Texas, and Dallas Can Academy — as well as numerous private and parochial schools. Each operates its own campus security program at varying scales. Texas state legislation following school safety incidents has driven increased investment in campus security staffing across all school types, making this one of the fastest-growing security employment categories in Dallas over the past several years.

School security positions in Dallas are distinctive in their schedule structure — they follow the academic calendar, which means summers off or reduced hours, and standard school-day shifts rather than overnight or weekend coverage. For candidates who value predictable daytime hours and a community-oriented work environment, school security is one of the most lifestyle-compatible security roles in the Dallas market. Active hiring zip codes are distributed across all Dallas ISD attendance zones.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does a School Security Officer Do in Dallas?

School security officers in Dallas manage campus access, monitor student common areas, respond to behavioral incidents, and serve as the primary safety presence on campus during the school day. The role blends traditional security functions with a strong community and de-escalation orientation — school security officers interact with students, parents, staff, and visitors throughout the day in a way that requires both authority and approachability.

At larger DISD high school campuses, school security officers may work in teams covering multiple access points, cafeteria monitoring during lunch periods, and hall monitoring between class periods. At elementary campuses, the role tends to focus on perimeter access control and visitor management. Charter school security varies significantly by campus size and operator.

DISD school safety officers with strong performance records have a structured advancement path within the district's security department. The district also has relationships with Dallas PD for school resource officer programs at high school campuses — creating a pathway for school security officers interested in law enforcement careers.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control campus access points — verify visitor IDs and enforce campus visitor policies
  • Monitor student common areas including cafeterias, hallways, and outdoor common spaces
  • Respond to behavioral incidents, fights, and disturbances on campus
  • Conduct de-escalation with students, parents, and community members
  • Coordinate with school administration and Dallas PD school resource officers on serious incidents
  • Monitor campus perimeter for unauthorized individuals during school hours
  • Write incident reports for all security events for district documentation
  • Support campus emergency drills and lockdown procedures as the security lead

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required — some DISD positions require additional school safety certification
  • Clean criminal background check — schools conduct thorough background screening including fingerprinting through the Texas DPS

  • Holiday breaks and teacher in-service days typically follow the campus schedule
  • Most predictable daytime schedule of any security role type in the Dallas market
