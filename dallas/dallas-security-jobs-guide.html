The Dallas Security Market

Dallas is the largest city in Texas by security employment complexity — not just by volume, but by the sheer range of industry types, neighborhood security profiles, and employer categories packed into a single city. The downtown CBD houses some of the most demanding corporate and government security accounts in the state. The Medical District around Parkland and UT Southwestern is one of the largest healthcare security employment zones in Texas. Deep Ellum and Uptown generate active nightlife and event security demand every Thursday through Saturday. The industrial corridors along the Stemmons Freeway and South Dallas I-20 provide consistent overnight patrol demand. And the suburban residential corridors across North Dallas and the surrounding neighborhoods create a massive apartment gate and HOA security market that runs 24 hours a day.

This diversity is what makes Dallas genuinely different from Fort Worth, Houston, or Austin as a security market. No single employer category dominates. Corporate security and homeless outreach security, luxury hotel patrol and overnight warehouse guards, Level I trauma center officers and event venue staff — all active, all paying different rates, all requiring different skills. Candidates who understand the Dallas market's structure can target the most appropriate entry point for their background and advance strategically from there.

Dallas security employment is anchored by two major direct-hire opportunities that define the top of the market: Parkland Health, where security officers are Dallas County employees with government benefits, and the downtown corporate security departments of AT&T, whose global headquarters anchor the CBD's premium security employment tier. Everything else in Dallas security exists in the large and active market between these anchors and the entry-level gate security that serves the city's residential base.

Dallas security pay averages approximately $18.37/hr (~$38,219/yr) for unarmed positions based on current market data — above the Texas statewide average. The range within Dallas spans from $14/hr at entry-level residential gate positions to $80+/hr for direct-hire corporate security management, depending on license level, account type, and employer.

Armed Security (Level 3)

Dallas's armed security market is anchored by its downtown banking corridor, the federal courthouse complex, corporate campus security at AT&T and other Fortune 500 headquarters, and an active overnight armed patrol market covering commercial and industrial properties across the city. The Level 3 commission unlocks these accounts — all of which pay $5–15/hr above equivalent unarmed positions. Dallas is also one of the more active executive protection markets in Texas, driven by the concentration of high-net-worth individuals and corporate leadership in the Turtle Creek, Preston Hollow, and Park Cities areas.

Dallas Security Pay by District

Pay in Dallas security is shaped more by account type and district than by raw experience level. Here is what candidates can expect across the major Dallas security environments in 2026:

  • Downtown CBD corporate and government security: $18–45/hr depending on armed/unarmed status and employer type. Direct-hire corporate security at Fortune 500 HQs reaches the top of this range.
  • Medical District (Parkland direct hire): $17–38/hr. The government benefit package adds significant total compensation value above the hourly rate at Parkland specifically.
  • Uptown luxury residential and hotel: $17–30/hr. Premium residential and boutique hotel accounts pay above the Dallas average for unarmed positions.
  • Deep Ellum nightlife: $16–25/hr per-diem. Above-average hourly for the environment and hours required.
  • South Dallas / Stemmons industrial overnight: $15–25/hr with $1–3/hr overnight differential. The entry point for most new Dallas security candidates.
  • North Dallas residential gate: $14–20/hr. High volume, consistent availability, accessible to entry-level candidates.
  • Dallas Love Field airport corridor: $17–32/hr. SIDA badging requirement adds a premium over standard unarmed rates.

The highest total compensation in Dallas security — including benefits — is at Parkland Health (direct county employment) and AT&T's in-house corporate security department. Both require documented experience and professional track records to access, but offer compensation packages that guard firm employment cannot match at equivalent seniority levels.

Major Dallas Security Employers

Parkland Health — Dallas County

Parkland is the most sought-after security employer in Dallas for candidates pursuing long-term career stability. Security officers are Dallas County employees with full government benefits — health insurance, Dallas County pension contributions, structured pay scales, and annual increases. The Parkland emergency department is the highest-acuity healthcare security environment in the city, handling trauma volume consistent with a major urban Level I center. Applications go through Parkland's HR portal directly. These positions are competitive — prior security, military, or law enforcement experience is essentially required.

UT Southwestern Medical Center

One of the nation's leading academic medical centers, UT Southwestern employs security officers across its large campus complex adjacent to Parkland in the Medical District. The combination of research hospital, clinical outpatient operations, and university campus creates a diverse security environment that covers access control, parking structure management, campus patrol, and clinical area security across multiple buildings and shifts.

AT&T — Global Headquarters

AT&T's global headquarters campus in downtown Dallas maintains a large in-house corporate security department. Corporate security positions at AT&T represent the top of the commercial security market in Dallas — Fortune 500 employee benefits, structured career development, and compensation that reflects the company's scale. These positions are highly competitive and typically require documented prior security, military, or law enforcement experience at a senior level.

Allied Universal and Securitas

Allied Universal and Securitas are the two primary guard firm employers in Dallas, holding major contracts across the CBD corporate market, Parkland-adjacent healthcare accounts, industrial corridor overnight patrol, and residential property management security across North and East Dallas. Both firms offer the fastest hiring pathway into the Dallas security market — full-time positions with benefits are available to qualified candidates within 2–4 weeks of application.

Dallas Independent School District (DISD)

DISD employs a large campus security force across its extensive campus network — security monitors, campus police officers, and access control staff across hundreds of Dallas school campuses. DISD security positions are direct district employment with school district benefits. Campus security monitor positions are among the more accessible direct-employment security roles in Dallas for candidates without prior law enforcement experience.

City of Dallas

The City of Dallas employs security staff at the Dallas Convention Center, City Hall, municipal facilities, and parks. The Dallas Convention Center security program is one of the larger municipal security operations in the city, scaling up significantly during major convention weeks and maintaining a substantial permanent staff year-round.

Getting Started in Dallas Security

For candidates entering Dallas security for the first time, the market offers three clearly accessible starting points:

Option 1 — Apply to Allied Universal or Securitas Dallas

Both firms actively hire entry-level unarmed officers across all Dallas districts. Apply online, express availability for overnight and weekend shifts, and expect placement within 2–4 weeks of your license being active. This is the fastest path from zero to employed in Dallas security. Allied Universal's Dallas office covers accounts across the CBD, Medical District, and Stemmons corridor. Securitas holds major corporate and healthcare accounts across the city.

Option 2 — Target Event Security at American Airlines Center

The most accessible per-diem entry point in Dallas proper. American Airlines Center hires event security officers for Mavericks and Stars games, concerts, and arena events throughout the calendar year. Weekend and evening availability is the primary requirement. Pay is per-diem, making this an ideal supplemental income source alongside other employment — or a starting point for candidates building their first Dallas security hours.

Option 3 — Residential Gate Coverage in North and East Dallas

The thousands of apartment communities across Dallas's residential corridors — along Northwest Highway, Greenville Avenue, LBJ Freeway, and the East Dallas growth corridors — create consistent gate security demand particularly on evenings, overnight, and weekends. Residential gate positions are among the most accessible entry-level security roles in Dallas for candidates with a valid guard card and reliable transportation. Apply directly through guard firms serving the Dallas residential market.

Career Paths in Dallas

Dallas offers the full security career ladder within a single city — from entry-level residential gate to Fortune 500 corporate security director. Here is what realistic progression looks like for a candidate entering the Dallas market today:

  1. Entry-level unarmed ($14–17/hr) — Residential gate, retail floor, event venue. Get your Level 2 license, get hired, document your performance. Dallas has more entry-level volume than any other Texas city.
  2. Premium unarmed account ($17–24/hr) — Corporate lobby, healthcare security, overnight industrial. Request a transfer within your guard firm after 12 months of clean performance. The Medical District and CBD are the primary targets at this stage.
  3. Level 3 Armed Commission ($18–33/hr) — The highest-return move in the Dallas security career path. Get your Level 3 within the first 18 months and request placement at a banking, government, or data center account. The pay jump is immediate and the investment payback is rapid.
  4. Supervisor / shift lead ($22–35/hr) — Multi-officer accountability, client relations, incident escalation. Express advancement interest early — Dallas guard firm supervisors are consistently promoted from within.
  5. Site security manager ($35–55/hr) — Full account responsibility, direct client interface. CPP certification from ASIS International is increasingly expected at this level in the Dallas corporate market.
  6. Corporate security director — direct hire ($55–120k+) — AT&T, Parkland leadership, DISD, Fortune 500 direct employment. The ceiling of the Dallas security market, with compensation and benefits that rival comparable management positions in other industries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What security jobs are hiring in Dallas right now?+
Dallas consistently has active openings across all security categories. At any given time the most available positions are overnight industrial and warehouse patrol in South Dallas and the Stemmons corridor, residential gate coverage across North and East Dallas apartment communities, event security at American Airlines Center, healthcare security at Parkland and UT Southwestern, and corporate lobby security in the downtown CBD. Sign up for alerts below to be notified when openings match your area and availability.
What is the best area of Dallas to find security work?+
It depends on what you're looking for. The Medical District offers the most stable long-term security employment with the best benefits at Parkland direct-hire positions. The downtown CBD offers the highest corporate security pay for experienced officers. South Dallas and the Stemmons corridor offer the most accessible overnight entry-level positions. Deep Ellum and Uptown offer the best per-diem event and nightlife rates. For new candidates, South Dallas overnight industrial or North Dallas residential gate are the fastest path to employment.
How do I get a security job at Parkland Health?+
Apply directly through Parkland Health's HR portal at parklandhealth.org/careers. Security officer positions are posted as Dallas County employment — not guard firm contractor roles. Prior security, military, or law enforcement experience is effectively required for most open positions. Parkland's emergency department security is the most competitive and demanding position in the Dallas healthcare security market. Having a Texas Level 2 or Level 3 commission active at the time of application is essential.
What does security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level unarmed security in Dallas averages approximately $14–17/hr. Experienced unarmed officers at premium corporate and healthcare accounts earn $17–24/hr. Armed (Level 3) officers at specialized accounts earn $18–33/hr. Corporate security managers at direct-hire employers reach $50–80/hr. Overnight positions carry $1–3/hr differentials above daytime equivalents across all categories. Dallas runs slightly above the Texas statewide average at approximately $18.37/hr for unarmed security overall.
Is Dallas or Fort Worth better for security jobs?+
Dallas offers more total volume, more employer diversity, and a higher ceiling for career advancement — particularly given the concentration of Fortune 500 headquarters and Parkland's direct county employment opportunity. Fort Worth has a stronger industrial and event security market relative to its size, JPS Health Network as its direct-hire healthcare security anchor, and the Fort Worth Stock Show as the largest annual event security deployment in North Texas. For most candidates, Dallas offers more options — but Fort Worth's market is strong and less competitive at the entry level.
Can I get a security job in Dallas with no experience?+
Yes. Dallas is one of the more accessible major Texas markets for entry-level security candidates. Event security at American Airlines Center, residential gate coverage across North and East Dallas apartment communities, and overnight industrial patrol in South Dallas are all consistently accessible to candidates with a valid Texas Level 2 guard card and clean background — without prior security experience.
