What Security Officers Do in Mansfield

Industrial gate and logistics security is the dominant Mansfield work type. Truck gate operation — verifying driver credentials, managing truck staging, coordinating with warehouse operations on appointment windows, logging all inbound and outbound traffic — is procedural, documentation-heavy work that Sunstates explicitly notes prior truck gate experience for. Officers who develop gate operation competence in Mansfield have skills applicable across DFW's logistics corridor, the Alliance zone in north Fort Worth, and comparable industrial accounts in Grand Prairie and Irving.

Construction site access control at SITEMETRIC accounts is a different environment — fewer truck volumes, more contractor credentialing, and the technology-assisted monitoring that SITEMETRIC's platform provides. Officers work with digital access systems rather than paper logs, building a tech-forward security skill set that's increasingly in demand as construction site security modernizes across the DFW market.
