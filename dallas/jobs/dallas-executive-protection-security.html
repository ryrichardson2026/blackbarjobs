What license do I need for executive protection in Dallas?+
Texas requires a Level 4 Personal Protection Officer (PPO) Commission from the Texas DPS for all paid EP work. This is separate from and in addition to the Level 3 Armed Commission. The Level 4 PPO requires completion of a DPS-approved EP training course covering advance work, protective driving, and close protection tactics. Most credible Dallas EP professionals also hold CPP certification and EP-specific tactical training from accredited programs.
How much do executive protection agents make in Dallas?+
Entry-level EP agents in Dallas with Level 4 credentials and military or law enforcement backgrounds earn $35–55/hr on contract assignments. Experienced EP professionals with established Dallas client relationships earn $55–80/hr on retainer. Senior EP specialists and team leaders working with Fortune 500 executives and high-net-worth clients reach $80–150/hr. Full-time retainer contracts with major Dallas executives provide annual income in the $114k–312k range depending on seniority.
How do I break into executive protection in Dallas?+
The standard path: law enforcement or military experience → Level 3 armed commission → Level 4 PPO certification → EP training program (ESI, Tony Scotti, etc.) → entry-level EP work through private event security and corporate EP entry roles. Building your Dallas network through ASIS chapter events and working private events that bring you into proximity with corporate clients is how most EP careers in Dallas develop. The private event security market is the primary proving ground for EP candidates in the city.
What is the difference between executive protection and bodyguard work in Dallas?+
Bodyguard is a colloquial term for close physical protection work. Executive protection is the professional term and encompasses a broader skill set — advance work, threat assessment, travel security, residential security design, and close protection. Professional EP agents in Dallas prefer the EP designation. The credential required (Level 4 PPO) applies regardless of title. High-quality EP work in Dallas is a professional security management function, not just physical proximity to a principal.
Do Dallas athletes hire personal protection?+
Yes. Dallas Cowboys, Mavericks, and Stars players — particularly those with public profiles, significant contracts, and active social media presence — regularly employ EP agents for event security, travel protection, and residential security management. Player EP work in Dallas circulates through EP firms with existing sports market relationships rather than through public job postings. Building relationships with Dallas-area sports agencies and security firms that serve the professional athlete market is the primary path into this segment.
Dallas EP Market

Executive Protection Demand in Dallas

Dallas is one of the most active executive protection markets in the southern United States. The city's concentration of Fortune 500 headquarters, high-net-worth individuals, celebrity athletes, entertainment personalities, and international business visitors creates consistent demand for professional close protection services. Unlike most security categories, executive protection in Dallas is driven more by the private wealth and corporate executive density of the city than by specific venue or facility requirements.

The Dallas EP market spans three primary client segments. Corporate executive protection covers C-suite executives at Dallas-headquartered companies who require personal security due to their public profile, international travel, or specific threat situations. High-net-worth individual protection covers Dallas's significant private wealth community — residents of Preston Hollow, Highland Park, and Turtle Creek who employ personal protection agents for residential, travel, and event security. Entertainment and athlete protection covers Dallas Cowboys, Mavericks, and Stars players, as well as touring performers and celebrities with Dallas-area connections.

EP work in Dallas requires a credential set that goes well beyond a standard security commission. Level 4 PPO (Personal Protection Officer) certification from Texas DPS is the state-required credential for EP work. Most credible Dallas EP agents also hold CPP, PSP, or executive protection-specific training from accredited programs. Prior law enforcement or military special operations experience is the standard background for Dallas's top EP professionals. Active hiring zip codes for EP work include 75205, 75219, 75225, and 75201.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does Executive Protection Look Like in Dallas?

Executive protection agents in Dallas provide close physical protection, advance work, and threat assessment for principals whose public profile, wealth, or threat situation warrants personal security coverage. Day-to-day work varies significantly by client — a corporate CEO may need EP coverage only during international travel and high-profile public appearances, while a high-net-worth individual with an active threat situation may require 24-hour residential and travel protection.

Advance work is a critical EP skill in Dallas — agents scout venues, routes, and facilities before a principal's arrival to identify threat vectors and establish contingency plans. Dallas's active event calendar (galas, charity events, corporate functions, sports events) means EP agents working the city's private wealth and corporate market regularly conduct advances at hotels, restaurants, private venues, and stadiums.

Dallas also has an active EP training and consulting community. Organizations like the Global Security Institute and several private EP training schools operate in the DFW area, producing EP-credentialed professionals for both the Dallas market and national deployment. EP agents who establish reputations in Dallas often work nationally and internationally, with the city serving as their home base for a mobile close protection career.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide close physical protection to assigned principals during all movements and public appearances
  • Conduct advance work — assess venues, routes, and facilities before principal arrival
  • Develop and maintain threat assessments for principals based on their profile and current risk environment
  • Coordinate residential security for high-net-worth clients — staff, access control, monitoring systems
  • Manage travel security logistics for executive and international travel
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD and venue security on principal movements at public events
  • Conduct security briefings for principals on travel risks and personal security protocols
  • Write detailed incident reports and threat logs for client and corporate legal documentation

Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 4 PPO (Personal Protection Officer) Commission — required by Texas DPS for EP work
  • Prior law enforcement, military (special operations preferred), or senior armed security background
  • EP-specific training from accredited programs (ESI, Tony Scotti Vehicle Dynamics, or equivalent)
  • CPP, PSP, or EP specialist certification strongly preferred for corporate and high-net-worth clients
  • Advance work proficiency — venue assessment, route planning, contingency development
  • Discretion, composure, and professional presentation at the highest level — EP agents represent their principals publicly
Schedules

  • Corporate EP often involves international travel on short notice — schedule flexibility essential
  • Event-based EP in Dallas concentrates around gala seasons (fall and spring) and major sporting events
  • Residential EP for high-net-worth clients may involve 24-hour coverage rotations with teams of agents
  • Day rates and retainer contracts are standard — not hourly security shift work
  • EP work in Dallas can be highly lucrative but requires tolerance for irregular hours and travel
