What festival security jobs are hiring in Dallas?+
The State Fair of Texas at Fair Park is the largest single festival security employer in Dallas — hiring hundreds of officers for its 24-day fall run. Dos Equis Pavilion concert security is active spring through fall. Klyde Warren Park and community event security is available year-round. How do I get hired for State Fair security in Dallas?+
State Fair security is staffed through contracted guard firms. Apply to Allied Universal and other major guard firms in Dallas 6–8 weeks before the State Fair opens in late September. Express your availability for the full 24-day run — officers who can commit to the full fair period are prioritized over those available for only partial runs.
What does festival security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level festival gate and floor security runs $15–18/hr. Experienced zone leads and patrol supervisors earn $18–24/hr. Shift supervisors and armed posts reach $24–34/hr. State Fair weekend days may carry slight premiums over weekday rates. Dos Equis Pavilion major touring act concerts sometimes pay above standard event security rates.
Is festival security in Dallas physically demanding?+
Yes — more so than indoor venue security. Outdoor festival security in Dallas involves extended periods of standing and walking on uneven ground in Texas heat, often in full uniform. State Fair security during peak fall weekends involves managing extremely dense crowds in a sprawling outdoor environment. Physical fitness and heat tolerance are genuine requirements for outdoor festival security work in Dallas.
When is the busiest season for festival security in Dallas?+
Fall is peak — the State Fair of Texas runs late September through mid-October and represents the single largest security employment event in Dallas annually. Spring concert season at Dos Equis Pavilion (April through June) is the second major peak. Summer outdoor festivals are active but limited by Texas heat. Winter holiday events at Klyde Warren Park and Fair Park create smaller seasonal demand in November and December.
Can beginners do festival security in Dallas?+
Yes — particularly at community events at Klyde Warren Park and smaller outdoor festivals. These are lower-pressure environments where entry-level candidates with a valid guard card can build outdoor event experience. State Fair and Dos Equis Pavilion assignments are more suitable for candidates with some prior security or event experience given the scale of the crowds involved.
Dallas Festival Security

Festival & Outdoor Event Security in Dallas

Dallas hosts some of the largest annual events in Texas — and the security operations supporting them are among the most significant single-event staffing deployments in the southern US. The State Fair of Texas at Fair Park is the crown jewel — running for 24 consecutive days in late September and October with attendance exceeding two million visitors, it is one of the largest state fairs in the country and requires hundreds of security officers across its 277-acre grounds throughout the run.

Beyond the State Fair, the Dos Equis Pavilion (formerly Starplex) is a major outdoor concert amphitheater in Fair Park that hosts dozens of touring acts each spring and summer. Klyde Warren Park in downtown Dallas hosts hundreds of free public events and private programs throughout the year. Reunion Park, White Rock Lake, and the AT&T Discovery District host outdoor festivals, cultural events, and city-sponsored programming that generates security staffing demand across every season.

Festival security in Dallas is primarily flexible and seasonal — the work clusters around the fall State Fair season, spring concert season, and the occasional summer outdoor festival. Officers who are available and reliable during the State Fair run can build significant income across 24 consecutive event days. Dos Equis Pavilion and Klyde Warren Park events round out the calendar for officers looking for consistent outdoor event work. Active hiring zip codes include 75210, 75226, and 75201.

What Does Festival Security Look Like in Dallas?

Festival security at large-scale events like the State Fair involves coordinated teams of officers working designated zones across expansive outdoor grounds. The State Fair footprint at Fair Park spans multiple city blocks — officers are assigned to specific gates, midway zones, exhibit halls, the Cotton Bowl stadium, and roving patrol positions. Crowd density during peak weekend days reaches tens of thousands of simultaneous visitors.

Concert security at Dos Equis Pavilion involves the same gate ingress, floor management, and crowd control functions as arena security — but in an outdoor setting where crowd behavior is influenced by heat, alcohol, and the open-air environment. Early afternoon setup and load-in security precedes the public opening by several hours, and post-show egress management is a significant operational phase at all outdoor venues.

Smaller outdoor festivals and community events at Klyde Warren Park and White Rock Lake offer more accessible flexible opportunities for candidates newer to event security. These events are lower-pressure environments with smaller crowds — a good starting point for building outdoor event security experience before moving to State Fair and Pavilion assignments.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff festival entrance gates and ticket checkpoints during ingress periods
  • Monitor designated zones across festival grounds — midway, exhibit halls, performance stages
  • Manage crowd density and flow in high-traffic areas and near performance stages
  • Respond to medical situations, lost children, and security incidents in outdoor environments
  • Conduct roving patrol of festival grounds perimeter and internal pathways
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD and EMS on incidents across large outdoor event footprints
  • Staff load-in and load-out security during artist and vendor access periods
  • Write incident reports for all security events during the festival run

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Outdoor work tolerance — festival security involves extended outdoor shifts in Texas heat and occasional rain
  • Physical fitness for extended standing and walking across large outdoor event grounds
  • Prior event, crowd management, or security experience preferred for State Fair and Pavilion assignments
  • Weekend availability strongly preferred — festival and outdoor event security peaks on Saturdays and Sundays
  • Reliable transportation to Fair Park and outdoor venue locations not accessible by transit
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • State Fair of Texas runs 24 consecutive days late September through mid-October — the biggest single security employment event in Dallas
  • Dos Equis Pavilion concert season runs April through October with multiple events per week during peak months
  • Klyde Warren Park and community events are distributed year-round with spring and fall peaks
  • Festival shifts typically run from 1–2 hours before gates open through 1 hour after last guest exits
  • Weekend festival days are the highest-demand and best-compensated shift windows
  • State Fair security officers can work daily across the full 24-day run for consistent income during the fall season
