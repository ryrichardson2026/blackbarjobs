Overview

DFW Event Security: NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, and the Busiest Entertainment Market in Texas

The Dallas–Fort Worth metro hosts more major professional sports teams than any other Texas market — Cowboys (NFL), Mavericks (NBA), Stars (NHL), Rangers (MLB), FC Dallas (MLS). Each franchise runs 40–82 home events per year creating the most consistent year-round event security employment calendar in Texas. Add Dos Equis Pavilion's summer concert season, the State Fair of Texas in October, Dickies Arena's year-round calendar, and Toyota Music Factory, and DFW's event security market runs essentially every week.

BEST Crowd Management, Andy Frain Services, and Contemporary Services Corporation are the dominant employers across DFW's major venues. Getting on the roster at two or three of these firms gives officers access to the full DFW event calendar at $16–$22/hr depending on the venue and event.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in DFW

Entry screening and magnetometer operation is the baseline function — verifying tickets, conducting bag checks, operating walk-through magnetometers at entry gates, and managing the flow of thousands of attendees into the venue. Officers at AT&T Stadium manage entry flows for 80,000+ attendees on Cowboys game days — the largest event security deployment in DFW.

In-venue crowd management covers seating area monitoring, aisle and concourse crowd flow, incident de-escalation, and medical emergency coordination. Officers in premium seating areas and floor-level positions work directly with venue operations and event staff to maintain a safe guest experience at some of the most recognized sports venues in the country.

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license for unarmed event positions
  • Physical stamina — event security requires standing 8–10 hours on concrete at high-capacity venues
  • Professional crowd management composure — major DFW venues require hospitality-grade interaction
  • Flexible evening and weekend availability — events run primarily Thursday through Sunday

Event Calendar

  • Rangers Globe Life Field — 81 home games March–October
  • Concert venues — Thursday–Saturday peak, year-round calendar across all DFW venues
  • State Fair of Texas — 24 days in October, daily security
