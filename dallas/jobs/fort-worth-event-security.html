What event security jobs are hiring in Fort Worth?+

How do I get hired for Fort Worth Stock Show security?+
Stock Show security is staffed through contracted guard firms — apply to Allied Universal and regional guard firms operating in Fort Worth 6–8 weeks before the late January opening. Officers who can commit to the full 23-day run are prioritized. The Stock Show is one of the highest-value single-event security opportunities in the DFW metro for officers available in January and February.
What does event security pay in Fort Worth?+
Entry-level event gate and floor security in Fort Worth runs $15–19/hr. Experienced officers in lead and VIP positions earn $19–25/hr. Shift supervisors and armed posts at Dickies Arena and major events reach $25–36/hr. Stock Show weekend days and major Dickies Arena concerts sometimes carry premium rates above standard event pay.
Is Fort Worth or Dallas better for event security work?+
They serve different schedules. Dallas has more events year-round with the Mavericks and Stars seasons at American Airlines Center. Fort Worth's strongest periods are the Stock Show run (January–February) and Dickies Arena concert season (spring and summer). Officers who cover both markets across the calendar year can build the most consistent event security income in North Texas.
Do I need experience for Fort Worth event security jobs?+
For entry-level gate and floor positions at smaller Sundance Square and convention events, no prior experience is required beyond a valid guard card. For Dickies Arena floor and VIP positions and Stock Show barn and arena assignments, prior event or crowd management experience is preferred. Professional presentation is consistently required at Bass Performance Hall and convention center assignments.
What is the Fort Worth Stock Show security job like?+
The Stock Show runs 23 consecutive days across multiple venues at the Will Rogers Memorial Center campus — arena, livestock barns, trade show floors, and outdoor midway. It's one of the most varied single-event security assignments in DFW. Officers work crowd management, livestock area monitoring, trade show floor security, and arena ingress across different assignments throughout the run. Physical stamina and outdoor work tolerance are genuine requirements given the scale of the facility.
Fort Worth Events

Event Security Demand in Fort Worth

Fort Worth has one of the most active event security markets in North Texas outside of the Arlington Entertainment District. Dickies Arena — the city's 14,000-seat multipurpose venue — hosts major touring concerts, rodeo events, and sports competitions throughout the year and is the largest single event security employer in Fort Worth. Bass Performance Hall in downtown is a world-class performing arts venue with an active schedule of concerts, operas, and ballets requiring professional event security staffing.

The Fort Worth Stock Show and Rodeo — held annually at Will Rogers Memorial Center for 23 days in January and February — is one of the largest indoor rodeos in the world and generates one of the biggest single-event security staffing deployments in the DFW metro. Sundance Square's outdoor plaza and surrounding entertainment district requires consistent Friday and Saturday night event and venue security. The Fort Worth Convention Center hosts major conventions, trade shows, and public events throughout the year.

Fort Worth event security is flexible and event-driven for most assignments. Officers who build relationships with the guard firms holding Dickies Arena and Will Rogers contracts can secure consistent work across the full annual event calendar. The Stock Show run in January–February and Dickies Arena summer concert season are the two highest-density event security employment periods in Fort Worth. Active hiring zip codes include 76102, 76107, and 76164.

What Does Event Security Look Like in Fort Worth?

Fort Worth event security spans several venue types. Arena security at Dickies Arena involves coordinated team operations covering gates, concourses, floor sections, and VIP areas during concerts, rodeos, and sports events — similar in structure to American Airlines Center in Dallas but at a somewhat smaller scale. Performing arts security at Bass Performance Hall is more formal and lower-intensity, requiring professional presentation in an upscale cultural venue.

The Fort Worth Stock Show and Rodeo — held annually at Will Rogers Memorial Center for 23 days in January and February — is one of the largest indoor rodeos in the world and generates one of the biggest single-event security staffing deployments in the DFW metro. Sundance Square's outdoor plaza and surrounding entertainment district requires consistent Friday and Saturday night event and venue security. The Fort Worth Convention Center hosts major conventions, trade shows, and public events throughout the year.

Sundance Square outdoor events and convention center security round out the Fort Worth event landscape. The Sundance Square plaza hosts free outdoor concerts and seasonal events year-round that require security staffing on evenings and weekends. Convention center assignments tend to be less physically demanding and more professional in orientation than arena and rodeo events.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff venue entrance gates and ticket checkpoints during event ingress
  • Monitor crowd behavior on arena floors, concourses, and seating sections
  • Conduct de-escalation and ejections per venue policy and Texas law
  • Staff VIP, backstage, and restricted-access areas at performing arts and arena venues
  • Monitor livestock barn, trade show floors, and outdoor grounds during Stock Show
  • Coordinate crowd flow during intermission and post-event egress
  • Conduct post-event venue sweep before clearing the facility
  • Coordinate with Fort Worth PD and venue management on escalated incidents
