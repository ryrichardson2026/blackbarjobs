What is the Telecom Corridor and why does it matter for security jobs?+
The Telecom Corridor is a dense concentration of technology company offices along US-75 in Richardson — home to Ericsson, Samsung, Fujitsu, AT&T, and dozens of other tech employers. These corporate campuses maintain active security programs and pay above the DFW average for professional corporate lobby and access control officers. It is the highest-compensating security employment zone in Richardson.
Does UT Dallas hire security officers?+
Yes. UT Dallas campus security employs officers across multiple shifts to manage building access, parking, emergency response, and campus patrol across its growing campus. Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available?

Corporate campus security along the Telecom Corridor is the highest-paying and most professionally demanding security category in Richardson. Tech company campuses require access control precision, professional presentation, and the ability to interface with corporate employees and visitors across multiple daily touchpoints. Experience with electronic access control systems and professional corporate environments is valued by Telecom Corridor security employers.

UT Dallas campus security provides stable multi-shift employment at one of the fastest-growing research universities in Texas. Campus security officers manage building access, parking enforcement, emergency response coordination, and after-hours patrol across a large and expanding campus footprint. UTD campus security positions are accessible to candidates with a valid guard card and professional demeanor.

Residential security across Richardson's apartment communities and HOA neighborhoods along Arapaho Road, Belt Line Road, and the US-75 residential corridors provides consistent overnight and weekend gate coverage demand. Richardson's proximity to Plano and Garland means security candidates in the city are part of a broader eastern Collin County and northern Dallas County patrol market.
