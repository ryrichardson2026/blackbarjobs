What Security Jobs Are Available in Garland?

Industrial security along Garland's manufacturing corridor is one of the most consistent overnight security employment categories in the city. Large plants and distribution operations along I-635 and Highway 78 require 24-hour security coverage with overnight and weekend shifts being the most in-demand. Vehicle patrol is the standard operating mode at Garland's larger industrial facilities.

Retail security at Firewheel Town Center — one of the largest open-air shopping centers in Dallas County — and the Town East Boulevard corridor provides both LP investigator and uniformed floor security opportunities. Baylor Scott & White Garland and the city's medical office corridor generate healthcare security demand across all three shifts.

Garland's diverse security market is accessible to candidates at all experience levels. The industrial sector provides entry-level overnight patrol opportunities. Retail and healthcare positions are available for mid-level candidates. The city's scale supports a full range of security career paths within a single market.
