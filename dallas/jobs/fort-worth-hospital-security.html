Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required — CPR/First Aid certification required at most Fort Worth hospitals
  • De-escalation training required or strongly preferred at JPS and Cook Children's
  • Prior security, law enforcement, military, or healthcare background strongly preferred
  • Physical ability to respond quickly and assist clinical staff in demanding environments
  • Comfort working with high-acuity and pediatric patient populations
  • Strong written documentation skills for reports reviewed by clinical and accreditation teams
Schedules

  • 12-hour rotations standard at JPS and Texas Health Harris Methodist
  • Overnight and weekend shifts carry additional differentials on top of healthcare base rates
  • Holiday coverage required — hospital systems maintain full security staffing on all holidays
  • Emergency department assignments at JPS are highest intensity — typically after probationary period
  • Cook Children's assignments require specific comfort with pediatric hospital environments
