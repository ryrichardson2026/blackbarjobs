Overview

DFW Airport Security: One of the World's Busiest Airports, Federal Pay, and Year-Round Hiring

Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport is the fourth-busiest airport in the world by operations and one of the largest employers in the DFW metro - approximately 28,000 employees on airport property across airlines, concessions, ground handling, and security. TSA at DFW operates one of the largest Transportation Security Officer workforces in the country, with continuous hiring given the scale of passenger throughput across 5 terminals and 200+ gates.

The federal employment benefits - FEHB health coverage, FERS retirement, TSP, paid leave, and locality pay - make TSA Officer positions at DFW among the most comprehensively compensated security positions in Texas. Starting Band D pay of $22-$25/hr with federal benefits packages that commercial security employers cannot match makes the 3-6 month hiring process worth pursuing for candidates who want long-term security employment stability.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Airport Security Officers Do at DFW

TSA Transportation Security Officers at DFW operate passenger and baggage screening lanes - running walk-through magnetometers and advanced imaging technology (AIT), processing carry-on baggage through X-ray, conducting pat-downs when required, and managing the flow of thousands of passengers per hour through DFW's high-throughput checkpoints. The operational environment is among the highest-volume TSA checkpoint operations in the US.

Private screeners and airline security officers work in more specialized environments - American Airlines security at terminals, ground handler airside security for ramp access control, and cargo facility security. SIDA credentialing and airside operational requirements distinguish airport security from standard contracted commercial security.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • TSA Transportation Security Officer - passenger and baggage screening, AIT operation, checkpoint management
  • DFW Terminal A/B/C/D/E checkpoints - 5 terminals, 200+ gates, American Airlines hub
  • Dallas Love Field TSA checkpoint - Southwest Airlines hub, 16M passengers annually
  • Private aviation screener - Unifi/AU/Securitas contractor screener roles at DFW terminals
  • American Airlines security officer - corporate and terminal security, airside access management
  • SIDA airside ramp security - fuel farm, cargo facility, ground handling area access control
  • Airport incident response - coordination with DFW Police and CBP

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • US citizenship - required for all TSA Officer positions
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED required for TSA
  • Vision, hearing, and color vision requirements - TSA medical evaluation required
  • 10-year background investigation - employment, criminal, financial, residential history
  • SIDA badge eligibility for airside positions - DFW Airport Authority background check
  • Texas Level 2 for contracted private screener and terminal security positions
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • Apply at USAJobs.gov - search 'Transportation Security Officer' and filter for DFW or Dallas TX
  • Computer Based Test (CBT) at an authorized testing center
  • Structured interview, medical evaluation, background investigation, drug test
  • TSA DFW hiring process: typically 3-6 months from application to start date
  • TSA operates 24/7 - all shifts including early morning (4am start) and overnight
  • Shift bid system for TSA - schedule preferences based on seniority
