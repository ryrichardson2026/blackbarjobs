Lewisville, TX Market

Security Jobs in Lewisville, TX

Lewisville is Denton County's largest city and one of the most active security markets in the northern DFW suburbs. The city's position along I-35E — the primary commercial corridor between Dallas and Denton — gives it a large and diverse retail, commercial, and industrial security employment base that is consistently active across all shift types.

Vista Ridge Mall is the largest single retail security employer in Lewisville — the mall's anchor stores and food court draw significant foot traffic that drives consistent demand for uniformed security and LP officers. The I-35E commercial corridor flanking the mall extends the retail security employment zone across a significant stretch of Lewisville's eastern edge. Medical City Lewisville and the city's growing medical office corridor add healthcare security demand.

Lake Lewisville and the surrounding waterfront communities create a distinctive residential and park security employment dimension unique to the city — seasonal outdoor security demand peaks significantly in spring and summer as the lake draws visitors. The dense apartment corridor along Main Street, Hebron Parkway, and the SH-121 frontage provides year-round residential gate coverage demand for overnight and weekend candidates. Active hiring zip codes include 75056, 75057, 75067, and 75077.

Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available?

Retail security at Vista Ridge Mall and the I-35E commercial corridor is the most consistently available security category in Lewisville. The mall's combination of anchor retailers, restaurants, and a cinema complex generates demand across daytime, evening, and overnight shifts. LP investigator roles and uniformed floor security are both active at Lewisville retail accounts.

Healthcare security at Medical City Lewisville provides stable multi-shift employment in a structured clinical environment. The hospital's emergency department and inpatient facilities generate security demand across all shifts including overnight and weekends. Lewisville's growing medical office corridor adds additional healthcare security opportunities adjacent to the main hospital campus.

Lake Lewisville area security — covering waterfront parks, recreational facilities, and lakeside residential communities — is a seasonally active category that peaks dramatically in spring and summer. Officers working lake area security gain experience in outdoor and public space security management that is transferable to parks, events, and outdoor venue security across DFW.
