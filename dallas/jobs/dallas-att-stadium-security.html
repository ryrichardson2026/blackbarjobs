Overview

AT&T Stadium Security Jobs — What You Need To Know

AT&T Stadium in Arlington is one of the largest domed stadiums in the world — a 100,000+ capacity venue that is home to the Dallas Cowboys and hosts some of the largest events in Texas including Super Bowls, Big 12 Championship games, college football playoffs, major concerts, and international soccer matches. Security at AT&T Stadium is handled through contracted security firms operating under stadium agreements.

Cowboys home game security is the primary ongoing deployment — 8 regular season home games plus preseason (typically 2 home games in August) and any home playoff games. Each game day requires a large security deployment across all entry gates, parking perimeters, concourses, field level, and suite corridors. Deployments typically begin a few hours before kickoff and extend through post-game egress.

How To Get On The AT&T Stadium Security Roster

  • Apply to the stadium security contractors — search "AT&T Stadium security" or "Cowboys game day security" on major job boards. Sign up for alerts below and we'll notify you when positions post.
  • Apply in summer — roster spots for the NFL season are filled before the September opener. Apply well before the preseason — roster spots fill before the first game. Late applications may still get added to the roster mid-season for playoff coverage.
  • Get your Level 2 guard card first — a valid Texas Level 2 Security License is required for all paid security positions at AT&T Stadium. No license means no placement. See our full Texas licensing guide.
  • Attend orientation — Stadium contractors run pre-season orientation covering post assignments, radio protocols, and emergency procedures specific to AT&T Stadium.
  • Be available for all home games — roster officers are expected to be available for all scheduled home games. Consistent unavailability results in removal from the active roster.

AT&T Stadium Security Posts & Assignments

  • Parking perimeter — outdoor patrol of the stadium parking lots and approaches; first arrival point for fans; physically demanding outdoor post in Texas heat/cold
  • Gate entry screening — managing fan entry, bag check coordination, and ticket verification at stadium gates; high fan interaction volume
  • Concourse patrol — interior stadium concourse coverage during the game; managing crowd flow, incident response, and medical coordination
  • Field level — field perimeter security covering access control to playing surface, sidelines, and team areas; highest-profile post assignment
  • Suite corridor — VIP and suite level access control; professional presentation standard is highest at this post
  • Post-game egress — parking lot and egress management as fans exit; shift extends through post-game crowd egress
Pay & What To Expect

Cowboys Game Day Security Pay

flexible — no commitment between games. Rates by post and event type.

  • Parking, gate entry: $17–20/hr
  • Concourse patrol: $18–22/hr
  • Suite and VIP corridor: $19–24/hr
  • Field level (senior roster): $20–25/hr
  • Playoff games: Above regular season rate
  • Super Bowl / major events: Premium rate

Typical game day shift: Report 4 hours before kickoff, released ~1 hour post-game. Cowboys game day shifts run from pre-game through post-game egress — figure on most of your day. At $17–22/hr per game, working a full regular season roster adds up meaningfully stacked on top of regular work.

Requirements

What You Need to Work Cowboys Football Security

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — required for all positions; no exceptions at major venue accounts
  • Professional presentation — Stadium security accounts hold officers to strict uniform and appearance standards; clean shave or neatly groomed facial hair, uniform pressed
  • Physical fitness — gate and parking posts involve extended standing and walking in outdoor Texas conditions; preseason August games are extremely hot
  • Customer service composure — high fan interaction volume including intoxicated fans; composure and de-escalation skills are essential
  • Game day availability — primarily Sunday home games during the NFL season; August preseason adds Saturday games
  • Clean background — All stadium security placements require thorough background screening
