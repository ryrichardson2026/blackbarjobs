Does Texas Health HEB hire security officers?+
Yes. Texas Health Harris Methodist HEB employs security officers across its North Richland Hills campus. Applications go through the Texas Health Resources HR portal. Texas Health direct employment includes hospital system benefits. Prior security or healthcare experience is preferred. Allied Universal also holds contracted security accounts at multiple Texas Health campuses across DFW.
What is North East Mall security like?+
North East Mall is one of the larger enclosed malls in Tarrant County. Mall security there covers uniformed floor patrol, loss prevention investigations, parking structure security, and overnight mall protection. It's a structured retail security environment with a defined management hierarchy and post orders across multiple security posts throughout the facility.
What does security pay in North Richland Hills TX?+
Entry-level security in NRH runs $15–18/hr. Experienced officers at mall and healthcare accounts earn $18–25/hr. Senior positions reach $25–33/hr. NRH tracks the Tarrant County mid-cities market — comparable to Hurst-Euless-Bedford and Arlington for equivalent role types.
What cities are near NRH for security work?+
NRH borders Hurst, Euless, and Bedford to the south (the HEB mid-cities corridor), Keller to the north, Richland Hills to the west, and Colleyville to the northeast. The mid-cities Tarrant County security market connects NRH candidates to accounts across a large and active suburban security zone between Fort Worth and DFW Airport.
What is NRH2O security like?+
NRH2O Family Water Park operates seasonally Memorial Day through Labor Day. Park security covers crowd management, perimeter patrol, incident response, and coordination with park operations staff. It's a high-volume outdoor public facility during summer weekends — physically demanding but accessible to candidates with a valid guard card and experience managing large public crowds.
North Richland Hills, TX Market

Security Jobs in North Richland Hills, TX

North Richland Hills is Tarrant County's fourth-largest city — a mid-cities suburb of 75,000+ positioned between Fort Worth and the DFW Airport corridor. The city's security market is anchored by North East Mall (one of the largest enclosed malls in Tarrant County), Texas Health Harris Methodist HEB medical campus (one of the major healthcare security employers in the mid-cities), and a dense commercial corridor along Loop 820 and Boulevard 26 that generates consistent retail and commercial security demand.

North East Mall is the largest single security employer in North Richland Hills — the enclosed mall's combination of anchor department stores, specialty retailers, and food court drives significant uniformed security and LP demand across multiple daily shifts. Mall security at North East is one of the most structured retail security environments in the Tarrant County mid-cities market.

Texas Health HEB — the Texas Health Harris Methodist HEB Campus on Precinct Line Road — is one of the most significant healthcare security employment sites in the Tarrant County mid-cities market. Healthcare security at Texas Health follows the standard Texas Health Resources employment and benefits structure. The city's NRH Centre recreation complex and NRH2O Family Water Park add seasonal event and public facility security demand unique to North Richland Hills. Active hiring zip codes include 76180 and 76182.

What Security Jobs Are Available?

Mall security at North East Mall is the highest-volume security employment site in North Richland Hills. Uniformed floor security, LP investigators, and overnight mall security officers are all active categories at this location. North East Mall's scale — it is one of the larger enclosed malls in Tarrant County — supports a significant security headcount across all three shifts.

Healthcare security at Texas Health HEB provides stable multi-shift employment in the professional healthcare security environment. The hospital's emergency department and inpatient facilities generate security demand across all shifts. Texas Health Resources direct employment includes hospital system benefits comparable to other major Texas Health campuses across DFW.

Residential security across North Richland Hills's apartment communities along Rufe Snow Drive, Precinct Line Road, and the Loop 820 corridor provides overnight and weekend gate coverage that is consistently active. The city's large residential base and proximity to the Hurst-Euless-Bedford mid-cities corridor extends the residential security employment zone for NRH-based candidates.
