What part-time security jobs are hiring in Dallas right now? +
Active part-time openings in Dallas span event security across Deep Ellum and Victory Park, weekend residential gate coverage, part-time retail LP at Preston Road and Mockingbird Lane shopping centers, and flexible roster positions with major guard firms. Can I do security part-time in Dallas while working another job? +
Yes — and many Dallas security officers do exactly that. Event security and flexible positions are particularly well-suited to secondary income arrangements because they're scheduled around specific events rather than fixed shifts. Weekend residential gate positions with fixed Saturday and Sunday schedules are another reliable part-time structure that's easy to work around a weekday job.
Do part-time security jobs in Dallas require a guard card? +
Yes. Texas law requires a valid Level II Security Officer Commission for all paid security work regardless of hours. There is no part-time exemption. Most candidates can obtain their commission within 2–4 weeks of starting training.
How many hours a week can I work doing part-time security in Dallas? +
It varies by employer and role type. Event security flexible can range from 0 to 20+ hours per week depending on the event calendar. Residential gate and retail LP part-time positions typically offer 16–24 guaranteed hours per week. flexible roster officers at guard firms can often build 20–32 hours per week during busy periods by accepting multiple assignments.
What does part-time security pay in Dallas? +
Part-time security in Dallas pays the same hourly rates as full-time equivalent roles — typically $15–18/hr for entry-level unarmed, $18–24/hr for experienced, and $24–32/hr for armed or supervisory part-time positions. Weekend and overnight shifts include the standard $1–3/hr differential.
Is part-time security a good way to start a security career in Dallas? +
Yes — it's one of the best ways. Part-time and flexible work lets you build a track record at multiple site types, get exposure to different employers and environments, and identify which security role type fits you before committing to a full-time position. Most full-time Dallas security openings are filled from within existing part-time rosters.
Dallas Part-Time Market

Part-Time Security Demand in Dallas

Dallas has one of the strongest part-time security markets in Texas, driven by the city's combination of high event volume, dense retail activity, and large residential communities that need supplemental weekend coverage. Guard firms operating in Dallas consistently struggle to fill Friday, Saturday, and Sunday shifts — which means candidates with weekend availability are in a strong position to find consistent part-time work.

Event security is the most reliably part-time-friendly category in Dallas. The American Airlines Center event calendar, Deep Ellum and Uptown nightlife, Fair Park seasonal events, and the city's active convention schedule all generate flexible staffing needs that are specifically structured for part-time officers. Residential community gate security is another strong part-time category — many Dallas HOA and apartment communities need weekend-only coverage that full-time officers don't want.

For candidates who want to test the security industry before committing full-time, or who need a second income stream around an existing job or school schedule, Dallas's part-time market offers more genuine options than most DFW suburbs. Role Overview

What Part-Time Security Jobs Are Available in Dallas?

Part-time security in Dallas spans several role types. Event security at venues across Deep Ellum, Uptown, and Victory Park is the most common flexible structure — you're staffed for specific events rather than fixed shifts, which makes scheduling highly flexible. Residential community gate positions often advertise specifically for weekend-only coverage.

Retail security and loss prevention positions at Dallas shopping centers frequently offer part-time scheduling, particularly for candidates who can work Friday evenings through Sunday. Many national guard firms operating in Dallas maintain flexible rosters that they draw from when they need additional coverage for specific shifts — getting on one of these rosters is a reliable way to build consistent part-time hours.

Part-time security in Dallas can convert to full-time relatively quickly for reliable performers. Most guard firms prefer to promote from their flexible roster rather than hire externally for full-time openings — candidates who show up consistently and perform well on part-time assignments are typically the first offered full-time positions when they open.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff assigned security posts during scheduled part-time or flexible shifts
  • Provide access control, crowd management, or patrol coverage based on assignment type
  • Monitor assigned areas for security incidents and respond appropriately
  • Write shift logs and incident reports as required by the employer
  • Maintain professional appearance and conduct expected by the client property
  • Coordinate with full-time security staff and supervisors during shared shifts
  • Respond to disturbances and emergencies within the scope of the assignment
  • Complete post-shift documentation and handoff to incoming staff
