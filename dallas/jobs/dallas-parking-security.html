What parking security jobs are hiring in Dallas?+
Active parking security openings in Dallas span downtown CBD parking structure overnight coverage, hospital campus parking at Parkland and UT Southwestern, event parking at American Airlines Center and Fair Park, airport parking facilities at Love Field, and surface lot security across commercial corridors. Is parking security a good entry-level job in Dallas?+
Yes — parking security is one of the more accessible entry-level security positions in Dallas. The role is straightforward, scheduling is predictable, and employers are generally more flexible on prior experience requirements than corporate campus or healthcare security employers. For candidates new to security who want to build hours and experience, a parking structure overnight shift is a solid starting point.
What does parking security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level parking security in Dallas runs $15–18/hr. Experienced officers at hospital campus and premium downtown structures earn $18–23/hr. Senior patrol supervisors and shift leads reach $23–30/hr. Overnight shifts carry $1–2/hr differentials on top of base pay.
Do parking security officers carry weapons in Dallas?+
Most Dallas parking security positions are unarmed — requiring only a Texas Level 2 commission. Some high-security parking facilities attached to government buildings, financial institutions, or hospitals may have armed parking security posts requiring a Level 3 commission. Confirm with the employer before applying if armed versus unarmed status matters for your application.
What are the main security concerns at Dallas parking structures?+
Vehicle break-ins and smash-and-grab theft are the primary concerns at downtown and entertainment district parking structures. Catalytic converter theft is an active problem across Dallas parking facilities — particularly at structures serving hospitals and office towers where vehicles park for extended periods. Unauthorized overnight parking, trespassing, and occasional assault incidents round out the security concerns at large public parking structures.
Can I do parking security overnight in Dallas?+
Yes — overnight parking security is the most consistently available shift type in the Dallas parking security market. Downtown office tower and hotel parking structures, hospital campus lots, and airport parking facilities all need overnight coverage when other staff is absent. Candidates with a valid guard card and comfortable with independent overnight work have good options across the Dallas parking security market.
Dallas Parking Security

Parking Security Demand in Dallas

Dallas's car-dependent urban layout and dense commercial core create significant parking security demand across the city. The downtown CBD alone has dozens of multi-level parking structures attached to office towers, hotels, and entertainment venues — each requiring security coverage during peak hours and overnight to deter vehicle break-ins, theft, and loitering. Victory Park parking structures serving American Airlines Center see security demand spike dramatically on event nights.

Hospital campus parking security is one of the most consistently staffed categories in Dallas — major health systems like Parkland, UT Southwestern, and Baylor Scott & White operate large parking facilities serving employees, patients, and visitors 24 hours a day. Airport parking at Dallas Love Field and DFW International requires security coverage across their multi-level facilities serving tens of thousands of daily travelers. Event parking lots at Fair Park and the Cotton Bowl generate seasonal security demand during the State Fair and major events.

Parking security in Dallas is primarily patrol-based — officers cover large multi-floor structures on foot or via elevator, conduct vehicle break-in deterrence, assist motorists with lockouts and jump-starts, and manage access at entry/exit points. The role is one of the more accessible security positions in the city for candidates who prefer independent, patrol-oriented work with predictable schedules. Active hiring zip codes include 75201, 75202, 75219, and 75235.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does Parking Security Look Like in Dallas?

Parking security officers in Dallas conduct floor-by-floor patrols of multi-level parking structures, monitor surface lots for suspicious activity, assist motorists, and manage access control at staffed entry and exit points. The primary security concerns are vehicle break-ins, catalytic converter theft, and unauthorized overnight parking — all of which are active problems in Dallas's downtown parking ecosystem.

Event parking security at venues like American Airlines Center and Fair Park involves directing traffic into and out of large surface lots and structures before and after events — requiring officers who can manage high-volume vehicle flow while maintaining safety protocols. These positions are event-driven and flexible offering flexible scheduling around the event calendar.

Overnight parking security at downtown office towers and hotels is one of the more independent security roles in Dallas — officers patrol largely empty structures during low-traffic hours, monitor for overnight unauthorized parkers, and respond to any vehicle incidents on the premises. The independent nature of the work suits candidates who are self-directed and comfortable with solo overnight assignments.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct regular floor-by-floor patrol of assigned parking structures
  • Monitor surface lots and perimeter areas for vehicle break-ins and theft
  • Assist motorists with lockouts, jump-starts, and directions
  • Manage vehicle access at staffed entry and exit points during operating hours
  • Direct vehicle traffic during event ingress and egress at major Dallas venues
  • Monitor for and respond to unauthorized overnight parking
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD on vehicle theft and break-in incidents
  • Write shift logs and incident reports for all parking security events

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Physical fitness for extended walking and stair climbing across multi-level parking structures
  • Valid driver's license for event parking and lot-access management roles
  • Comfort with independent overnight patrol — parking security involves extended solo work with minimal supervision
  • Basic customer service skills for motorist assistance and visitor directions
  • Prior security or patrol experience helpful but not required for most parking security positions
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Overnight shifts (10pm–6am) are the most consistently available — parking structures need coverage when other staff is absent
  • Event parking shifts are flexible tied to the event calendar at American Airlines Center and Fair Park
  • Hospital campus parking security is available across all three shifts with 24-hour demand
  • Downtown office tower parking follows business hours with overnight coverage for attached hotel and residential towers
  • Weekend event parking demand peaks Friday and Saturday nights at entertainment district structures
  • Shift differentials of $1–2/hr for overnight parking security positions
