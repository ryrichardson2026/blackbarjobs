How much do armed overnight security officers make in Dallas?+
Armed overnight is the highest-compensating standard security role in Dallas. Entry-level positions run $22–26/hr. Experienced officers at data centers and financial institutions earn $26–32/hr. Senior and specialist roles reach $32–42/hr. These rates reflect the Level 3 commission premium plus overnight shift differential stacked together.
Do I need a Level 3 license for armed overnight security in Dallas?+
Yes — without exception. Any paid security role where the officer carries a firearm in Texas requires a valid Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer Commission. There are no waivers or temporary authorizations. Officers must also carry the specific firearm they qualified with on their range qualification.
Is armed overnight security in Dallas dangerous?+
Armed overnight security in Dallas is not considered a high-risk assignment compared to active law enforcement work. The primary purpose of armed overnight coverage at data centers and financial institutions is deterrence — the visible presence of an armed officer is typically sufficient to prevent unauthorized access attempts. Physical confrontations are uncommon at these assignment types. Industrial armed overnight patrol involving vehicle patrol of large perimeters carries slightly more active response potential.
What companies hire armed overnight security in Dallas?+
Allied Universal, Securitas, and several specialized corporate security firms hold contracts at Dallas's major data centers and financial institutions for armed overnight coverage. Some large financial institutions and data center operators also hire armed overnight officers directly as in-house security staff rather than through contracted guard services.
Can I work armed overnight security in Dallas if I just got my Level 3?+
Yes — a freshly issued Level 3 commission is valid for armed work. However, most armed overnight positions at financial institutions and data centers prefer candidates with at least some prior armed security experience. Starting with an armed daytime or evening post at a less restrictive site is a common path to building the track record needed for the premium overnight accounts.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does an Armed Overnight Security Officer Do in Dallas?

Armed overnight security officers in Dallas work at sites where the combination of high asset value and reduced staffing during overnight hours creates an elevated security requirement. The job is fundamentally different from daytime armed security — overnight shifts involve longer periods of independent monitoring with less human traffic, punctuated by access control events at the start and end of the shift when authorized personnel arrive or depart.

At data centers, armed overnight officers manage access control for after-hours maintenance personnel, monitor server room access logs, and conduct scheduled perimeter patrols of large campuses. At financial institution locations, the focus shifts to after-hours vault monitoring, ATM security, and ensuring no unauthorized access occurs between close of business and opening. Industrial armed overnight officers combine vehicle patrol with gate control for authorized freight and maintenance vehicles.

The overnight nature of these assignments requires strong self-direction and the ability to maintain vigilance during low-stimulation periods. Officers who build a track record in Dallas armed overnight roles are among the most sought-after in the market — their combination of Level 3 commission, reliable overnight availability, and proven performance at high-security sites makes them highly employable across the city's top accounts.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control after-hours access at financial institutions, data centers, and industrial facilities
  • Conduct armed vehicle and foot patrols of high-value property perimeters during overnight hours
  • Monitor CCTV systems and access control panels throughout the overnight shift
  • Verify and log all authorized personnel entering or exiting during overnight hours
  • Respond to alarm activations, perimeter breaches, and unauthorized access attempts
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD on incidents requiring law enforcement response
  • Write detailed overnight shift logs and incident reports with full documentation
  • Maintain firearm, holster, and equipment in compliance with employer standards and Texas law

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer Commission — non-negotiable
  • Current firearms range qualification with the specific firearm carried on duty
  • Clean criminal background and no federal firearms prohibitions
  • Reliable overnight availability — the primary scheduling requirement
  • Prior military, law enforcement, or armed security experience strongly preferred
  • Valid driver's license — most Dallas armed overnight roles involve vehicle patrol
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Overnight shifts (10pm–6am or 11pm–7am) are the standard for armed overnight positions in Dallas
  • 12-hour overnight rotations increasingly common at data centers and financial institutions preferring fewer shift handoffs
  • Weekend and holiday overnight coverage required — high-value facilities do not reduce armed coverage on holidays
  • Overtime opportunities common at financial institutions and data centers during high-demand periods
  • Shift differentials of $2–4/hr on top of armed base pay standard for overnight hours
  • Indoor and outdoor patrol mix — data centers are climate-controlled; industrial and perimeter patrol involves outdoor exposure
