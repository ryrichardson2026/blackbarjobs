How do I get a job doing security at American Airlines Center?+
American Airlines Center security is staffed through contracted guard firms — primarily Allied Universal. Apply directly to Allied Universal for their American Airlines Center event security roster. Express your availability for weeknight and weekend evening shifts during the NBA and NHL seasons. Once on the roster, you'll be called for individual games and events based on your availability.
What does sporting event security pay in Dallas?+
Standard game-day security at Dallas sporting events runs $15–19/hr for gate and floor officers. Experienced section leads and VIP positions earn $19–26/hr. Shift supervisors and armed posts reach $26–38/hr. Playoff games, championship events, and major concerts at arena venues pay above standard game-day rates.
Is sporting event security in Dallas seasonal?+
The NBA and NHL seasons together cover October through June — making the seasonal gap July through September when arena event demand is lowest. However, American Airlines Center hosts concerts and non-sporting events year-round that fill most of the gap. Cotton Bowl fall football and the State Fair season add to the fall calendar. Officers who cover multiple venues across the metro can find consistent event security work year-round.
Do I need experience for sporting event security in Dallas?+
For entry-level gate and concourse positions at Mavericks and Stars games, prior security experience is helpful but not always required — the guard firm handles site-specific orientation. For VIP, tunnel, and supervisory positions at major arena events, prior experience in crowd management or large-venue security is preferred. A valid guard card and physical fitness are the baseline requirements.
Can I work sporting event security in Dallas part-time?+
Yes — sporting event security is one of the most naturally part-time-friendly security categories in Dallas. You work individual games rather than fixed weekly shifts. Officers who are available for Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday evening home games can build 8–15 hours per week during the Mavericks and Stars seasons while maintaining other employment or commitments.

Sporting Event Security Demand in Dallas

AT&T Stadium in Arlington anchors the Cowboys' NFL season and hosts the College Football Playoff.

Role Overview

What Does Sporting Event Security Look Like in Dallas?

Sporting event security in Dallas involves large-scale crowd management operations that differ significantly from bar, venue, or commercial security work. A sold-out Mavericks or Stars game at American Airlines Center requires 100–200 security officers working simultaneously across gates, concourses, seating sections, VIP areas, tunnel access, and the arena floor. Officers work in coordinated teams under shift supervisors with radio communication across the building.

Gate and ingress security handles the highest-volume period — managing thousands of fans through ticketing checkpoints and bag screening in the 90 minutes before tip-off or puck drop. Concourse and section officers monitor crowd behavior throughout the event and respond to ejection situations. VIP and suite-level officers work in a more client-service-oriented capacity. Post-event sweep and egress management is the final phase of each game-day deployment.

Playoff games, marquee matchups, and major non-sporting events (concerts, boxing, championship fights) at Dallas venues carry premium event rates above standard game-day pay. Officers who build tenure with the contracted guard firms covering American Airlines Center and AT&T Stadium often get priority scheduling for premium events.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff venue gates — manage ticketing checkpoints, bag screening, and fan ingress
  • Monitor seating sections and concourses for disruptive behavior and policy violations
  • Conduct de-escalation and ejections in accordance with venue policy and Texas law
  • Staff VIP, suite, and restricted-access areas requiring elevated access control
  • Manage tunnel and locker room access for players, coaches, and credentialed personnel
  • Coordinate crowd flow during halftime, intermission, and post-event egress
  • Conduct post-event venue sweep before the building is cleared
  • Write incident reports for ejections, medical situations, and security events

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Ability to manage large crowds in a high-energy, high-noise arena environment
  • Physical fitness for extended standing and rapid response across large venue footprints
  • Weekend and weeknight evening availability — games run primarily Tuesday through Saturday evenings
  • Prior event, crowd management, or security experience preferred at American Airlines Center and Cotton Bowl assignments
  • Professional conduct required — sporting events involve families, children, and high-profile clientele
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Game-day scheduling based on team home schedules — Mavericks and Stars run October through June
  • Shifts start 2 hours before tip-off or puck drop and run through venue clear (typically 4–5 hours total)
  • Mavericks and Stars seasons overlap October through April creating near-continuous Tuesday–Saturday evening demand
  • Cotton Bowl and Toyota Stadium events are concentrated in fall for college football season
  • Playoff games and premium events carry extended shift times and premium pay rates
  • Off-season concert and non-sporting events at American Airlines Center fill scheduling gaps year-round
