Can I get a security job in Fort Worth with no experience?+
Yes. The Fort Worth Stock Show, Dickies Arena event security, residential community gate positions, and retail floor security at Hulen Mall and Alliance Town Center all hire candidates without prior security experience. How do I get my guard card in Fort Worth?+
Complete a DPS-approved Level 2 training course in the Fort Worth area — DFW Metroplex Training Academy on West Vickery Boulevard is one of the longest-running DPS-licensed providers in the city. Pass the written exam, submit fingerprints through IdentoGO, and apply through the Texas DPS Private Security portal. Total cost is typically $100–$260 and the process takes 2–4 weeks.
What is the easiest security job to get in Fort Worth?+
The Fort Worth Stock Show is the most accessible large-scale entry-level security job in Tarrant County — they hire hundreds of officers across the 23-day run and are relatively flexible on prior experience. Outside of the Stock Show, residential gate security at Fort Worth apartment communities and event security at smaller Sundance Square venues are the most accessible entry points.
What does entry-level security pay in Fort Worth?+
Entry-level unarmed security in Fort Worth starts at $14–17/hr. Most officers move into the $17–22/hr range within 12–18 months. Overnight and weekend shifts carry $1–3/hr differentials from day one. Stock Show and Dickies Arena event security often pays flexible flat rates with guaranteed minimums.
Is Fort Worth a good city to start a security career?+
Yes — Fort Worth's combination of major annual events (Stock Show, Dickies Arena), growing residential development, active retail corridors, and expanding corporate base in the Alliance corridor provides a solid ladder from entry-level to experienced security work. Officers who start with the Stock Show and build into Alliance corridor and JPS Health Network security have a clear progression path within the Fort Worth market.
How long does it take to get a security job in Fort Worth after getting a guard card?+
For event and residential gate positions — 1–2 weeks from an active commission. Apply to Allied Universal, Securitas, and regional guard firms operating in Fort Worth immediately after your commission activates and express clear availability for evenings and weekends. The Fort Worth market moves faster for entry-level candidates who can work overnight and weekend shifts.
