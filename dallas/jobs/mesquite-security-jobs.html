Mesquite, TX Market

Security Jobs in Mesquite

Mesquite is a major eastern Dallas County suburb of 150,000+ residents — an established industrial and retail market with a distinct security employment profile anchored by its US-80 industrial corridor, the Town East Mall retail zone, and the Mesquite Arena and Rodeo complex that generates significant event security demand. The city's location at the intersection of I-635 (LBJ Freeway) and US-80 makes it a natural distribution and logistics corridor, creating consistent overnight industrial security demand.

The Mesquite Arena is one of the more distinctive security employers in eastern Dallas County — the facility hosts the Mesquite Rodeo (one of the longest-running professional rodeos in the country), concerts, trade shows, and indoor sporting events year-round. Arena event security at the Mesquite facility provides flexible work comparable to smaller Fort Worth Stock Show assignments, with the convenience of a Dallas County location.

Mesquite's residential security market spans a mix of established apartment communities along Gus Thomasson Road and the US-80 corridor and newer residential development in the city's southern growth areas near Lawson Road. The city's proximity to Garland and Balch Springs means Mesquite security candidates are typically part of a broader eastern Dallas County patrol market. Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available in Mesquite?

Industrial security along the US-80 corridor and at Mesquite's I-635 industrial zone is the largest overnight security employment category in the city. Manufacturing plants, distribution operations, and logistics facilities along this corridor require overnight and weekend vehicle patrol coverage that is consistently available for candidates with a guard card, clean driving record, and overnight availability.

Event security at Mesquite Arena provides flexible opportunities across the rodeo, concert, and trade show calendar. The Mesquite Championship Rodeo runs Friday and Saturday evenings from spring through fall — providing a predictable weekly flexible security income stream for candidates available on weekend evenings. Additional events throughout the year add variety and income potential.

Retail security at Town East Mall and the Towne Centre shopping corridor on US-80 provides LP and uniformed floor security opportunities. Town East Mall LP positions are the primary Mesquite retail security employment category — the mall's mix of anchor stores and specialty retailers maintains an active security program year-round.
