Overview

DFW Loss Prevention: Major Retail Corridors, Direct Employer Hiring, and Corporate LP Tracks

The Dallas-Fort Worth retail market is one of the largest in the US - Stonebriar Centre, NorthPark Center, Galleria Dallas, Alliance Town Center, Grapevine Mills, and hundreds of power centers across the metro generate LP demand that keeps Target, Walmart, Home Depot, and Macy's continuously hiring. DFW's population growth means new retail development and new LP positions are a constant feature of the market.

Direct retail employer LP is the highest-paying accessible entry point in the DFW security market that doesn't require a security license. Target's Asset Protection Specialist at $18-$22/hr with a direct employer benefits package and internal advancement track competes favorably with contracted security rates. Apply directly to Target, Home Depot, and Walmart before pursuing contracted LP.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Loss Prevention Officers Do in DFW

Target's Asset Protection Specialist model represents the professional retail LP standard in DFW - deterring, detecting, and investigating external and internal theft; using advanced CCTV and analytics platforms; supporting civil recovery processes; and working with local law enforcement on criminal matters. Target's AP training program is one of the most comprehensive in retail LP.

Mall LP at Macy's and department stores involves floor presence and plainclothes surveillance across multi-floor retail environments. NorthPark Center, Stonebriar, and Galleria Dallas generate consistent LP activity given high traffic volume and merchandise density. The plainclothes surveillance skill set developed in department store LP is the most transferable LP competency in the DFW retail market.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Target Asset Protection Specialist - $18-22/hr, CCTV surveillance, civil detentions, internal investigations
  • Macy's Loss Prevention Detective - NorthPark, Galleria, Stonebriar; plainclothes floor surveillance
  • Home Depot Asset Protection Specialist - multiple DFW locations, external and internal theft prevention
  • Walmart/Sam's Club Asset Protection - DFW network, CCTV monitoring, floor presence
  • Contracted retail LP (AU/Securitas) - Level 2 required, flexible account assignments across DFW
  • ORC investigation support - coordinating with law enforcement on organized retail crime in DFW
  • LP incident documentation - civil detention records, police reports, evidence chain of custody

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 for contracted retail LP - not required for direct retail employer positions (Target, Walmart, Home Depot)
  • Clean background - retail LP involves civil detention authority; felonies disqualify
  • Observation skills and composure - plainclothes surveillance requires patience and professional judgment
  • Reliable transportation across DFW's large geographic spread
  • Valid Texas driver's license
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • Retail hours - typically 8am-10pm coverage with two-shift model
  • Weekends essential - highest retail LP activity Thursday through Sunday
  • Target: full-time and part-time, rotating schedule including evenings and weekends
  • Contracted LP: flexible assignments, pick up shifts across multiple retail accounts
  • Mall LP: follows mall hours with extended holiday season coverage
