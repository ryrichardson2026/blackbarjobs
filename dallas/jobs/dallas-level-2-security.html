Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Level 2 security license in Texas? +
A Level 2 Security Officer Commission is a non-commissioned (unarmed) security officer license issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety. It authorizes the holder to work as a paid security officer without carrying a firearm. It is the standard starting credential for security work in Texas and is required for the majority of security jobs in Dallas.
How do I get a Level 2 security license in Dallas? +
Complete a DPS-approved Level 2 training course (available in weekend intensive formats in Dallas), pass the written exam, submit fingerprints through IdentoGO, and apply through the Texas Online Private Security portal. Total cost is typically $100–$260. The process takes 2–4 weeks from enrollment to active commission.

How much do Level 2 security officers make in Dallas? +
Entry-level Level 2 security in Dallas runs $15–17/hr. Experienced officers earn $17–23/hr. Senior and supervisory Level 2 roles reach $23–30/hr. Corporate campus and healthcare assignments pay at the upper end of each range. Overnight and weekend shifts carry additional $1–3/hr differentials.
What is the difference between Level 2 and Level 3 security in Texas? +
Level 2 is a non-commissioned (unarmed) commission — officers cannot carry a firearm. Level 3 is a commissioned (armed) commission — officers are authorized to carry a handgun on duty. Level 3 requires additional firearms training and range qualification on top of Level 2 credentials, and typically pays $5–10/hr more than comparable Level 2 positions.
Can I upgrade from Level 2 to Level 3 in Texas? +
Yes. To upgrade from a Level 2 to a Level 3 commission, you complete a DPS-approved firearms training course (minimum 40 hours) and pass a live-fire range qualification. You do not need to repeat Level 2 training — the Level 3 builds on your existing commission. Several DPS-licensed training schools in the DFW area offer Level 3 upgrade courses for officers who already hold Level 2.

Role Overview

What Is a Level 2 Security Officer in Texas?

A Level 2 Security Officer Commission is issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety and authorizes the holder to work as a paid, non-commissioned (unarmed) security officer anywhere in Texas. It is the baseline credential for security work in the state — equivalent to what other states call a guard card or security officer license.

To obtain a Level 2 commission, candidates complete a DPS-approved training course, pass a written examination, submit fingerprints, and apply through the Texas Online Private Security portal. The process typically takes 2–4 weeks and costs $100–$260 total. Once active, the commission is valid for two years and requires 6 hours of continuing education to renew.

Level 2 officers in Dallas work across a wide range of environments — from corporate lobby access control in the CBD to overnight warehouse patrol in South Dallas to event security at Deep Ellum venues. The commission covers all of these assignments. Candidates who want to advance to armed roles must subsequently obtain a Level 3 Armed Commission, which requires additional firearms training and qualification on top of the Level 2 credential.
