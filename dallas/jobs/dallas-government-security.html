What government security jobs are hiring in Dallas?+
Active government security openings in Dallas span Federal Protective Service contract positions at federal buildings along the Stemmons and downtown corridors, courthouse security at Dallas County's court complex, City of Dallas municipal facility security, and county administrative building security. Do I need a federal clearance for government security jobs in Dallas?+
For Federal Protective Service contract positions and federal building security, yes — a background investigation is required that goes beyond standard commercial security screening. The investigation covers criminal history, financial history, and personal associations. This process can take several weeks to complete. State and local government security positions (courthouse, city facilities) typically require thorough background checks but not full federal clearance.
How much does government security pay in Dallas?+
Government security in Dallas runs $20–24/hr at entry level for armed government posts. Experienced officers at federal facilities earn $24–31/hr. Senior government security and supervisory roles reach $31–42/hr. Federal Protective Service contract positions are governed by Service Contract Act wage determinations that set minimum rates above typical commercial security pay.
Is courthouse security a good job in Dallas?+
Yes — courthouse security at the Dallas County court complex is one of the more stable and professionally structured security roles in the city. The environment is formal and accountability is high, but schedules are consistent (court hours), pay is above the commercial average, and the experience is directly relevant for officers considering law enforcement careers. The George Allen and Frank Crowley courthouse complexes are among the most active courthouse security employers in North Texas.
What companies hold government security contracts in Dallas?+
Allied Universal, Securitas, and several specialized government security contractors hold FPS and federal facility contracts in Dallas. G4S Government Solutions, Inter-Con Security, and Centerra Group (a Chenega company) are among the firms active in federal government security contracting in the Dallas area. Government contracts are publicly posted and re-bid periodically — watching for new contract awards is a way to identify upcoming hiring activity.
Can I work government security in Dallas without military experience?+
Yes — prior military or law enforcement experience is strongly preferred but not universally required for all government security positions in Dallas. Municipal facility and county administrative building security is accessible to candidates with standard armed security credentials and clean backgrounds. Federal building and courthouse security is significantly more competitive and typically favors candidates with military or law enforcement backgrounds.
Dallas Government Security

Government Security Demand in Dallas

Dallas is home to a significant concentration of federal, state, and local government facilities that require dedicated security coverage. The federal courthouse complex downtown, the Earle Cabell Federal Building, IRS and Social Security Administration offices, and various federal agency field offices along the Stemmons and LBJ corridors collectively represent the federal layer of government security demand in the city. Most federal facility security is contracted through the Federal Protective Service (FPS) and its contracted guard providers.

At the state and local level, Dallas County's courthouse complex — including the George Allen Courts Building, the Frank Crowley Criminal Courts Building, and the Henry Wade Juvenile Justice Center — maintains active security staffing for courtroom access control, inmate transport security, and public area monitoring. The City of Dallas operates dozens of municipal facilities including Dallas City Hall, convention facilities, and public service centers that require security coverage across business and extended hours.

Government security positions in Dallas are among the most stable and professionally structured in the market. Contracts are typically multi-year, turnover is lower than commercial security, and the federal and state regulatory environment ensures consistent pay scales and working conditions. Armed posts at courthouses and federal buildings are some of the best-compensated security positions in the city. Active hiring zip codes include 75201, 75202, and 75207.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Government Security Jobs Are Available in Dallas?

Federal facility security in Dallas involves access control at government buildings, x-ray and screening operations at courthouse entrances, and armed deterrence at high-security federal installations. Federal Protective Service contract officers work under strict federal standards — background investigations, regular firearms qualification, and compliance with DHS contractor requirements apply on top of standard Texas Level 3 requirements.

Courthouse security at Dallas County's court complex is among the most structured government security work in the city — officers manage public screening, courtroom access, and occasional inmate processing support in a high-accountability environment reviewed by judges, attorneys, and county administrators. City of Dallas municipal facility security is less intensive but provides stable weekday daytime schedules across a network of city-owned properties.

Government security experience in Dallas is a meaningful credential for officers interested in transitioning to law enforcement or federal investigative careers. The professional standards, documentation requirements, and government-client exposure of courthouse and federal building assignments build skills and references that are directly relevant to law enforcement application processes.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control public access at federal buildings, courthouses, and government facilities
  • Operate x-ray screening equipment and magnetometers at government facility entrances
  • Provide armed deterrence at high-security federal and judicial facilities
  • Escort authorized personnel through secured areas of government campuses
  • Monitor CCTV and access control systems at government security operations centers
  • Respond to incidents and coordinate with Dallas PD and federal law enforcement as required
  • Maintain detailed security logs and incident reports meeting government documentation standards
  • Complete federal contractor background investigation and compliance requirements

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer Commission required for most government armed posts
  • Federal background investigation clearance — government facility security requires more thorough screening than commercial work
  • Current firearms range qualification — federal facilities verify qualification currency independently
  • Prior military or law enforcement experience strongly preferred and often required for federal courthouse and FPS-contract positions
  • X-ray and magnetometer operation certification required or provided at screening-intensive government posts
  • Clean financial history and no foreign national associations — federal clearance requirements go beyond standard criminal background
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Courthouse and municipal facility security follows government business hours — primarily Monday through Friday daytime shifts
  • Federal building security includes overnight and weekend shifts for 24-hour facilities
  • Courthouse security peaks during court session days — lighter coverage during recesses and holidays
  • Armed government posts are among the most stable schedules in Dallas security — contract-driven consistency
  • Overtime available at federal facilities during high-security periods and special events
  • Holiday schedules vary by agency — federal holidays reduce court security demand significantly
