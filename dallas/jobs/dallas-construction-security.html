What construction security jobs are hiring in DFW?+
Active DFW construction security openings include overnight site watch at residential and commercial build sites across Dallas and Tarrant counties, access control at major commercial construction projects in the Uptown, Alliance, and Legacy corridors, and mobile patrol routes covering multiple active construction sites. What does construction site security involve in DFW?+
Overnight site watch — stationed at an active build site from end of work day through crew arrival the next morning. You walk the site perimeter, document observations, deter theft and vandalism, and respond to any incidents. Most shifts involve extended outdoor exposure in varying weather, walking unfinished surfaces, and operating without supervision for long periods. The primary threats are copper wire theft, tool theft, and HVAC equipment theft.
What does construction security pay in DFW?+
Entry-level construction site watch in DFW runs $15–18/hr with overnight differentials of $1–3/hr. Experienced officers at commercial construction projects earn $18–25/hr. Senior patrol supervisors covering multiple construction sites reach $25–34/hr. Pay has increased modestly as construction theft activity and volume have grown in DFW.
Is construction security dangerous in DFW?+
The primary risk is encountering organized theft groups targeting materials and equipment — officers are trained to observe, document, and call law enforcement rather than physically intervene in active theft situations involving multiple individuals. Slip and fall hazards on unfinished construction surfaces are the most common physical safety concern. PPE including hi-vis vests and steel-toe boots are typically required at major construction sites.
Do I need construction experience for construction site security?+
No — a valid guard card, clean background, and physical readiness for outdoor overnight work are the baseline requirements. Prior construction industry experience is helpful for understanding site layout and operations but is not required for entry-level overnight site watch positions.
How long do construction security assignments last in DFW?+
It depends on the project. Large commercial builds — office towers, multifamily complexes, mixed-use developments — can provide 12–24 months of continuous security coverage. Smaller residential and commercial projects may only need 3–6 months. Guard firms running construction accounts actively manage officer reassignments as projects complete and new projects begin — officers who establish good performance records move between projects with minimal employment gaps.
DFW Market

Construction Security in Dallas–Fort Worth, TX

The Dallas–Fort Worth metro has been one of the most active construction markets in the United States for the past decade — and that activity directly drives security demand. Major commercial, multifamily, and infrastructure projects across both counties require overnight and weekend security coverage to protect equipment, materials, and partially completed structures from theft and vandalism. Construction theft is one of the most significant property crime categories in DFW, with copper wire, power tools, HVAC equipment, and heavy machinery representing the primary targets.

Construction security in DFW is a volume business — the metro has hundreds of active build sites at any given time across residential, commercial, infrastructure, and industrial construction. Guard firms serving DFW general contractors and construction managers maintain active rosters of construction site security officers who rotate across multiple active projects as work phases shift. The construction security employment pool in DFW is consistently active year-round and expands significantly as new major projects break ground.

The most active DFW construction security zones track the metro's development corridors — the Uptown and Design District redevelopment zone in Dallas, the Frisco and Prosper northern growth corridor, the Alliance commercial and industrial expansion in north Fort Worth, the Legacy West and Legacy Business Park zone in Plano, and the ongoing multifamily construction wave across suburban corridors metro-wide. Officers who establish relationships with guard firms running construction accounts access a consistent pipeline of site assignments.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does The Role Look Like?

Overnight site watch is the dominant construction security role in DFW — officers stationed at active build sites from close of work through morning crew arrival, deterring theft, monitoring for unauthorized access, and responding to any on-site incidents. The overnight window is when construction theft activity peaks, making overnight coverage the primary security investment for DFW general contractors and construction managers.

Access control at larger active build sites manages subcontractor, delivery, and vendor access during working hours. Officers manage entry logs, verify credentials for trades and delivery personnel, and enforce site safety protocols at entry points. Access control at major commercial construction projects is a daytime and swing shift role that complements the overnight watch function.

Mobile patrol coverage across multiple active construction sites is common in DFW — guard firms running construction accounts frequently route patrol officers across 4–8 active sites per shift during overnight hours, conducting timed check-ins at each property rather than stationing one officer per site. This model is cost-effective for smaller build sites and provides broader coverage across a guard firm's active construction portfolio.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide overnight site watch at assigned active construction projects
  • Deter theft of copper wire, HVAC equipment, power tools, and building materials
  • Monitor unauthorized access to active build sites during off hours
  • Manage access control and subcontractor credentialing during active construction hours
  • Conduct timed vehicle patrol across multiple construction sites on assigned routes
  • Document and photograph any theft, vandalism, or unauthorized access incidents
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD, Fort Worth PD, or local jurisdiction on construction theft incidents
  • Write shift logs and incident reports for general contractor and construction management review

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Valid driver's license — mobile patrol across multiple construction sites is standard
  • Physical stamina for outdoor overnight work in all weather conditions
  • Comfort in active construction site environments including unfinished structures
  • Overnight availability — the dominant demand window for DFW construction security
  • Prior construction, security, or military background relevant but not required for entry-level site watch
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Overnight shifts (4pm–6am or dusk to dawn) are the primary construction security schedule in DFW
  • Weekend coverage required year-round — construction sites are most vulnerable Saturday and Sunday
  • Shift differentials of $1–3/hr for overnight construction site watch positions
  • Project-based scheduling — assignments shift as build phases change and new projects begin
  • Access control daytime shifts available at larger active commercial construction projects
  • Mobile patrol multi-site routes available for candidates with clean driving records and reliable vehicles
