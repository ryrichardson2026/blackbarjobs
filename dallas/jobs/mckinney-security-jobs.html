McKinney, TX Market

Security Jobs in McKinney

McKinney is one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States — Collin County's largest city by population and one of the most active suburban development markets in the DFW metro. The city's explosive residential growth has been matched by significant commercial, healthcare, and corporate development along the US-75 (Central Expressway) corridor, generating a security job market that has expanded dramatically over the past decade.

The McKinney security market is concentrated in several distinct zones. The US-75 commercial corridor from the Eldorado Parkway interchange through the medical district hosts corporate offices, medical facilities, and retail development that drives daytime and healthcare security demand. The Craig Ranch mixed-use development in western McKinney has become one of the most active security employment zones in Collin County, with PGA headquarters, corporate campuses, and a dense residential base.

McKinney's residential security market — apartment communities, HOA neighborhoods, and gated residential developments across the city's northern and western growth corridors — is one of the largest residential security employment segments in Collin County. Weekend and overnight gate coverage at McKinney's apartment communities is consistently in demand. Active hiring zip codes include 75069, 75070, and 75071.

What Security Jobs Are Available in McKinney?

Corporate campus security along the US-75 corridor and at Craig Ranch is one of the strongest categories in McKinney — the city's concentration of corporate relocations from higher-cost markets has built a professional campus security employment base comparable to Plano and Frisco. Healthcare security at Baylor Scott & White Medical Center McKinney and Collin County's medical office corridor provides stable multi-shift demand.

Retail security at McKinney's major shopping centers — Stonebridge Ranch Town Center, the McKinney Marketplace along US-75, and the Highway 380 commercial corridor — provides entry-level LP and uniformed security opportunities. The city's Historic Downtown entertainment district generates event and nightlife security demand on evenings and weekends.

Residential security across McKinney's rapidly growing apartment and HOA community base is one of the most accessible entry points in the Collin County security market. Gate security at the dozens of apartment communities along Eldorado Parkway, Custer Road, and the Craig Ranch residential corridors provides consistent overnight and weekend positions for candidates with a guard card and reliable transportation.
