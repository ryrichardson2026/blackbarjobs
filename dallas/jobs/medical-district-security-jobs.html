Frequently Asked Questions

What security jobs are hiring in the Dallas Medical District?+
Active Medical District openings span emergency department security at Parkland Memorial, access control and research facility security at UT Southwestern, patient and family security at Children's Medical Center, campus patrol at Clements University Hospital, and parking structure security across the district. How much does Medical District security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level Medical District security runs $17–20/hr above the standard unarmed rate. Experienced officers at Parkland and UT Southwestern earn $20–27/hr. Senior and supervisory roles reach $27–36/hr. Overnight and weekend shifts carry additional differentials on top of the healthcare base rate.
Is Parkland Memorial security a hard job?+
Parkland's emergency department is among the most demanding security assignments in Dallas. As a Level I trauma center processing hundreds of ED visits daily, the security environment involves regular behavioral health crises, combative patients, and high-tension family situations. Officers assigned to Parkland's ED are among the most experienced and well-compensated healthcare security officers in the city. It is not an entry-level assignment — most Parkland security officers build experience at lower-acuity healthcare posts before moving to ED coverage.
Does UT Southwestern hire security officers directly?+
UT Southwestern Medical Center employs security officers as part of its facilities and operations team. Some positions are direct university employment with UT System benefits. Others are staffed through contracted guard services. Both paths are active in the Medical District — check both the UT Southwestern HR portal and major guard firm job boards for current openings.
Do I need healthcare experience for Medical District security jobs?+
Prior healthcare, security, or law enforcement experience is strongly preferred at Parkland and UT Southwestern. De-escalation training is required at some Medical District assignments. Entry-level candidates with strong backgrounds in military or security can access Medical District positions — but expect a competitive hiring process compared to retail or residential security postings.
What is the difference between hospital security and Medical District security in Dallas?+
Medical District security refers specifically to the concentrated campus along Harry Hines Boulevard — Parkland, UT Southwestern, Children's, and Clements. These are academic medical centers and Level I trauma facilities operating at a scale and complexity level above most other Dallas hospital security environments. Pay rates, de-escalation requirements, and professional standards are correspondingly higher than community hospital security positions elsewhere in Dallas.
Medical District

Security Demand in the Dallas Medical District

The Dallas Medical District along Harry Hines Boulevard is one of the largest medical campuses in the southern United States — and one of the most concentrated security employment zones in the entire DFW metro. Within a roughly two-mile corridor, Parkland Memorial Hospital, UT Southwestern Medical Center, Children's Medical Center of Dallas, the William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital, and numerous affiliated clinics, research facilities, and medical office buildings collectively employ hundreds of security officers across all three shifts, 365 days a year.

Parkland Memorial Hospital alone is one of the busiest Level I trauma centers in Texas — processing hundreds of emergency department visits daily and operating with a 24-hour patient census that creates continuous, non-negotiable security staffing requirements. UT Southwestern's research campus adds a distinct security profile: laboratory access control, pharmaceutical research security, and overnight monitoring of sensitive research facilities. Children's Medical Center and Clements University Hospital round out the district's diverse security environment.

Security in the Dallas Medical District is among the most demanding and best-compensated unarmed security work in the city. Officers deal with a genuinely complex patient and visitor population — psychiatric holds, intoxicated individuals, domestic situations, and the daily human urgency of a major trauma and research medical campus. Pay rates reflect this complexity. Active hiring zip codes are 75235 and 75390.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available in the Medical District?

The Medical District offers the full range of healthcare security roles concentrated in one geographic area. Emergency department security at Parkland is the most demanding assignment on the campus — officers regularly manage combative patients, psychiatric crises, and high-tension family situations in a high-volume environment. Access control at UT Southwestern's research facilities involves managing credentialed access to laboratories and pharmaceutical storage areas with strict documentation requirements.

Children's Medical Center security requires a specific type of officer — one comfortable working in a pediatric environment where interactions with distressed parents and families are constant. De-escalation in this context demands empathy alongside authority. Parking structure and perimeter patrol across the district's large campus footprint rounds out the Medical District security profile with more independent, patrol-oriented work.

Officers who build experience in the Medical District are among the most sought-after in the Dallas security market. The combination of healthcare security credentials, high-volume emergency environment experience, and the professional standards required by major academic medical centers translates directly into supervisor and security management opportunities at health systems across the metro.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control access to patient care areas, research labs, and restricted clinical and pharmaceutical zones
  • Respond to Code Gray behavioral health incidents, combative patients, and psychiatric crises
  • Conduct de-escalation with agitated patients, visitors, and family members in clinical environments
  • Patrol hospital campuses, research buildings, parking structures, and perimeter areas
  • Escort staff, patients, and visitors safely between buildings across the Medical District campus
  • Assist clinical staff during medical emergencies and security-sensitive patient care situations
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD and EMS on incidents requiring law enforcement or emergency response
  • Write detailed incident reports reviewed by clinical, legal, and regulatory teams

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required — CPR/First Aid certification required at most Medical District employers
  • De-escalation training strongly preferred and required at Parkland and UT Southwestern for emergency-adjacent assignments
  • Prior security, law enforcement, military, or healthcare background strongly preferred
  • Physical ability to respond quickly and assist with patient management in demanding clinical environments
  • Comfort working with high-acuity patient populations including psychiatric, trauma, and substance-involved cases
  • Strong written documentation — Medical District incident reports are reviewed by clinical, legal, and accreditation bodies
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All three shifts available — Medical District hospitals operate 24/7/365 with no seasonal slowdown
  • 12-hour rotations standard at Parkland and UT Southwestern for most security positions
  • Overnight and weekend shifts carry additional differentials on top of healthcare security base pay
  • Holiday coverage required year-round — medical facilities do not reduce security staffing on holidays
  • Emergency department assignments are highest intensity — typically assigned after a probationary period at lower-acuity posts
  • Research facility and laboratory security available for officers preferring lower patient-interaction environments
