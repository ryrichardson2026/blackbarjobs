Grapevine is one of the most distinctive suburban security markets in DFW — a city that combines major resort-convention hospitality (Gaylord Texan), large-scale family entertainment (Great Wolf Lodge), Fortune 500 corporate campus (Paycom), and DFW Airport adjacency into a security employment base that most comparably sized DFW cities don't approach. The diversity of account types in Grapevine means officers can build genuinely varied credentials without extending past city limits.

Loss prevention at Gaylord Texan is year-round, all-shift work in a prestigious hospitality environment.

Role Overview

What Security Officers Do in Grapevine

Hospitality security in Grapevine is the dominant work type — and it's genuinely different from commercial or industrial security. At Great Wolf Lodge, officers patrol a family water park resort environment, respond to incidents involving children and families, and maintain the visible, friendly security presence that the lodge's family brand requires. The skill set is customer service-first, with incident response capability. The overnight differential at Great Wolf reflects the shift's demand for responsible independent judgment when families are sleeping and staff is minimal.

Corporate campus security at Paycom is the contrast environment — professional, corporate, indoor, with structured overnight console monitoring and technology-integrated security systems. A Fortune 500 payroll company campus has different security protocols than a resort: access control for sensitive data systems, executive and employee safety, and after-hours building security at a software company scale.
