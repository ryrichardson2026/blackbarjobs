Frequently Asked Questions

What theme park security jobs are hiring in DFW?+
Six Flags Over Texas and Six Flags Hurricane Harbor in Arlington are the largest theme park security employers in DFW. NRH2O in North Richland Hills, Legoland and Peppa Pig Theme Park in Grapevine are additional regional employers. Sign up for alerts to be notified when openings are posted.
Does Six Flags hire security in Arlington?+
Yes. Six Flags Over Texas and Hurricane Harbor maintain active security hiring during their operating seasons. Apply through the Six Flags careers portal. A valid Texas Level 2 guard card, clean background, and outdoor work tolerance are the baseline requirements. Hiring typically ramps up in March and April ahead of summer season.
Is theme park security seasonal in DFW?+
The core season runs Memorial Day through Labor Day, but Six Flags extends into fall with Fright Fest and winter with Holiday in the Park. Officers who commit to the full operating calendar including holiday events can work 9–10 months without a significant off-season gap.
What does theme park security pay in DFW?+
Entry-level runs $14–17/hr — at the lower end of the Dallas security range reflecting accessible entry requirements. Zone supervisors earn $17–22/hr. Security supervisors and armed posts reach $22–28/hr.
Is theme park security a good first security job?+
Yes — a solid entry point. The experience managing large crowds in a family environment, responding to medical situations, and maintaining order in a high-volume public space applies directly to better-paying commercial, healthcare, and event security roles. Many officers use theme park security as their starting point before moving into higher-paying categories.
What are the biggest security challenges at DFW theme parks?+
Heat-related medical incidents during summer are the most common response event. Guest altercations in queue lines during high-attendance days are second. Lost children, intoxicated guests at Fright Fest evening events, and prohibited item discoveries at entrance gates round out the primary challenges.

Theme Park & Attraction Security in DFW

The DFW metro is home to several major theme parks and family entertainment attractions. Six Flags Over Texas and Six Flags Hurricane Harbor — both in Arlington — are the largest theme park employers in the region, drawing millions of visitors annually and requiring substantial security staffing across their operating seasons. NRH2O in North Richland Hills, Legoland Discovery Center at Grapevine Mills, and Peppa Pig Theme Park in Grapevine round out the regional market.

Theme park security in DFW is primarily seasonal — core demand runs spring through fall. Six Flags extends its season with Fright Fest (October) and Holiday in the Park (November–January). Spring break weekends and summer holidays create above-normal demand across all DFW attractions.

Theme park security is one of the more accessible entry-level categories in DFW. Six Flags and similar attractions hire candidates with a valid guard card and clean background — prior security experience is helpful but not universally required. The seasonal structure and family entertainment environment suit candidates building their first security resume.

What Does Theme Park Security Look Like in DFW?

Theme park security officers manage crowd flow and guest behavior across large outdoor properties with thousands of simultaneous visitors. The environment is high-energy, family-oriented, and requires a combination of authority and approachability different from nightlife or commercial security work.

Roles include entrance gate screening, queue management, roving patrol across park zones, incident response for guest altercations and medical situations, and after-hours property sweep at close. Water park security adds pool area monitoring and aquatic safety coordination.

Seasonal theme park security is a practical way to gain security experience before transitioning to year-round commercial or healthcare work. Six Flags also converts reliable seasonal officers into year-round roles for holiday events and permanent operations teams.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff park entrance gates — screen guests, check bags, and manage ticketed entry
  • Conduct roving patrol across park zones monitoring guest behavior and safety
  • Manage queue areas and crowd density near high-traffic rides and attractions
  • Respond to guest altercations, medical incidents, and security situations
  • Monitor pool and aquatic zones in coordination with lifeguard staff at water parks
  • Conduct after-hours property sweep and secure park facilities at close
  • Coordinate with local PD and EMS on incidents requiring emergency response
  • Write incident reports for all security events during park operations

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Outdoor work tolerance — theme park security is largely an outdoor role in Texas heat
  • Family and community-oriented demeanor — daily interaction with children and families
  • Physical fitness for extended standing and walking across large outdoor park footprints
  • Weekend and holiday availability required — peak demand falls on Saturdays, Sundays, and school holidays
  • Prior security, customer service, or youth-oriented experience all relevant
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Core operating season runs Memorial Day through Labor Day — peak staffing in this window
  • Fright Fest (October) and Holiday in the Park (November–January) extend demand into fall and winter
  • Spring break weeks and holiday weekends create above-normal staffing demand year-round
  • Day and evening shifts during operating hours — overnight security for property patrol after close
  • Weekend availability is essential — Saturday and Sunday are the highest-attendance days
  • Seasonal to year-round conversion available for returning officers and supervisors
