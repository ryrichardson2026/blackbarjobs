Do I need a guard card for overnight security in Texas? +
Yes. Texas requires a valid Security Officer Commission (Level II) for all paid security work. Armed overnight roles require a Level III commission. Most candidates complete training and obtain their commission within 2–4 weeks through a DPS-licensed training school.

Role Overview

What Does an Overnight Security Officer Do in Plano?

Overnight security in Plano spans three distinct environments depending on the assignment. Legacy Business Park corporate campus overnight shifts involve access control, lobby checks, parking structure patrol, and perimeter monitoring across large multi-building campuses after business hours.

Healthcare overnight assignments at Medical City Plano and Texas Health Presbyterian are higher-intensity — involving patient access management, de-escalation, and coordination with clinical staff in addition to standard security functions. These roles typically require prior security or healthcare environment experience.

Industrial overnight assignments in east Plano are more patrol-heavy, with vehicle routes covering warehouse yards, loading docks, and perimeter fencing across large single-tenant or multi-tenant facilities. A valid driver's license is effectively required for most east Plano overnight assignments.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct scheduled foot and vehicle patrols throughout the shift
  • Monitor CCTV systems and access control panels at corporate and healthcare facilities
  • Control access to Legacy Business Park campuses during after-hours periods
  • Respond to alarms, disturbances, and medical situations
  • Document all shift activity in logs and incident reports
  • Secure building entrances, parking structures, and loading dock areas
  • Coordinate with building management, clinical staff, and law enforcement as needed
  • Deter trespassing, theft, and vandalism during overnight hours
