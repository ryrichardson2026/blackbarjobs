The market is somewhat less saturated than Dallas, making it a competitive environment for candidates who want professional unarmed security work.
Do I need a guard card for unarmed security in Fort Worth?+
Yes. Texas requires a valid Level 2 Security Officer Commission for all paid unarmed security work. The commission is issued by the Texas DPS and requires completing a licensed training course, passing a written exam, and submitting fingerprints. DFW Metroplex Training Academy in Fort Worth is one of the longest-running DPS-approved training providers in the area.
