Do I need a guard card for overnight security in Texas? +
Yes. Texas requires a valid Security Officer Commission (Level II) for all paid security work. Armed overnight roles require a Level III commission. Most candidates complete training and obtain their commission within 2–4 weeks.
Are there overnight security jobs at AT&T Stadium or Globe Life Field? +
Post-event overnight positions do exist at and around both venues — covering property protection after crowds clear until morning. These are typically staffed through contracted guard firms. The best path is to apply with the security firms contracted to those venues and sign up for alerts here.
Is a driver's license required for overnight security in Arlington? +
For most Arlington overnight positions, yes. Industrial sites along I-20 and Mayfield Road are large campuses where vehicle patrol is the standard mode of coverage. A valid Texas driver's license and clean driving record are required for the majority of overnight patrol openings in Arlington.
Do overnight security jobs in Arlington pay shift differentials? +
Yes. Most Arlington employers offer $1–3/hr above base pay for overnight hours. Post-event and Entertainment District overnight assignments often carry premium rates above the standard differential.
Are 12-hour overnight shifts common in Arlington? +
Yes — particularly at I-20 industrial and manufacturing sites. These clients prefer 12-hour rotations to minimize shift handoffs at large facilities. For officers who prefer fewer but longer shifts, Arlington's industrial overnight market is a strong fit.
Arlington Market

Overnight Security Demand in Arlington

Arlington's overnight security market has two distinct faces. The Entertainment District along I-30 generates post-event and overnight property protection demand from AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field, and the surrounding hotels, parking facilities, and retail properties. After major events conclude, overnight officers take over property security until the next morning — covering thousands of vehicles, vendor equipment, and event infrastructure.

The industrial and manufacturing corridor along I-20, Mayfield Road, and Green Oaks Boulevard is Arlington's other major overnight demand driver. A concentration of aerospace manufacturing, automotive parts distribution, and general warehousing operations along these corridors runs continuous operations requiring round-the-clock security coverage.

Construction activity across west Arlington and the continued development of mixed-use properties near the Entertainment District adds a third tier of overnight demand for site security officers. Active hiring zip codes include 76010, 76011, 76013, 76014, and 76017.

Role Overview

What Does an Overnight Security Officer Do in Arlington?

Overnight security in Arlington is more physically demanding than most DFW suburban markets. The combination of large-scale venue properties, expansive industrial campuses, and active construction sites means most overnight assignments involve significant vehicle or foot patrol rather than stationary posts.

Post-event overnight assignments at AT&T Stadium and Globe Life Field involve property sweeps, vendor area security, and perimeter coverage after crowds clear — typically from midnight through 6am on event nights. These are among the higher-paying overnight assignments in the mid-cities area.

Industrial overnight assignments along I-20 and Mayfield Road tend toward 12-hour rotations covering large manufacturing and distribution campuses. Vehicle patrol, gate access control, and perimeter monitoring are the core functions at most of these sites.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct scheduled vehicle and foot patrols across large industrial and venue properties
  • Perform post-event property sweeps and overnight security coverage at Entertainment District venues
  • Control vehicle and pedestrian access at distribution center gates during overnight hours
  • Monitor surveillance systems, alarm panels, and perimeter sensors
  • Respond to alarms, disturbances, and emergency situations across large properties
  • Document all shift activity in logs and incident reports
  • Secure building entrances, loading dock doors, and perimeter gates
  • Deter cargo theft, trespassing, and vandalism during overnight hours
