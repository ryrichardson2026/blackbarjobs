About BlackBarJobs

A security-focused job alert platform built for hardworking professionals in Texas — not a general job board.

What We Do

BlackBarJobs connects security professionals with job openings across Dallas–Fort Worth and Houston. We send instant alerts when new positions post — armed, unarmed, overnight, event, loss prevention, TSA, and hospital security — so you're first in line, not last.

Sign up once, get notified when something matches. No resume required. No account to manage. Just your ZIP code and a phone number.

Who It's For

Security officers, guards, and professionals at every level — from entry-level candidates getting their Level 2 license to experienced Level 3 commissioned officers looking for better accounts. If you work security in Texas or want to, this is built for you.

How It Works

Sign up with your ZIP code and phone number. We match you to security openings in your area based on your location and role preference. When something relevant posts, you get notified directly — no logging in, no searching.

For employers: submit your hiring requirements and we match you with pre-qualified candidates from our captive security network across DFW and Houston.

The Network

Every candidate in our network is a security professional — not a general job seeker who applied to a retail listing. We prequalify candidates against employer requirements before they're ever seen. That's the difference between a staffing platform and a security-specific network.

Contact

For candidate inquiries, use the alert signup on any job page. For employer inquiries, visit employers-dfw or email us directly at hello@blackbarjobs.com.
