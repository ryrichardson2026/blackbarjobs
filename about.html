About BlackBarJobs

A security-specific job network for officers across Texas and Chicago, not a general job board. Security isn't just a job, it's a career, and we built this to treat it like one.

What We Do

We know the job search is stressful, so we built BlackBarJobs to take the friction out of it. No resume to upload, no account to babysit, and no paid subscription, ever. You tell us where you are and what you're after, and we bring the openings to you.

When a security position posts near you, whether it's armed, unarmed, overnight, event, loss prevention, airport, hospital, or warehouse, you hear about it first instead of scrolling past it on a crowded general board. We cover one industry, so the matches actually fit.

For Officers and Future Officers

Just browsing? That's exactly who this is for. You don't need a resume, an account, or a subscription to get value here. Drop your ZIP and a phone number once, and we'll alert you when security work opens near you. That's the whole ask.

New to the field? We walk you through what it actually takes to get licensed and start working, so you're not guessing. Already carded and experienced? We surface the better accounts and the immediate-hire openings that are easy to miss on a general board.

For Employers Hiring Now

We partner with employers who need officers on post, not applications in a pile. Every candidate in our network opted in for security work. These aren't general job seekers who applied to a retail listing by accident.

Tell us your requirements and your markets, and we match you with officers who fit, including candidates prequalified on license level and availability. When you need immediate hires, a security-only audience beats a general board every time. Start at our employer page.

We Know the Requirements

Licensing is where most officers get stuck, so we made it part of what we do. We keep plain, current guides to the requirements in every market we serve, from the Texas Level II and Level III commissions to the Illinois PERC card and the armed FCC pathway, covering training, fees, and renewals.

Our goal is to educate the security community, not just list jobs. When the rules change, we update. When you're ready to get certified or move up, we point you to the real, approved path. Start with the Texas or Illinois requirements guide.

Why a Network, Not a Board

A general board treats a security officer like any other listing. We don't. Everyone here chose security, and every employer here is hiring security. That focus is the whole point. It's what makes the matches faster for officers and the hires better for employers.
