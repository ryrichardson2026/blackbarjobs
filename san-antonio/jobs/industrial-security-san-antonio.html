Overview

San Antonio Industrial Security: Toyota, Port San Antonio, and the South Side

San Antonio's industrial base runs on the south side, anchored by Toyota Motor Manufacturing Texas on Lone Star Pass and its cluster of on-site suppliers, the Port San Antonio aerospace and manufacturing campus, and food, beverage, and consumer plants across the metro. These facilities run all shifts, and their security programs need consistent gate and patrol coverage around the clock.

Industrial security is gate-and-patrol work with a safety edge. Officers control the truck and employee gates, verify contractors, patrol the plant footprint, and enforce site safety and access rules. Contractors like Marksman Security staff major accounts such as Toyota, and some industrial posts extend into emergency response, covering fire, medical, and hazmat alongside security.

Access control and documentation are the core output. Officers log every vehicle and contractor, check credentials at the gate, and maintain the records that keep a plant compliant and secure. Prior gate or plant experience moves officers toward the higher-paying supervisory and emergency-response posts.

Roles & Duties
Role Types

Types of Industrial Security Roles

  • Gate and access control, processing trucks and contractors and verifying credentials at plant entrances
  • Plant patrol, covering the facility footprint, production areas, and perimeter across the shift
  • Contractor and vendor management, badging and escorting outside crews on site
  • Emergency response security, covering fire, medical, and hazmat alongside patrol at larger plants
  • Console and CCTV monitoring from the plant security center
Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff the truck and employee gates, verifying credentials and logging every vehicle and contractor
  • Patrol the plant footprint, production areas, generator yards, and perimeter fencing
  • Enforce site safety, PPE, and access rules across the facility
  • Monitor CCTV, alarm, and fire-suppression systems from the plant security center
  • Screen and badge contractors and vendors, escorting outside crews where required
  • Respond to security, safety, fire, medical, and hazmat situations per the site emergency plan
  • Complete gate logs, inspection records, and incident reports to plant standards
  • Maintain radio contact with the plant security team throughout the shift

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license, required for unarmed plant posts
  • Level 3 commission for armed industrial posts, which pay above unarmed rates
  • Valid Texas driver's license, since plant patrol and multi-gate coverage involve vehicle operation
  • Reliable personal transportation, because south-side plant transit is limited
  • A safety mindset and willingness to follow PPE and plant access rules
  • Prior gate or plant experience preferred, and emergency-response, fire, or hazmat certification a plus for the higher-paying posts
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All-shift 24/7 plant operations, with security coverage across days, swings, and overnights
  • Third shift patrol at plants that run with minimal overnight staff
  • 4x10 and 12-hour patterns common at continuous operations
  • Weekend coverage consistently in demand
  • Consistent voluntary overtime, since plant security understaffing is common
  • Weekly pay common at the larger security firms
