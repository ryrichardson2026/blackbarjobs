What benefits matter most in SA security employment?+
Weekly pay is the top differentiator cited by security officers choosing between SA employers — Sunstates and GardaWorld's DailyPay both address the financial flexibility that bi-weekly pay doesn't provide. After that: 401k match (Sunstates and GardaWorld both offer employer matching contributions), health insurance quality (large employers like Allied Universal and GardaWorld offer comprehensive packages; smaller firms often offer basic coverage only), paid time off (Sunstates includes PTO and premium holiday pay explicitly), and tuition reimbursement (Sunstates is one of the few SA security employers to offer this — meaningful for officers pursuing licensing upgrades or continuing education). For hospital and state accounts, the institutional benefits (county employment at University Health, state benefits at SASH) can significantly exceed what contracted security firms provide.
What full-time schedule patterns are common in SA security?+
The most common full-time schedules in San Antonio security: 5x8 — five 8-hour shifts per week, standard at commercial and corporate accounts (the FT 78230 Medical Center post is Monday–Friday 11am–7pm). 4x10 — four 10-hour shifts, common at industrial and logistics accounts near Port San Antonio and the Toyota corridor. 3x12 — three 12-hour shifts, standard in hospital security at Methodist and University Health campuses; this gives officers four days off per week but means 12-hour physical shifts. Rotating shifts — many full-time SA security positions require availability across all three shifts, particularly at accounts that need 24/7 coverage; Sunstates specifically notes 'all days including weekends' as available. The Allied Universal financial institution post (Mon–Fri swing 4pm–midnight) is an example of a fixed-shift full-time pattern that allows predictable scheduling.
