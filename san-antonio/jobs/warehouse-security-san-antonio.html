What is Port San Antonio and why does it matter for industrial security?+
Port San Antonio is a 1,900-acre aerospace, manufacturing, and defense campus on the former Kelly Air Force Base on San Antonio's southwest side. Tenants include Boeing, StandardAero, Lockheed Martin, General Dynamics, and dozens of defense and aerospace contractors. Port San Antonio generates industrial security employment across two distinct tiers: standard industrial patrol and access control for the campus's commercial tenants, and defense-adjacent secured-facility security requiring background investigation compatibility and DoD contract compliance. The Boeing and StandardAero facilities at Port SA handle military aircraft maintenance and modification — security at these accounts operates under DoD facility security protocols rather than standard commercial post orders. Veterans with industrial security backgrounds are the most competitive candidates for Port SA accounts.

Overview

San Antonio Industrial Security: Toyota, Port SA, and the South Side Manufacturing Belt

San Antonio's industrial security market is anchored by three geographic concentrations. South SA — where Toyota Motor Manufacturing Texas runs a 3-shift continuous production operation on over 2,000 acres — generates the most visible warehouse and industrial security demand in the city. Marksman Security's presence at the Toyota plant is the most directly accessible entry point: flex hours at $17.50/hr, Fridays and Saturdays required, and the credentialing that comes from securing one of the most recognized manufacturing facilities in Texas.

Port San Antonio's 1,900-acre aerospace and defense campus is the second concentration — a distinct employment environment where Boeing, StandardAero, and defense prime contractors generate security demand that ranges from standard access control to DoD-cleared facility security. The cleared tier ($28/hr per Securitas CIS SA postings) is inaccessible without an active federal security clearance, but standard industrial patrol at Port SA is accessible with a Level 2 license and a background that passes DoD suitability review.

The third concentration is the distribution and logistics corridor along I-35, I-10, and Loop 410 — truck yards, travel centers, distribution centers, and manufacturing facilities that Premier Protective Security and Prosegur serve at $18.25/hr and $15–$17/hr respectively. This is the broadest and most consistently hiring segment of SA's industrial security market, with Premier Protective actively posting multiple SA locations simultaneously.

Role Overview

What Warehouse and Industrial Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Standard warehouse and industrial security in San Antonio follows a consistent pattern regardless of employer: perimeter patrol of the facility and parking areas, access control at entry gates (verifying credentials for employees, contractors, and delivery drivers), CCTV monitoring from a central console or guard post, incident logging in daily activity reports, and coordination with facility management on maintenance issues, safety hazards, and emergency responses. The physical environment — large warehouse floors, loading docks, outdoor perimeter — requires officers who are comfortable on their feet for extended shifts and alert to both vehicle and pedestrian traffic in active logistics environments.

The Toyota plant adds the manufacturing-specific dimension: officers understand that production operations don't stop for security issues, so responses need to be efficient and minimally disruptive to the production line. Gate officers at automotive plants manage a constant flow of parts suppliers, logistics vendors, and employee vehicles across multiple entry points simultaneously — it's higher-tempo access control than a standard commercial building post.
