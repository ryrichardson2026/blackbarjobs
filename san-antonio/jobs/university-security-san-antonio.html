Frequently Asked Questions

What universities in San Antonio hire security officers?+
San Antonio has a substantial higher education security employment base. UTSA (University of Texas at San Antonio) is the largest — 42,000+ students, Carnegie R1 research university, Hispanic-Serving Institution designation, and one of the fastest-growing universities in Texas. UTSA's Department of Public Safety (DPS) employs police officers, public safety officers, access control assistants, security system technicians, and other civilian security roles. Alamo Colleges District — the community college network operating San Antonio College, Palo Alto College, Northwest Vista, Northeast Lakeview, and St. Philip's — runs a Department of Public Safety with a 24/7 dispatch operation (current posting closes July 27, 2026 at 2222 N. Alamo St.). Trinity University (private, 2,600 students), UIW — University of the Incarnate Word (8,000+ students, multiple campuses including the main campus and several health professional campuses), and St. Mary's University are all active SA higher education security employers.
Does UTSA hire its own security or use contracted firms?+
Both. UTSA's Department of Public Safety directly employs police officers and public safety officers who carry peace officer status, as well as civilian roles including access control assistants and security system technicians. These are state employment positions through the UT System with UT System benefits. However, UTSA also contracts additional security services through firms like Allied Universal for specific accounts, events, and overflow coverage. The UTSA DPS pages confirm they continuously seek applicants for police officer, public safety officer, and telecommunications officer roles. For civilian security roles at UTSA, position openings are listed through UTSA's People Excellence (HR) portal. Security system technician and access control assistant roles are technical positions distinct from patrol officer roles.
What does campus security pay in San Antonio?+
Campus security pay in San Antonio runs $15–$21/hr depending on institution and role type. Alamo Colleges District positions (state community college employment with state benefits) follow the district's pay plan. UTSA police and public safety officers follow UT System compensation, which for peace officer roles runs above standard contracted security. Contracted security serving SA university accounts through Allied Universal and similar firms runs $15–$18/hr at the standard contracted market rate. Saint Mary's Hall (an SA prep school, not university) posts a security officer position with competitive campus pay. For EISD (East Central Independent School District), which shows up in SA campus security searches, entry-level is $19.48/hr per their Police Pay Plan P2. The premium for direct university employment vs. contracted security is meaningful — state and UT System employment includes retirement, health benefits, and tuition waivers that contracted security doesn't approach.
What is the Alamo Colleges District and how does their security work?+
The Alamo Colleges District is one of the largest community college systems in Texas, operating 5 campuses across San Antonio: San Antonio College, Palo Alto College, Northwest Vista College, Northeast Lakeview College, and St. Philip's College. The District's Department of Public Safety operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across all campuses and district locations. The DPS dispatches both police officers and security officers. A verified Alamo Colleges DPS posting (closing July 27, 2026) seeks a dispatcher at 2222 N. Alamo St., SA TX 78215 — the posting requires 6 months of public contact, dispatching, or related experience, high school diploma or GED, and the ability to work all shifts in the 24/7 operation. Security officer positions at the district follow comparable requirements to the dispatcher role.
What's the difference between university police officer and university security officer roles in SA?+
At UTSA and Alamo Colleges, the distinction is significant. Public Safety Officers and police officers at UTSA carry Texas peace officer licenses — they can make arrests, carry firearms under peace officer authority, and operate as commissioned law enforcement within their jurisdiction. This requires TCOLE (Texas Commission on Law Enforcement) licensing, which is a separate track from civilian security licensing. Security officers and access control assistants at universities are civilian roles — they hold Texas Level 2 or Level 3 security licenses (not TCOLE peace officer), perform patrol, access control, event security, and monitoring functions without arrest authority. For officers interested in the law enforcement pathway, UTSA and Alamo Colleges' peace officer roles represent a meaningful step; for those building civilian security credentials, the access control and security technician civilian positions are the relevant track.
Overview

San Antonio Campus Security: The Education Pillar in a University City

Education is the fourth pillar of San Antonio's economy — the city hosts dozens of colleges and universities ranging from UTSA's 42,000-student R1 research campus to the Alamo Colleges District's five-campus community college network to Trinity University's intimate liberal arts environment. Together they create a campus security employment market that spans direct university employment (with state or UT System benefits), contracted security through Allied Universal and other firms, and specialized campus law enforcement through UTSA Police and Alamo Colleges DPS.

UTSA's growth trajectory makes it the most significant driver of SA campus security employment. As the third-largest research university in Texas with over 42,000 students and 17,000 faculty and staff across the Main Campus (north SA) and Downtown Campus, UTSA's Public Safety department employs across multiple civilian and sworn roles. The university's R1 designation and 2022–2023 Forbes Best-in-State Employer recognition signal an institution that takes its employment seriously — campus security employment at UTSA carries institutional credentials that travel well in the Texas higher education security market.

The Alamo Colleges District's 5-campus 24/7 DPS operation creates consistent all-shift security demand across the community college network. With campuses distributed across San Antonio from the northwest (Northwest Vista) to the southeast (Palo Alto), Alamo Colleges security serves a geographically diverse student population with a security model that mirrors small-city policing rather than single-campus corporate security.

What Campus Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Campus security in San Antonio spans two distinct work environments. UTSA's Main Campus in north SA is a large, residential-adjacent research university with dormitories, research labs, athletic facilities, and thousands of daily visitors — the full complexity of a small city's security needs concentrated in a few hundred acres. Access control at research lab entrances, event security for UTSA Roadrunner athletic events at the Convocation Center, overnight residential area patrol, and CCTV monitoring across a multi-building campus are all part of the civilian security officer's scope.

Alamo Colleges' multi-campus dispatch environment is distinct — the 24/7 DPS operation coordinates security responses across five campuses simultaneously, requiring radio communication discipline, incident logging, and coordination with both campus security officers and local law enforcement. For officers who want to develop dispatch and coordination skills alongside patrol work, Alamo Colleges DPS offers an environment that civilian commercial security rarely replicates.
