How does Allied Universal's Anchor Shift model work in San Antonio?+
Allied Universal's Part Time Enhanced positions in San Antonio follow an 'Anchor Shifts' model: you are hired for a designated site and weekly shift that serves as your reliable base schedule, requiring a six-month commitment. On top of that base, you can pick up additional shifts through Allied Universal's Claim a Shift platform — the mobile scheduling tool that shows available open shifts at other SA accounts, letting you build your income beyond your anchor hours. This means a part-time officer can start with a predictable baseline schedule (say, weekends at a corporate account) and layer in extra shifts at warehouse, hospital, or event accounts as availability allows. The model is designed specifically for candidates supplementing income from another job, students, or anyone who wants security work without committing to a full 40-hour week from day one.

What is the difference between part-time and flex at Allied Universal?+
Allied Universal uses two distinct part-time designations in their SA postings. 'Part Time Enhanced' (or 'Part Time') positions have Anchor Shifts — a fixed designated site and weekly shift with a 6-month commitment. You know exactly where you're working and when. 'Flex' or 'Security Flex Officer' positions have no fixed site or anchor shift — you use the Claim a Shift platform to pick up available shifts at various accounts across SA. Flex gives maximum schedule flexibility but less schedule predictability. The Downtown Tower flex role explicitly notes 'must be able to work any shifts to include nights, weekends and holidays' and may be called upon to work with little advance notice. For candidates who want reliable part-time income, the Anchor Shift Part Time Enhanced model is the more structured choice. For candidates with fully variable availability, Flex at $15/hr lets them work as much or as little as their schedule allows.

What Part-Time Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Part-time security in San Antonio covers the full range of account types — corporate campus, warehouse, armed retail, event, and hotel — with schedule flexibility as the differentiating factor rather than role type. The Allied Universal Corporate Part Time Patrol role covers commercial real estate accounts with all-shift availability. The Armed Part Time Enhanced role handles retail armed patrol including sales floor coverage, entrance/exit monitoring, parking area patrol, and crowd management during deliveries and store events. The Tower Patrol is commercial high-rise security in a fixed-building environment.

Event security is the most purely part-time model in SA: Allied Universal Event Services roster officers pick up Alamodome and AT&T Center assignments on the event calendar with no fixed schedule commitment. A part-time event roster officer might work five shifts during Fiesta week in April, then three Spurs home games in January, then two NCAA events at the Alamodome — all as flexible add-on assignments rather than a regular schedule. For SA officers who want to maximize hourly earnings per shift worked rather than maximize total weekly hours, event security is the most efficient model.
