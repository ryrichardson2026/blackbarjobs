San Antonio Loss Prevention: Military Market, North Star Mall, and Direct Employer Hiring

San Antonio's retail LP market is driven by a military consumer base that creates distinctive retail patterns - JBSA's 80,000+ military and civilian personnel, combined with the tourist economy around the River Walk and downtown, generate retail activity that keeps Target, Walmart, H-E-B, and major mall retailers continuously hiring LP staff across Bexar County.

Direct retail employer LP (Target AP Specialist at $17-$21/hr, Walmart AP Associate, Home Depot AP Specialist) is the most accessible entry point in the SA security market that doesn't require a security license. H-E-B, the dominant Texas grocery chain, also employs LP across its SA store network. For candidates who want retail LP specifically, direct employer applications before contracted LP is the recommended sequence.

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 for contracted retail LP - not required for direct positions (Target, Walmart, Home Depot, H-E-B)
  • Clean background - retail LP involves civil detention authority; felonies disqualify
  • Observation skills and composure - plainclothes surveillance requires patience and professional judgment
  • Reliable transportation across San Antonio's geographic spread
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • Weekends essential - highest retail LP activity Thursday through Sunday
  • Target: full-time and part-time, rotating schedule including evenings and weekends
  • Mall LP: follows mall hours with extended holiday season coverage
  • H-E-B: flexible schedule across store network, full-time and part-time available
