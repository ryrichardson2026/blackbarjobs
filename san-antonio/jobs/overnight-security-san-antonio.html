Overview

San Antonio Overnight Security: River Walk, Military Adjacent, and a Persistent Shortage

San Antonio's overnight security market is consistently undersupplied across every major employer. The River Walk hotel corridor runs 24/7 with overnight security demand at 40+ hotels year-round. Military-adjacent residential communities surrounding JBSA need overnight gate and patrol coverage seven nights a week. The South Texas Medical Center's hospital network runs full overnight staffing across multiple campuses.

Officers with genuine 3rd shift availability and a clean background place faster in SA security than any other candidate profile. GardaWorld's FLEX DailyPay model and Sunstates' weekly pay with overnight differential make the financial argument for overnight work clear - you earn more, place faster, and have first choice of available shifts across the metro.

River Walk hotel overnight is the most distinctive overnight security environment in SA - 40+ hotels concentrated in a walkable corridor, each with overnight officers for guest safety, CCTV monitoring, late-night incident management, and the hospitality-oriented professionalism that the tourism economy demands. Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license for unarmed overnight
  • Level 3 for armed overnight accounts - adds $1-3/hr to overnight rate
  • Reliable transportation - overnight transit not available across most of San Antonio
  • Genuine 3rd shift availability - the single most valuable attribute in the SA overnight market
  • Valid Texas driver's license for patrol and mobile overnight roles
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • 3rd shift (10pm-6am or midnight-8am) - highest demand, highest differential
  • 2nd shift (2pm-10pm or 3pm-11pm) - bridges day and overnight; also in high demand
  • River Walk hotels: all-week overnight coverage year-round
  • Military-adjacent residential: 7 nights/week including holidays
  • GardaWorld FLEX: text-to-pickup, any overnight shift across SA accounts
