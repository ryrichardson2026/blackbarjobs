Overview

San Antonio Armed Security: Military-Adjacent, DoD Contractor, and Government Accounts

San Antonio's armed security market is defined by JBSA and the DoD contractor ecosystem that surrounds it. Armed security positions at defense contractor facilities, government buildings, and military-adjacent accounts require Level 3 officers with backgrounds compatible with DoD-adjacent security clearance requirements - making SA's armed market distinctly different from Houston's energy sector focus or DFW's corporate campus emphasis.

Sunstates, Allied Universal, and GardaWorld all maintain active SA armed accounts at rates running $20-28/hr. Officers with prior military backgrounds - especially veterans of JBSA installations - are the most competitive candidates for SA's armed security market, combining Level 3 licensing with the operational background and DoD familiarity that SA's government-adjacent clients value.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Armed Security Officers Do in San Antonio

DoD contractor facility security in SA means armed access control and physical security at defense contractor offices, research facilities, and support operations surrounding JBSA. Officers at these accounts must present professionally in a government contractor environment, maintain strict access logs, and operate with the understanding that the facilities they protect support active military operations.

Downtown SA and River Walk armed security has expanded as the entertainment district has grown - officers at nightlife venues on the River Walk and in the entertainment corridors downtown provide armed deterrence and incident response in a hospitality-oriented environment where professional de-escalation is as important as armed readiness.

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer license - minimum age 21, 45-hour course, MMPI, DPS range qualification
  • Own firearm and duty gear - required at most SA armed positions
  • Clean background compatible with DoD-adjacent security requirements - prior military is an asset
  • Level 4 PPO for executive protection accounts
  • Veteran status is a significant competitive advantage for SA's government-adjacent armed market
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • All shifts including overnight - overnight armed is highest demand and best-paying in SA
  • GardaWorld: walk-in hiring for armed positions, all shifts, DailyPay
  • Sunstates: all shifts all days, $22/hr+ weekly pay
  • DoD contractor facilities: business hours primary with 24/7 perimeter coverage
  • River Walk armed: evening and overnight, Thursday-Saturday entertainment district peak
