Frequently Asked Questions

What civilian security jobs exist at JBSA in San Antonio?+
Joint Base San Antonio comprises three primary installations — JBSA-Lackland (Lackland AFB, including Medina Annex and Camp Bullis), JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, and JBSA-Randolph — plus eight additional operating locations and 266 mission partners. Defense Consulting Services holds a contracted security position at JBSA-Lackland and Randolph AFB called the HARPS Guard I (Security Specialist). HARPS stands for Healthcare Access Response and Protection System — officers monitor electromagnetic door locks, door contact switches, and duress alarms; operate CCTV systems (Identipass, Genetech, and Premisys access control platforms); manage the security console's paging system; maintain security logs; and coordinate radio communications with Security Forces and facility management. It is a 24/7 position, 8-hour shifts, 365 days a year. DSI Security posts full-time Army Base Security Officer positions in SA, Monday–Friday 12-hour shifts, explicitly stating 'no experience is necessary; we will train you.'
What is the JBSA HARPS Guard role and who can apply?+
The HARPS Guard I (Security Specialist/HARPS Guard I) at JBSA-Lackland and Randolph is a contracted civilian position held by Defense Consulting Services. The role monitors the Healthcare Access Response and Protection System — the access control, CCTV, and alarm infrastructure for the medical treatment facilities on base. Contractors report to DCS supervisors and the base installation Security Forces. Specific duties include monitoring Identipass, Genetech, and Premisys access control systems (with initial training provided by those systems' representatives); operating and observing CCTVs; using the government-provided radio system; operating the HARPS/Information Desk intercom master station; utilizing the overhead paging system for emergency codes; maintaining the security log; and activating control devices for interior and exterior doors. The role requires 12 months of experience as an alarm monitor or dispatcher (military or civilian) for some positions, though a floater/part-time version is also available. US citizenship is required for base access.
Do I need a security clearance for JBSA security positions?+
It depends on the specific role. The Defense Consulting Services HARPS Guard positions require base access credentials (fingerprinting and background check) but not necessarily a full federal security clearance — the role operates within the medical facility access control system rather than in classified environments. However, Allied Universal posts 'Armed Security Officer – DoD Top Clearance' and 'Armed Security Officer – Armed Patrol – DoD Clearance' specifically in San Antonio for roles at defense-related facilities requiring active federal security clearances (Secret or higher). Securitas Critical Infrastructure Services posts at $28/hr in SA requiring an active DoD Top Secret/SCI Full Scope Polygraph clearance — this is a specialized tier for candidates with existing clearances from prior military or intelligence community service. Port San Antonio AOCC dispatcher roles require background investigation compatibility but not necessarily full clearance.
Why are veterans the most competitive candidates for SA military base security?+
San Antonio has the deepest veteran pipeline in Texas — JBSA's three installations continuously transition service members into the civilian workforce. Military base security roles favor veterans for documented reasons: existing base access familiarity, security protocols training (force protection, access control, badge verification), physical security discipline, and in many cases existing federal security clearances that civilian candidates cannot obtain without years of government investigation processing. Defense Consulting Services lists previous military or civilian alarm monitoring experience for their HARPS roles. DSI Security's Army Base post — while explicitly training from scratch — aligns naturally with veteran candidates given the base environment. Allied Universal's active DoD-clearance armed positions in SA are effectively accessible only to candidates with pre-existing clearances from military or intelligence community service.
What does JBSA-adjacent security pay?+
The range is wide because of the clearance premium. Standard contracted base-adjacent security (DSI Army Base post, entry-level HARPS Guard) runs $15–$18/hr, consistent with the SA contracted security market. Defense Consulting Services' HARPS Guard positions follow their contracted government rates. DoD-cleared contracted positions through Securitas Critical Infrastructure Services at $28/hr (TS/SCI Full Scope Poly required). Allied Universal's DoD clearance armed roles do not list public rates but pay above standard commercial armed security. Port San Antonio AOCC dispatcher roles follow city-adjacent contractor compensation. ZipRecruiter's JBSA average covers all role types and skews high due to technical contractors — for physical security specifically, the standard contracted range is $15–$20/hr for non-cleared roles, $28–$35+/hr for cleared positions.
Overview

San Antonio Military Base Security: The Largest Joint Base in DoD

Joint Base San Antonio is the largest joint base in the Department of Defense — three primary installations, eight operating locations, and 266 mission partners spread across San Antonio and the surrounding region. It directly employs over 80,000 people and serves as the training hub for the U.S. Air Force (JBSA-Lackland houses Basic Military Training), the Army Medical Center of Excellence (JBSA-Fort Sam Houston), and the Air Education and Training Command (JBSA-Randolph). This scale creates civilian security contracting demand that has no parallel in DFW or Houston.

The HARPS Guard I role at JBSA-Lackland and Randolph represents the most accessible civilian entry point into JBSA-specific contracted security — operating the Healthcare Access Response and Protection System requires training on government-provided systems (provided by the government), US citizenship, and clean background, but does not require a federal security clearance. This makes it accessible to candidates who want JBSA-adjacent security work without the years-long clearance investigation process.

The DoD-cleared tier — Securitas CIS at $28/hr with TS/SCI Full Scope Poly, Allied Universal's armed DoD clearance patrols — is accessible only to candidates with existing clearances from military or intelligence community service. For transitioning service members with active clearances and a Texas Level 3 license, this tier represents the highest-paying contracted security work available in San Antonio.

Role Overview

What Military Base Security Officers Do in San Antonio

The HARPS Guard at JBSA-Lackland operates from the centralized security console — monitoring access control systems, evaluating CCTV feeds across the medical treatment facility, answering telephone calls within the control area, operating the paging system for emergency codes, coordinating radio communications with Security Forces, and maintaining the security log with control device activations and door releases. It is console-centered, high-attention, documentation-intensive work with immediate coordination responsibility during any alarm or security incident on the installation.

DSI Security's Army Base post is patrol-oriented — the full-time Monday–Friday 12-hour shift at the SA Army Base post focuses on access control, perimeter patrol, and the professional demeanor required in a federal government environment. DSI trains candidates from scratch for this environment. For transitioning service members who want to remain connected to the base environment while building civilian security credentials, this post offers the most familiar work environment in SA's contracted security market.
