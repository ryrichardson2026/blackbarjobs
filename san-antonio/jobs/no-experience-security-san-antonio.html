Do I need a Texas Level 2 license before applying with no experience?+
Not at all employers, but it depends on where you apply. GardaWorld's walk-in events and DSI Security's no-experience posting do not require a license at hire — they support you through the licensing process. What backgrounds transfer into security with no prior experience?+
The most transferable backgrounds for SA security without direct experience: military service (JBSA transitions thousands of veterans annually — military discipline, access control, and firearms experience are all valued); customer service and retail (the interpersonal skills directly transfer to visitor management, lobby security, and hotel accounts); hospitality (River Walk hotel staff who move into security bring strong guest interaction skills); delivery and logistics drivers (situational awareness, route familiarity, and reliability transfer to mobile patrol); and first responder training or EMT experience (highly valued at hospital and campus accounts). Even without any of these, being 18+, having a clean background, reliable transportation, and demonstrating reliability in interviews gets you in the door at GardaWorld and DSI.

San Antonio: One of the Most Accessible Entry-Level Security Markets in Texas

San Antonio's security market is one of the most accessible for candidates without prior experience because of two structural factors that don't exist at the same scale in DFW or Houston. First, JBSA continuously transitions thousands of service members annually — the veteran influx into the civilian workforce has normalized rapid security employment and built an employer culture in SA that is accustomed to training people from scratch rather than requiring civilian security experience. Second, the city's hospitality-heavy economy — driven by River Walk tourism, Fiesta, and the convention center — creates consistent event and hotel security demand for entry-level candidates with strong customer service skills rather than security credentials.

The practical access points are clear: GardaWorld's Tuesday walk-in events at 84 NE Loop 410 make same-week hiring genuinely possible. For candidates willing to show up, San Antonio has fewer barriers to entry-level security employment than any comparable Texas market.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Entry-Level Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Most entry-level positions in San Antonio fall into three categories. Construction security — the most accessible no-experience track, patrolling active construction sites, controlling access, and ensuring only authorized personnel and equipment enter the site. Securitas' SA construction hiring event at $16/hr is the model. The work is outdoor, physical, and straightforward: know who belongs and who doesn't, log everything, and call the supervisor when something unusual happens.

Fixed commercial post security is the second track — sitting or standing at a building entrance, verifying IDs, directing visitors, and monitoring CCTV. The interpersonal component is high; the incident rate is usually low. It's the ideal environment to develop the routine, documentation, and professional presence that employers evaluate before moving officers to more demanding accounts. Hotel and hospitality security is the third — River Walk hotels and event venues hire entry-level officers for lobby and parking patrol, leveraging customer service backgrounds rather than security experience.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Construction site access control — verify credentials, log authorized entries, prevent unauthorized access
  • Fixed commercial post — lobby presence, visitor management, ID verification, CCTV monitoring
  • Parking lot and perimeter patrol on foot or vehicle at assigned commercial accounts
  • Write accurate daily activity reports and incident reports per employer post orders
  • Hotel and hospitality security — lobby patrol, guest assistance, parking structure monitoring
  • Army Base post security (DSI) — access control and patrol with full on-the-job training provided
  • Monitor alarm systems and respond to alerts at assigned commercial accounts
  • Maintain professional appearance and communication at all times — customer-facing roles require this from day one
