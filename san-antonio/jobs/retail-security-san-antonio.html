What Retail Security Officers Do in San Antonio

H-E-B LP is grocery-specific — floor observation across high-transaction environments, CCTV monitoring across departments including pharmacy and prepared foods (the highest-shrink categories), active ORC surveillance, and the community-facing professional demeanor that H-E-B's brand demands. LP officers at H-E-B interact with San Antonio neighbors constantly and represent a brand that the city's residents feel genuine loyalty toward.

La Cantera mall and resort LP is observation-first, action-last. Plain-clothes coverage, multi-camera coordination, and team communication between LP officers characterize the luxury retail environment. Individual theft is less frequent than at grocery LP but more complex when it occurs, and ORC incidents require multi-store coordination and thorough case documentation. Allied Universal's armed mall patrol at La Cantera adds the deterrence dimension — visible armed presence in parking areas and perimeter zones that plain-clothes LP alone doesn't provide.
