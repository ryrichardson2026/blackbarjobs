What is the difference between entry level and no experience in SA security?+
In practice, the distinction is about where you are in the licensing and career track. 'No experience' applies to candidates who have zero background in security and may not yet have their Level 2 license. 'Entry level' covers candidates who may already have their Level 2, have completed their first 0–6 months in the industry, and are applying to roles that list a license as required or strongly preferred — with some willingness to take candidates at the very start of their career. Kerberos SA, for example, requires a current Texas Level 2 card as listed in their posting, but trains candidates free of charge — suggesting they're targeting candidates at the transition point between 'no experience' and 'entry level.' Securitas' entry level SA role does not require prior security experience but lists the standard Level 2 licensing requirement. The practical advice: get your Level 2 first, then apply to entry level SA positions at Kerberos, Securitas, Tarian, and United Protective Services simultaneously.

What should I do in my first year of SA security to maximize career growth?+
Three things that compound fastest in the first year. First, get your Level 2 immediately if you don't have it — the SA training school network makes this achievable in two weeks for under $100. Second, prioritize site diversity over rate in months 1–6: working commercial, hospital, and residential accounts in SA in your first year builds a profile that commands $18–$20/hr by month 12, while staying at a single low-complexity post caps your growth. Third, get your CPR/First Aid and IAHSS Basic certifications as early as possible — these cost under $100 total and open hospital, hotel, and campus accounts that are consistently better-paying than standard commercial accounts. Officers who pursue Level 3 armed licensing by month 9–12 and enter SA's armed market see the most significant pay jump in the SA security career arc.

Role Overview

What Entry Level Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Entry level security in San Antonio typically starts with fixed-post assignments: access control at a building entrance, lobby monitoring at a commercial property, patrol of a defined campus area, or visitor management at a corporate facility. These accounts build the fundamental documentation and observation habits — writing accurate daily activity reports, managing visitor logs, operating CCTV consoles, and communicating with supervisors — that define a security officer's professional baseline.

The most impactful early entry points in SA are commercial buildings and medical-adjacent accounts. Commercial building access control at the 1604 corridor or Medical Center builds professional demeanor in a low-incident, customer-facing environment. Medical-adjacent accounts (outpatient clinics, medical office buildings near the South Texas Medical Center) build awareness of healthcare security without the full complexity of a hospital ER. Both environments create the documentation habits and professional track record that make a Level 2 officer competitive for the $17–$20/hr positions within their first year.
