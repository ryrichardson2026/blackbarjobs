FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TSA hiring at San Antonio International Airport?+
Yes. TSA's official SAT airport fact sheet confirms the agency is currently assessing candidates for part-time Transportation Security Officer positions at San Antonio International Airport. The current starting hourly rate for TSO positions is $17.87/hr with an 18% locality pay component already included. Part-time positions run 16–32 hours per week with shifts available any day Sunday through Saturday including nights, holidays, and irregular hours. New hire training requires up to six weeks of full-time duty travel away from SAT — employees are paid for compensable hours and reimbursed for authorized travel expenses. Apply at jobs.tsa.gov and search for San Antonio International Airport (SAT).
What is the TSA hiring process for SAT?+
After applying on USAJOBS or jobs.tsa.gov: Computer-Based Test (CBT) — a standardized assessment of the skills needed for TSO work. Structured interview — evaluates decision-making and ability to work with teams and the public. Drug screening. Job-related medical evaluation including color vision test, vision exam, hearing exam, and adequate joint mobility exam — the physical evaluation includes confirming the ability to repeatedly lift and carry up to 50 pounds, stand for 3–4 hours without a break, and walk up to three miles during a shift. Pre-hire background investigation. Final placement into Best Qualified, Highly Qualified, or Qualified category. The process can take several months from application to start date. Candidates who pass all assessments are placed in a hiring pool for SAT and notified when positions are filled.
What federal benefits do TSA TSOs receive at SAT?+
TSA federal employment includes a comprehensive benefits package: health insurance (Federal Employees Health Benefits program — one of the broadest health plan selections available), dental and vision insurance, life insurance and long-term care insurance, retirement through the Federal Employees Retirement System (FERS) with pension component, Thrift Savings Plan (TSP — the federal equivalent of a 401k) with agency matching contributions, Flexible Spending Account, Employee Assistance Program, personal leave days, and paid federal holidays. Uniforms are provided. Additional benefits may include transportation subsidies, tuition reimbursement, health and wellness programs, and access to fitness centers. TSA also offers military spouse hiring preference — spouses of active-duty members, 100% disabled veterans, or service members killed on active duty receive preference in the scheduling of required assessments.
Who else hires airport security officers at SAT besides TSA?+
ACTS-Aviation Security Inc. (subsidiary of ICTS Europe, established 2001, operating at 30+ US airports) has an active Aviation Security Agent posting at San Antonio International Airport. ACTS focuses on aviation security functions and catering inspection — screening food deliveries, galley carts, and supplies before they go airside per TSA protocols. The City of San Antonio Human Resources directly employs Airport Security Officer I and Airport Security Supervisor at SAT — both requiring TSA Security Threat Assessment clearance and CJIS fingerprint-based background check. Airport Security Supervisors coordinate with TSA and manage the city's airport security staff. Port San Antonio at the former Kelly AFB employs Airport Operations Communications Center (AOCC) dispatchers for their aviation and security operations.
What is the TSA Security Threat Assessment and who needs it?+
The TSA Security Threat Assessment (STA) is a federal background check required for anyone who needs access to the sterile (post-security) areas of a commercial airport or other restricted aviation areas. It is required for City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer I, Airport Security Supervisor, and ACTS-Aviation Security Agent roles at SAT — these are not TSA employment positions but they operate in TSA-regulated environments and must clear the STA. The STA is conducted by TSA using the TSA eQIP background investigation system. Disqualifying factors include certain criminal convictions, immigration status issues, and other factors specified in TSA's credentialing regulations. The STA also requires a CJIS (Criminal Justice Information Services) fingerprint-based background check for City of SA airport security positions. US citizenship is required for airside access at SAT.
What is Port San Antonio and what airport security roles does it have?+
Port San Antonio (formerly Kelly AFB, IATA: SKF) is a 1,900-acre aerospace, manufacturing, and defense campus on the former Kelly Air Force Base on SA's southwest side. It houses Boeing, StandardAero, Lockheed Martin, and other defense contractors and operates as a civilian airport alongside its industrial operations. The Airport Operations Communications Center (AOCC) at Port San Antonio is a security and operations dispatch function — coordinators monitor security equipment, CCTV systems, access control, and manage communications across the 24-hour facility. The AOCC role requires knowledge of FAA and FAR regulations and prior aviation industry exposure. Port SA's combination of defense, aerospace, and civilian airport operations makes it the most security-intensive aviation employment environment in SA outside of SAT itself.

San Antonio Airport Security: TSA, City Employment, and Aviation Contractor Roles

San Antonio International Airport (IATA: SAT) served over 9 million passengers in 2023 and operates as a mid-size international gateway connecting to over 40 non-stop destinations across the US and to Mexico. The airport's security employment spans three distinct tiers: federal TSA employment (TSO positions at $17.87/hr with full federal benefits), City of San Antonio direct employment (Airport Security Officer I and Supervisor with city benefits), and contracted aviation security (ACTS-Aviation Security agents performing catering inspection and airside functions).

TSA is currently assessing part-time candidates for SAT — the part-time pathway runs 16–32 hours per week with the flexibility to pick up additional shifts and the federal benefits that full-time TSO employment provides. For candidates interested in federal employment, TSA's part-time pathway at SAT is the most accessible federal security job in San Antonio. The six-week paid training program — conducted away from SAT with reimbursed travel — is one of the most comprehensive paid onboarding programs of any security employer in the SA market.

Port San Antonio adds a fourth dimension — a 1,900-acre aerospace and defense campus with its own airport (Kelly Field) and AOCC operations. For candidates with aviation industry background or interest in defense-adjacent airport security, Port SA offers a uniquely SA employment environment that no other Texas city replicates.

Role Overview

What Airport Security Officers Do in San Antonio

TSA Transportation Security Officers at SAT operate the security checkpoints that screen passengers and baggage before they enter the sterile area of the terminal. The work combines physical screening (magnetometers, body scanners, baggage X-ray operation) with passenger interaction, prohibited item identification, and the steady procedural discipline that federal security screening requires across thousands of daily passengers. TSOs rotate through checkpoint positions — document checker, X-ray operator, Advanced Imaging Technology operator, and lane lead — developing competency across all screening functions over time.

City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer I works within TSA's regulatory framework while employed by the city — patrolling terminal secure areas, checking air carrier ramps and access points for compliance with TSA regulations, managing the SIDA (Security Identification Display Area) program, and coordinating with TSA, airport operations, and SAPD. ACTS-Aviation agents work the airside supply chain — inspecting catering deliveries and galley carts before they board aircraft, ensuring no prohibited items enter through the catering channel.
