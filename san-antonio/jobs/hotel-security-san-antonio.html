What is the River Walk's hotel security employment landscape?+
The San Antonio River Walk's 15 miles run through the core of downtown hospitality — major hotels within the River Walk corridor include the Grand Hyatt (at the Convention Center), JW Marriott San Antonio Hill Country Resort, Marriott Rivercenter, Hilton Palacio del Rio, La Quinta Inn by Wyndham SA Riverwalk (303 Blum), and dozens of boutique and limited-service properties. Each of these employs security either directly or through contracted firms. The River Walk's 24/7 tourist activity — peaking during Fiesta in April, major convention weekends, and holiday seasons — means overnight and weekend security at River Walk hotels is consistently undersupplied and commands schedule premium or higher hourly rates.
Is hotel security in San Antonio different from other security work?+
Yes — in three important ways. First, the customer service component is dominant. Hotel security officers are the first point of contact for guests who feel unsafe, have lost a room key, or witnessed an incident. The ability to communicate professionally, de-escalate without escalating the guest's anxiety, and resolve situations without creating a scene is the primary skill employers select for. Second, the reporting environment is specific — most major SA hotel properties use web-based incident management systems where officers log activities digitally rather than on paper logs. The Hyatt Regency SA posting specifically notes 'ability to complete security reports in a web-based system with minimal guidance.' Third, hours follow hotel operations — peak demand is late evening through 2am on weekends, and the overnight shift at any large SA hotel carries the full security responsibility for the property.
What experience do luxury SA hotel LP roles require?+

The St. Anthony, A Luxury Collection Hotel, notes candidates with a valid security license are strongly preferred along with hotel LP experience of at least 6 months. For officers entering the hotel security market, the accessible pathway is economy-to-mid-scale hotels first (Drury, La Quinta, Hyatt Vacation Club at $17.60/hr), then building toward luxury resort LP with 1–2 years documented hotel security experience.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Hotel Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Hotel security in San Antonio is defined by the Hyatt Vacation Club posting's job description, which mirrors what every major SA hotel expects: patrol all areas of the property by foot or vehicle, administer first aid/CPR to guests or associates as required, ensure guest and meeting rooms are secure, assist guests with room access, monitor CCTV and perimeter alarm systems, duress alarms, and fire/life safety systems, and respond to the scene of accidents to determine if emergency aid is required. It's comprehensive safety work, not just access control.

At luxury properties (La Cantera, Hotel Emma, St. Anthony), the LP dimension adds theft prevention, incident investigation, and the specific protocols that protect high-value guests and luxury merchandise. At River Walk hotels, the overnight officer manages the wind-down from the entertainment district — coordinating with SAPD on the River Walk, responding to intoxication incidents, and maintaining the professional calm that large hotels need to project even when adjacent outdoor venues are at peak activity.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Foot and vehicle patrol of hotel property — all areas including parking, perimeter, pool, and common spaces
  • Monitor CCTV, perimeter alarm systems, duress alarms, and fire/life safety systems
  • Respond to guest incidents — medical emergencies, accidents, safety concerns; administer first aid/CPR
  • Assist guests with room access and resolve security-related concerns
  • Loss prevention at luxury properties — theft deterrence, incident investigation, high-value merchandise protection
  • Maintain digital daily activity reports using hotel web-based incident management systems
  • Emergency response team member — fire, medical, and evacuation procedures
  • Coordinate with SAPD on River Walk corridor incidents and escalated hotel security events
