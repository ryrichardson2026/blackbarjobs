TSA is currently assessing candidates for part-time TSO positions at San Antonio International Airport (SAT). What does the City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer role involve?+
The City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer I is a municipal employment position at SAT. Overview

San Antonio Airport Security: SAT, Port San Antonio, and Federal Aviation Roles

San Antonio International Airport (SAT) served over 9 million passengers in 2023 and operates as a mid-size international gateway with connections across the US and to Mexico. The airport's security employment spans three distinct employment tiers: federal (TSA Transportation Security Officers, with part-time positions currently being assessed), city employment (City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer I and Supervisor), and contracted aviation security (ACTS-Aviation Security agents performing catering inspection and airside security functions).

Port San Antonio at the former Kelly AFB adds a fourth dimension — a 1,900-acre industrial-aviation campus housing Boeing, StandardAero, and defense contractors with its own airport security and operations dispatch infrastructure. Port SA's combination of military heritage, active aerospace manufacturing, and civilian airport operations creates security roles that are distinct from commercial terminal security and more closely aligned with defense facility and industrial aviation security.

For candidates interested in aviation security, San Antonio offers a progression path: start with ACTS-Aviation Security agent work at SAT to build airport badge credentials and aviation familiarity, then compete for City of SA Airport Security Officer positions as they open, and eventually target TSA positions if federal employment is the goal. The airport badge requirement — involving TSA STA clearance and CJIS fingerprinting — is the qualifying threshold for all paid aviation security work at SAT, and building a clean record that passes those checks is the foundational requirement.

Role Overview

What Airport Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Aviation security at SAT operates on two tracks. The ACTS-Aviation Security catering inspection track focuses on the supply chain that reaches aircraft — inspecting food deliveries, galley carts, and supplies before they go airside, ensuring no prohibited items enter the aircraft through the catering channel. This is procedural, regulation-driven work that requires knowledge of TSA catering inspection protocols and strong attention to documentation. Officers work in an industrial-aviation interface environment rather than a passenger terminal.

The City of San Antonio Airport Security Officer track is public-facing and compliance-oriented — patrolling terminal secure areas and public access areas, checking air carrier ramps and access points for compliance with TSA regulations (TSR 1540, TSR 1542, and the Airport Security Program), conducting employee and tenant security training, and managing the ACE SIDA (Security Identification Display Area) program. These officers work directly within TSA's regulatory framework and coordinate regularly with TSA, airport operations, and SAPD.
