Overview

San Antonio Event Security: Spurs, Alamodome, Fiesta, and River Walk Festivals

San Antonio hosts the Spurs (NBA) at the AT&T Center, major college football and events at the Alamodome, and one of the richest annual festival calendars in Texas - Fiesta San Antonio in April, the Texas Folklife Festival, Valero Texas Open, Wurstfest, and the San Antonio Stock Show and Rodeo all generate event security demand across the metro throughout the year.

BEST Crowd Management holds contracts at major SA venues. Getting on event security rosters at multiple firms gives officers access to the full SA event calendar - NBA games, college bowl games, major concerts at the Alamodome, and the year-round River Walk festival circuit - at $15-21/hr depending on the venue and event type.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in San Antonio

AT&T Center security for Spurs games involves entry screening and crowd management for 19,000+ attendees across one of the NBA's most consistently attended arenas. Officers conduct bag checks, operate magnetometers at entry gates, manage concourse crowd flow during game operations, and respond to incidents in coordination with SAPD and arena operations staff.

Fiesta San Antonio is the most operationally unique event security environment in Texas - ten days of citywide festivals across dozens of venues simultaneously, with events ranging from intimate neighborhood celebrations to massive outdoor gatherings at Fiesta Flambeau and the Fiesta Battle of Flowers parade. Officers working Fiesta develop crowd management versatility across multiple venue types in a compressed ten-day window.',

Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license for unarmed event positions
