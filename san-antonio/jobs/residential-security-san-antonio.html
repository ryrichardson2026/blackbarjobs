What are the gated communities in San Antonio that hire security?+
San Antonio's gated community security market is tiered by HOA dues and security level. The 24/7 guard-staffed tier includes The Dominion (one of SA's most prestigious gated communities), Shavano Park, and Anaqua Springs Ranch — these run HOA dues of $350–$500/month and maintain 24/7 manned entry points with full perimeter patrol. The staffed-entry tier includes Rogers Ranch and The Heights at Stone Oak — gate staffing with evening patrol at the $250/month HOA range. Electronic access communities in Alamo Ranch along Loop 1604 operate at $150–$250/month with keypad or RFID entry and roving security patrol rather than manned gates. Security patrol demand also exists throughout the master-planned communities of Fair Oaks Ranch, Helotes, Boerne, and Cibolo adjacent to SA's city limits.
What does HOA gate guard work pay in San Antonio?+
ZipRecruiter's June 2026 data shows the average hourly pay for gate guard in SA at $15.39/hr, with most positions running $13.12–$16.59/hr. Statewide Patrol's SA unarmed postings run $30,000–$37,000/year (roughly $14.42–$17.79/hr). Blue Skies of Texas Courtesy Officer follows their residential security compensation structure. Standard HOA gate guard and residential patrol work in SA runs $14–$16/hr for entry-level unarmed positions, with patrol supervisory and armed residential roles trending $17–$18/hr. The premium for 24/7 guard-staffed community accounts (The Dominion tier) over electronic-access patrol accounts is reflected in overnight and weekend differentials consistent with the rest of the SA market ($1–$2/hr above base).
What does a Courtesy Officer at Blue Skies of Texas do in San Antonio?+
The Blue Skies of Texas Courtesy Officer role at their SA complex covers: making rounds by walking, driving a company vehicle, or golf cart patrol of the entire exterior; observing and responding to unauthorized persons or vehicles; directing and controlling traffic and parking for large functions; providing transportation to and from Freedom House as requested; acting as First Responder for problems and emergencies; regulating emergency vehicle entry and exit; monitoring security and patrolling grounds and buildings. The role requires a valid Texas driver's license in good standing, insurable under Blue Skies carriers, and professional demeanor consistent with a residential community environment. Blue Skies of Texas is a faith-based senior living and community organization — the Courtesy Officer role is positioned as a community service function, not just a deterrence post.
Is residential security different from other security work in SA?+
Meaningfully so. The primary difference is community relationship. Residential security officers interact with the same residents repeatedly — gate guard officers become recognizable faces to homeowners who pass through the entry point daily. The professional standard is hospitality-adjacent rather than deterrence-oriented: knowing residents by face, assisting with guest access, providing information about community amenities, and handling rule enforcement (parking violations, noise complaints) with the tact that a neighbor interaction requires rather than the authority that a commercial security incident demands. Officers who thrive in residential security tend to have strong interpersonal communication, comfortable knowledge of the community's specific rules and layout, and the patience for work where 95% of interactions are routine and pleasant — a very different professional profile from hospital, construction, or event security.
Overview

San Antonio Residential Security: Gated Communities Across 355 Square Miles

San Antonio's residential security market is shaped by the city's extraordinary geographic scale — 465 square miles of city proper, surrounded by master-planned communities and suburbs that add another 355+ square miles of residential development in Bexar County alone. The concentration of gated communities on the north side (Stone Oak, Rogers Ranch, The Dominion, Shavano Park), the northwest side (Helotes, Alamo Ranch, Deerfield), and the Hill Country adjacent communities (Fair Oaks Ranch, Boerne suburbs, Cibolo) creates a diverse residential security market ranging from 24/7 manned gate operations to periodic roving patrol contracts.

The Dominion is SA's most prominent guard-staffed gated community — 24/7 entry point staffing and full perimeter surveillance at the $350–$500/month HOA tier. Rogers Ranch and The Heights at Stone Oak represent the next tier: staffed entry gates with evening patrol coverage at $250/month HOA dues. These accounts create consistent, relationship-oriented employment for officers who want structured residential security work in established communities. Alamo Ranch's electronic access communities create demand for mobile patrol accounts that cover multiple neighborhoods on a circuit — a model that Statewide Patrol and Cascadia Global Security serve explicitly in SA.

Blue Skies of Texas adds the senior living and faith-based community dimension — the Courtesy Officer role at their SA complex is one of the most community-service-oriented security positions in the SA market, requiring vehicle patrol, emergency first response, and genuine resident service orientation within a specific institutional community setting.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Residential Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Gate guard at SA's 24/7 residential communities is access management by relationship. At The Dominion and comparable communities, officers manage two-way traffic at the entry point — verifying resident access credentials, logging visitor vehicles, confirming contractor authorizations with community management, and maintaining the professional presence that high-end community residents expect. The interaction quality matters: gate officers who know regular residents by face and treat guests with the same professional courtesy as any hotel concierge are the ones who get long-term placement on premium residential accounts.

Mobile HOA patrol across the Alamo Ranch corridor, Stone Oak, and Helotes is geographically larger and more autonomous — driving a circuit through multiple communities, checking for vehicles that don't belong, observing for property damage, parking violations, and security incidents, and logging patrol activity with smartphone-based reporting tools. Statewide Patrol's SA unarmed officers post $30k–$37k/yr for this type of work with no experience required — making HOA patrol one of the most accessible paid patrol roles in the SA market.
