FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officer job seekers in Chicago.

Do I need a PERC card to apply for security officer jobs in Illinois?
▼
Yes. Illinois requires a Permanent Employee Registration Card (PERC) from the Department of Financial and Professional Regulation, plus completion of a 20-hour approved training course, to work as a security officer. Some employers hire candidates who are still in the process and give a short window to finish. Entry-level and unarmed roles tend to be the most flexible while your PERC is pending.
How do I get an Illinois PERC card?
▼
Apply through the IDFPR online CORE system, complete the 20-hour training from an approved provider, submit fingerprints for the background check, and pay the 55 dollar fee. A PERC is valid for three years. Most applicants are cleared within a few weeks. Armed work requires additional steps.
What is the difference between armed and unarmed security officer roles?
▼
Unarmed officers carry no firearm and focus on access control, patrol, and observation. Armed officers carry a firearm and work higher-risk sites like banks, dispensaries, and government buildings. In Illinois, armed work requires you to be 21 or older with a valid FOID card, a Firearm Control Card, extra firearms training, and range qualification on top of your PERC. Armed roles usually pay several dollars an hour more.
What does a security officer earn in Chicago?
▼
In the Chicago metro, unarmed officers generally earn about 18 to 20 dollars an hour depending on experience and site. Armed roles run roughly 22 to 25 dollars an hour. Overnight and weekend shifts often add a differential. Specialized campus and corporate posts can pay more, and federal roles like TSA include benefits that raise total compensation.
Do security officer jobs drug test?
▼
Most security employers require a pre-employment drug screen, and many test randomly during employment. This is especially common for armed roles, federal positions, and assignments at hospitals, government sites, and industrial facilities. Expect a standard drug test as part of hiring.
Can I work security with no experience?
▼
Yes. Many unarmed roles are entry-level and do not require prior security experience. A PERC card, a clean background check, and reliable transportation are usually the core requirements. Customer service, retail, military, and hospitality backgrounds all count as relevant. Event security is often the easiest way in.
What is the interview process like for security officer jobs?
▼
Most are straightforward: a phone screen followed by one in-person meeting. Employers ask about your availability, work history, and how you would handle common situations like a disturbance, a medical call, or a trespasser. Having your PERC card or one in progress, a clean background, and reliable transportation ready to confirm moves things along. Large firms like Allied Universal and Securitas often hire faster than smaller contractors.
