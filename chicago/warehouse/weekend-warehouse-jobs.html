Getting hired

What the process usually looks like from applying to your first shift.

  1. Apply onlineMost Chicago warehouse employers use a short form, not a resume upload. Ten minutes.
  2. Phone screen or textUsually within two to five days. They confirm your shift availability, whether you can lift the stated weight, and how you will get to the facility.
  3. On-site interview or hiring eventOften a walk-through of the floor. Larger distribution centers run open hiring events where you can interview the same day you show up.
  4. Background check and drug screenStandard at most large facilities. Cannabis is legal in Illinois, but employers can still test and can withdraw an offer over a positive result.
  5. Start dateTwo weeks from application is typical. Peak season can be days.

Forklift certification

OSHA requires certification to operate a powered industrial truck, so it is one of the most requested add-ons in warehousing. Most Chicago employers pay for it after hire, and an online course alone does not certify you: OSHA also requires a hands-on employer evaluation. Holding the classroom portion still helps you start faster and broadens the roles and shifts you can be placed in.

Shifts, pay structure and benefits

Weekend schedules

Two patterns dominate: weekend-only roles, which pair well with a weekday job or classes, and Friday-through-Sunday blocks at sites that run heavy weekend volume. Overnight weekend shifts stack the shift differential on top.

Weekly pay

Roughly a third of Chicago warehouse employers pay weekly, which suits weekend workers using the role as a second income.

Benefits

Full-time roles at larger employers typically include health coverage after 30 to 90 days, a 401(k) with some match, and paid time off that accrues by hours worked. Illinois also requires up to 40 hours of paid leave a year under the Paid Leave for All Workers Act. Part-time and seasonal roles usually carry less.

What the work is actually like

Weekend warehouse work is the same picking, packing, sorting, and loading as any other shift, run on Saturday and Sunday when many facilities still move volume. Some sites treat the weekend as a lighter crew; others run full weekend operations because e-commerce does not stop.

The draw is scheduling. Weekend-only roles pair cleanly with a weekday job, weekday classes, or childcare that flips on weekends, and they compete less because fewer people want them. That makes weekend shifts a practical way to add income without giving up a weekday commitment.

The tradeoff is transit and staffing: weekend service can be thinner, and a smaller crew means you carry a bit more of the floor. If weekends work for your life, the reduced competition is a real advantage.

Common questions

Are weekend warehouse jobs weekend-only?

Some are. This board carries both weekend-only roles and Friday-through-Sunday blocks. The listing states the pattern, so read before you apply.

Do weekend shifts pay more?

Pay matches weekday rates, and some sites add a weekend or shift premium. Overnight weekend shifts stack the overnight differential on top of the $20 median.

Can I work weekends around a weekday job?

Yes, that is the main draw. Weekend-only roles pair cleanly with a weekday job, classes, or childcare, and they compete less because fewer people want them.

Do I need experience for weekend work?

Often no. Many weekend roles are entry level, especially sort and handler positions, and employers train the process.

Is the weekend crew smaller?

Sometimes. Some sites run a lighter weekend crew, which can mean you carry a bit more of the floor. Others run full weekend operations because e-commerce volume does not stop.

What about getting there on a weekend?

Weekend transit service can be thinner, especially early or overnight. Confirm the commute before you accept, particularly in the I-55 and I-80 corridors.

Do weekend roles have benefits?

Full-time weekend roles at larger employers carry the same benefits as any full-time schedule. Weekend-only part-time roles usually carry less.

Will I be drug tested?

Many large facilities test regardless of schedule. Illinois allows lawful off-duty cannabis use, but employers can still enforce zero-tolerance policies.

Can weekend work lead to full-time?

Yes. Reliable weekend workers are often first in line for weekday or full-time openings when volume grows.

How do I raise my pay on weekends?

An overnight weekend shift adds the shift differential, a dollar to three dollars an hour. Forklift certification, usually employer-paid, broadens the roles you qualify for.
