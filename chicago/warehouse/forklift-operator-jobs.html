Forklift Operator Jobs in Chicago

Chicago and the surrounding metro run one of the densest warehouse and distribution corridors in the country, from the I-55 and I-80 lanes out to the Joliet and Elwood intermodal yards. Openings turn over every week across picking, packing, loading, and equipment roles.

Most roles move product on a set shift: receiving, picking orders, packing, staging, and loading trucks to a rate and accuracy target. Equipment roles pay more and may ask for certification.

What You Need

  • Forklift or powered equipment experience helps
  • OSHA-aligned certification for some roles (some train on site)
  • Reliable transportation to the facility
  • Able to lift and stand for a full shift

Common Duties

  • Receive, pick, pack, and stage product
  • Load and unload trailers safely
  • Hit shift rate and accuracy targets
  • Keep the dock and aisles clean and organized

Frequently Asked Questions

Do forklift operator jobs in Chicago need experience?

Many do not. Entry level and no experience roles are common, and this page lists current openings, some of which hire without prior warehouse work.

What shifts are available?

Openings span day, overnight, night, and weekend schedules across the Chicago metro. Filter the full board to the shift you want.

Do I need a forklift certification?

Some forklift roles train and certify on site; others want a current OSHA-aligned certification. Each listing states what it requires.
