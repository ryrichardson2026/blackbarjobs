Overview

Overnight Security Officer Jobs in Chicago: The Night-Shift Market

Overnight is one of the strongest corners of the Chicago market. Data centers around Elk Grove Village, the distribution and logistics corridor through Joliet, Aurora, and Bolingbrook anchored by CenterPoint Intermodal, hospitals, corporate lobbies after hours, and industrial sites all run around the clock and need officers on nights. Overnight almost always pays a differential: Northwestern Memorial, for example, adds about $2 an hour for the night shift, and logistics sites in Will County do the same, so night officers often out-earn their day-shift counterparts at the same post.

Night work often pays a shift differential on top of the base rate, so overnight officers frequently out-earn their day-shift counterparts at the same site. For people who prefer quieter posts and steadier routines, the overnight shift is a solid fit.

Role Overview

What Overnight Security Officers Do in Chicago

Overnight officers run perimeter and interior patrols, staff gatehouses and access points, monitor cameras and alarms, and check locks and equipment through the night. With fewer people on site, observation and reliable reporting carry more weight.

The collar-county logistics corridor is thick with distribution centers and data centers that staff overnight gatehouse and patrol posts, which makes this the best on-brand fit for officers who want night hours near the suburbs.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Overnight perimeter and interior patrol rounds
  • Gatehouse and access control for after-hours entry
  • Monitoring CCTV and responding to alarms
  • Lock, door, and equipment checks through the shift
  • Logging activity and writing incident reports
  • Coordinating hand-off with the incoming day shift
Compensation

What This Role Pays in Chicago

Chicago overnight officers typically earn 18 to 23 dollars an hour once the shift differential is included. Data center and industrial posts tend to pay at the higher end, and leads earn more.

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED
  • Consistent overnight availability
  • Reliable transportation, since overnight transit runs limited
  • Alertness and dependable attendance through long shifts
  • Clean background check
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to firms staffing data centers, distribution sites, and hospitals
  • Confirm you can commit to a consistent overnight schedule
  • Ask about the shift differential when comparing offers
  • Corridor sites in Joliet, Aurora, and Bolingbrook hire heavily for nights
  • Most overnight posts are full-time with some part-time weekend options

FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officer job seekers in Chicago.

Do overnight security jobs pay more in Chicago?
▼
Often yes. Night posts usually add a shift differential on top of the base rate, so overnight officers frequently earn more than day-shift officers at the same site, commonly landing in the 18 to 23 dollar range.
What sites hire overnight officers in Chicago?
▼
Data centers, distribution and logistics sites through Joliet, Aurora, and Bolingbrook, hospitals, corporate lobbies, and industrial facilities. Many run 24/7 and need dedicated night coverage.
Do I still need a PERC card for overnight work?
▼
Yes. Overnight roles carry the same Illinois requirements as day roles: a PERC card and the 20-hour training. Armed overnight posts also require the FOID and Firearm Control Card.
Is overnight security quieter than day shift?
▼
Usually. With fewer people on site, the work leans toward patrol, monitoring, and access control. Reliability and good reporting matter most on nights.
How do I handle transportation for overnight shifts?
▼
Plan ahead, since overnight transit runs on a limited schedule in much of the metro. Reliable transportation is one of the most common requirements employers list for night roles.
