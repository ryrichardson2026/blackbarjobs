Overview

Industrial Security Officer Jobs in Chicago: Critical-Infrastructure Posts

Industrial Chicago spans rail yards, the Calumet industrial corridor, utilities, chemical and energy sites, and heavy industrial parks. These are critical-infrastructure-adjacent posts, often 24/7, and some are armed.

The work is access control, gatehouse, and patrol at sites where security is taken seriously. Officers with a clean record and reliability do well here, and armed-qualified officers have more options.

Role Overview

What Industrial Security Officers Do in Chicago

Officers control access at industrial gates, staff gatehouses, patrol on foot and by vehicle, secure rail, dock, and utility sites, and watch for safety and hazard issues. Coordination with site operations is constant.

Some industrial posts call for armed officers given the risk profile, which opens higher-paying assignments for FOID and Firearm Control Card holders.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Industrial site access control and gatehouse coverage
  • Foot and vehicle perimeter patrol
  • Rail yard, dock, and utility site security
  • Hazard and safety observation
  • Coordination with plant and site operations
  • Incident and activity reporting

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • FOID card and Firearm Control Card for armed posts
  • Minimum age 18, or 21 for armed assignments
  • Reliability and a clean background check
  • Comfort at industrial and critical-infrastructure sites
  • Valid driver's license for vehicle patrol posts
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to industrial security contractors across the metro
  • Armed-qualified officers have access to more and higher-paying posts
  • Note your shift availability, since sites run 24/7
  • Keep your PERC current, and your FCC too if you work armed
  • Highlight any industrial, rail, or utility experience

Are industrial security posts armed?
