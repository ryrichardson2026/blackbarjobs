Overview

Warehouse Security Officer Jobs in Chicago: The Logistics Corridor

Will County and the western collar counties form one of the largest inland distribution hubs in North America. The CenterPoint Intermodal Center in Elwood and Joliet, built on the former Joliet Arsenal around Union Pacific and BNSF rail yards, hosts tenants like Amazon, Home Depot, and Walmart, and the fulfillment centers spreading through Aurora, Bolingbrook, and Romeoville run around the clock. Warehouse and distribution security surges in the fourth quarter, when holiday-fulfillment volume peaks and sites add gatehouse, trailer-yard, and overnight patrol coverage.

Warehouse security is gatehouse, gate access, and patrol work, not warehouse-floor labor. It is one of the steadiest sources of overnight posts in the metro and a strong on-brand fit for night-shift officers.

Role Overview

What Warehouse Security Officers Do in Chicago

Officers staff the truck gate and gatehouse, check trailers and seals, verify driver and visitor check-in, patrol the yard and docks, and monitor cameras. The focus is access control and asset protection at high-throughput sites.

The logistics corridor runs 24/7, so overnight gatehouse and patrol posts are common and steady, and many add a shift differential.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Truck gate and gatehouse access control
  • Trailer and seal checks
  • Driver and visitor check-in and verification
  • Yard and dock patrol
  • CCTV monitoring
  • Incident and activity reporting
Compensation

What This Role Pays in Chicago

Chicago warehouse and distribution security typically pays 18 to 22 dollars an hour. Overnight gatehouse posts often add a differential, and leads earn more.

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED
  • Overnight availability for many corridor posts
  • Reliable transportation, since corridor sites have limited transit
  • Dependable attendance and alertness
  • Clean background check
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to firms staffing the Joliet, Elwood, Aurora, and Bolingbrook corridor
  • Note your overnight availability, since many posts are night gatehouse
  • Ask about the shift differential when comparing offers
  • Corridor distribution sites hire heavily for nights
  • Keep your PERC current, since all posts require it

What does a warehouse security officer do?
