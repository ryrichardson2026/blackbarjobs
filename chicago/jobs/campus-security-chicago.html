Overview

Campus Security Officer Jobs in Chicago: A Deep Higher-Ed Base

Chicago has one of the densest higher-education bases in the country: the University of Chicago in Hyde Park, UIC on the Near West Side, Northwestern in Evanston and its downtown Streeterville campus, DePaul in Lincoln Park and the Loop, Loyola on the North Side, plus Columbia College, IIT, and the City Colleges. Many run in-house public-safety departments, some with sworn campus police, and the medical campuses at UChicago, UIC, and Northwestern blend campus and hospital security. The work is community-oriented and steady, and in-house departments often add benefits.

The work spans foot, bike, and vehicle patrol, safety escorts, building and residence-hall access, and event coverage. It suits officers who want a service-first environment and consistent schedules.

Role Overview

What Campus Security Officers Do in Chicago

Campus officers patrol grounds and buildings, provide student and staff safety escorts, control access to residence halls and labs, cover campus events and games, and respond to incidents. Many campuses run their own police alongside security, and officers coordinate closely with them.

The community-facing nature of the work rewards approachability and good judgment. In-house roles at universities often come with benefits and a clearer path to advancement.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Foot, bike, and vehicle patrol of campus grounds and buildings
  • Student and staff safety escorts
  • Access control at residence halls, labs, and facilities
  • Event and game-day coverage
  • Emergency and incident response
  • Coordination with campus police and Chicago PD

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • Community and customer-service orientation
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED
  • Clean background check
  • CPR and first aid certification is a plus
  • Valid driver's license for vehicle patrol posts
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to university public-safety departments and campus security contractors
  • In-house university roles often add benefits and advancement
  • Highlight community, customer-service, or first-aid experience
  • Patrol posts may require a valid driver's license
  • Keep your PERC current, since campus roles require it

FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officer job seekers in Chicago.

What does a campus security officer do?
▼
Campus officers patrol grounds and buildings, provide safety escorts, control access to residence halls and labs, cover events, and respond to incidents, often coordinating with campus police.
How much do campus security officers make in Chicago?
▼
Typically 19 to 23 dollars an hour, with in-house university departments often adding benefits. Supervisors and campus dispatchers earn more.
Which Chicago schools hire campus security?
▼
The University of Chicago, UIC, Northwestern, DePaul, Loyola, Columbia College, and the City Colleges are among the largest, hiring both in-house and through contractors.
Do I need a PERC card for campus security?
▼
Yes. Campus security roles in Illinois require a PERC card and the 20-hour training. CPR and first aid certification is a common plus.
Is campus security in-house or contract?
▼
Both. Many universities run in-house public-safety departments, which often add benefits, while others use security contractors. You will find openings on both sides.
