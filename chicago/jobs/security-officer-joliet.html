Overview

Security Officer Jobs in Joliet: The Logistics Corridor

Joliet and Will County sit at the center of the Chicago logistics corridor. The CenterPoint Intermodal Center in Elwood and Joliet is one of the largest inland ports in North America, so warehouse, distribution, and industrial security posts dominate the local market, and many of them run overnight.

Beyond the corridor, Joliet has hospitals, casinos, and retail that hire security too. For officers who want steady work and are open to nights, this is one of the strongest suburban markets in the metro.

Role Overview

What Security Officers Do in Joliet

Most Joliet posts are gatehouse, distribution, and patrol work at logistics sites: controlling the truck gate, checking trailers, verifying drivers, and patrolling yards and docks. Retail, healthcare, and industrial posts round out the local options.

The corridor runs around the clock, so overnight and weekend availability opens the most work here.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Gatehouse and truck gate access control
  • Trailer, seal, and driver verification
  • Yard, dock, and perimeter patrol
  • CCTV and alarm monitoring
  • Access control at retail, healthcare, and industrial sites
  • Incident and activity reporting
Compensation

What This Role Pays in Joliet

Joliet security officers typically earn 18 to 21 dollars an hour. Overnight logistics posts often add a differential, and leads earn more.

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED
  • Overnight availability for corridor posts
  • Reliable transportation, since corridor sites have limited transit
  • Clean background check
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to firms staffing the Elwood and Joliet logistics corridor
  • Note your overnight availability, since many posts are night gatehouse
  • Retail, healthcare, and industrial sites hire locally too
  • Ask about shift differentials when comparing offers
  • Keep your PERC current, since all posts require it

FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officer job seekers in Joliet.

What security jobs are in Joliet?
▼
Mostly logistics: gatehouse, warehouse, distribution, and industrial security across the Elwood and Joliet corridor, plus retail, healthcare, and casino posts in town.
How much do security officers make in Joliet?
▼
Typically 18 to 21 dollars an hour. Overnight logistics posts often add a differential, and leads earn more.
Is Joliet security work mostly warehouse and overnight?
▼
Largely, yes. The CenterPoint corridor is one of the biggest inland ports in North America and runs 24/7, so gatehouse and overnight posts are the most common.
Do I need a PERC card to work security in Joliet?
▼
Yes. All Illinois security roles require a PERC card and the 20-hour training, Joliet included.
Is reliable transportation important for Joliet posts?
▼
Yes. Many corridor sites sit outside walkable transit, and overnight shifts run when transit is limited, so employers list reliable transportation as a core requirement.
