Overview

Security Dispatcher Jobs in Chicago: The Console Side of Security

Dispatch and control room work is the indoor, technology-focused side of security. Corporate security operations centers, campuses, hospitals, and large facilities run consoles that watch cameras, alarms, and access control and dispatch field officers where they are needed.

It suits people who prefer a desk-based, systems-heavy role over field patrol, and it often pays a step above comparable field posts. Clear communication and steady multitasking are the core skills.

Role Overview

What Security Dispatchers Do in Chicago

Dispatchers monitor multi-camera CCTV walls and alarm panels, dispatch officers to incidents, operate access control and visitor systems, handle radio and phone traffic, and log everything in detail. In a security operations center, they are the coordination hub for the whole site.

The role is a natural move for field officers who want indoor work and a technical focus, and it is a common path toward supervisor and SOC lead positions.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Monitoring multi-camera CCTV walls and alarm panels
  • Dispatching field officers to incidents
  • Operating access control and visitor management systems
  • Radio and phone communication with field teams
  • Detailed incident logging and shift reporting
  • Coordinating emergency response with first responders

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Illinois PERC card plus 20-hour training
  • Comfort with monitoring systems and computers
  • Clear communication and steady multitasking
  • Minimum age 18; high school diploma or GED
  • Clean background check
  • Prior dispatch or security experience is a plus
Getting Started

How To Get Started

  • Apply to corporate security operations centers, campuses, and hospitals
  • Highlight any dispatch, radio, or monitoring experience
  • Console roles are a common move from field patrol
  • SOC lead and supervisor roles offer a clear path up
  • Keep your PERC current, since dispatch roles require it

FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officer job seekers in Chicago.

What does a security dispatcher do?
▼
Dispatchers monitor CCTV and alarm systems, dispatch field officers to incidents, operate access control, handle radio and phone traffic, and log incidents. They are the coordination hub for a site.
How much do security dispatchers make in Chicago?
▼
Typically 19 to 25 dollars an hour, a step above comparable field posts. SOC leads and supervisors earn more.
Is dispatch an indoor, desk-based job?
▼
Yes. Control room and SOC work is indoor and systems-focused, which suits officers who prefer a console over field patrol.
Where are security dispatcher jobs in Chicago?
▼
Corporate security operations centers, campuses, hospitals, and large facilities that run their own consoles. Downtown corporate SOCs are a common employer.
Do I need a PERC card to be a security dispatcher?
▼
Yes. Dispatch and control room roles in Illinois generally require a PERC card and the 20-hour training, the same as other security work.
