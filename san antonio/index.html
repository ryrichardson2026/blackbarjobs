FAQ

Security Job FAQs

Common questions from security officers looking for work in San Antonio.

Who is hiring security officers in San Antonio right now?
▼
GardaWorld holds walk-in hiring events every Tuesday 9:30am–12pm at 84 NE Loop 410, Suite 345 (Century Building) — bring your I-9 docs and direct deposit info for on-the-spot offers starting at $15–$18/hr with DailyPay. Sunstates Security is actively hiring full-time unarmed officers at $17–$19/hr with weekly pay and full benefits. Allied Universal has over 97 active San Antonio postings on Indeed covering unarmed, armed, patrol, and event security across all shifts. SMR Security Services posts at $18–$20/hr.
Do I need a license to get hired in San Antonio?
▼
Not always before your first day. GardaWorld and Allied Universal can support licensing after hire — some roles allow 30–60 days to complete your Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license. However, Sunstates' active SA postings specifically require a Level 2 in hand. Having your license active in the Texas DPS TOPS system before applying to Sunstates or hospital accounts is the strongest position. The Level 2 course takes 6 hours and typically costs $50–$100 total.
Does military experience help with SA security jobs?
▼
Significantly. San Antonio has the deepest veteran candidate pool in Texas — JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, and JBSA-Randolph continuously transition service members into the civilian workforce. Kerberos International explicitly recruits prior military and law enforcement. Allied Universal's SkillBridge program provides up to 180 days of on-the-job security training for transitioning service members in their final months of active duty. San Antonio State Hospital's Security Officer II posting lists military MOS codes directly in the job description. Veterans with Level 3 licenses are the most competitive candidates in the SA armed security market.
What does security pay in San Antonio?
▼
Unarmed security in San Antonio runs $15–$20/hr across active postings — GardaWorld at $15–$18/hr, Sunstates at $17–$19/hr, SMR at $18–$20/hr, Allied Universal healthcare accounts at $17.65/hr. Armed security ranges from $17/hr at the entry end to $25–$30/hr for experienced Level 3 officers at specific south SA accounts; Sunstates armed SA hiring event was $20–$21.50/hr. Hospital security at Methodist averages $16.67/hr; San Antonio State Hospital at $18.46–$26/hr with state benefits. Overnight and 2nd/3rd shift differentials add $1–$2/hr above base across most accounts.
Is bilingual Spanish required for SA security jobs?
▼
Not formally required at most employers, but strongly preferred across nearly all San Antonio security accounts. San Antonio is a majority-Hispanic city — the vast majority of the population speaks Spanish, and security officers who can communicate in Spanish are measurably more effective in community patrol, hospital, residential, and River Walk hospitality environments. At hospital accounts, bilingual officers are actively preferred. At residential HOA accounts in predominantly Spanish-speaking neighborhoods, bilingualism is practically essential. It's one of the highest-leverage differentiators a San Antonio security officer can have.

▼
GardaWorld's Tuesday walk-in event at 84 NE Loop 410, Suite 345 is the fastest pathway — show up with I-9 documents and direct deposit info, interview on the spot, and potentially start within days at $15–$18/hr with DailyPay. For candidates with a Level 2 license already in hand, Sunstates and Allied Universal both have active SA postings that can move quickly through digital interview processes. What events need security in San Antonio?
▼
The major event security venues: Alamodome (Allied Universal holds the event day security contract for 100+ annual events including UTSA football, Valero Alamo Bowl, NCAA tournaments, and major concerts — up to 65,000 capacity). AT&T Center — San Antonio Spurs NBA home games (41 per season, October through April) plus concerts. Fiesta San Antonio — 11 days in April, 100-plus events citywide, one of the largest annual festivals in the US. Henry B. González Convention Center year-round. Get on the Allied Universal Event Services roster in San Antonio and you'll have access to the full calendar.
