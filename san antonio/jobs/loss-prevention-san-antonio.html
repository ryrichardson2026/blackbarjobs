What makes San Antonio's LP market distinctive?+
H-E-B is headquartered in San Antonio — the company employs thousands of San Antonians across its stores, distribution centers, and corporate campus, and its Loss Prevention team supporting the SA-area store network is one of the most active retail LP operations in the city. H-E-B LP Specialists are direct H-E-B employees, not contracted through a security firm, which means they receive H-E-B's full institutional benefits package. For candidates who want LP employment at a company that is genuinely embedded in San Antonio's civic identity rather than a national contracted security firm, H-E-B LP is the distinctive local option. The company's expansion into the Dallas-Fort Worth market has also created LP career paths across the state for officers who build their track record in San Antonio.
Which retail LP employers are active in San Antonio?+
H-E-B stores across the SA metro with direct LP employment. Costco has multiple San Antonio locations with LP and asset protection roles carrying Costco's industry-leading benefits package. Target operates numerous SA locations with Asset Protection Specialist positions. North Star Mall (Loop 410 and McCullough) and The Shops at La Cantera (Loop 1604 and La Cantera Pkwy) are the two primary upscale retail concentrations with contracted security and LP through Allied Universal and contracted firms. Walmart and Sam's Club at multiple SA locations generate LP demand. Allied Universal and GardaWorld both serve SA retail accounts. The Medical Center corridor on Floyd Curl Drive adds pharmacy and medical supply LP adjacent to the hospital security segment.
What does LP pay in San Antonio?+
Contracted retail LP through Allied Universal and GardaWorld in San Antonio runs $15–$18/hr, consistent with their SA market rates. H-E-B LP Specialist direct employment offers competitive pay plus H-E-B's institutional benefits — the company does not publish LP-specific pay rates but its SA employment is competitive with national LP standards for the role. Costco LP nationally runs $19–$24/hr with Costco's comprehensive benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401k, PTO), though SA-specific rates vary by role level. Target Asset Protection Specialists nationally average $16–$19/hr. La Cantera and North Star Mall LP for luxury retail contracted accounts runs slightly above standard LP rates reflecting the higher-value merchandise environment.
What is organized retail crime like in San Antonio?+
San Antonio's retail LP market has organized retail crime activity concentrated at the major retail corridors — the Loop 410 commercial belt, the La Cantera / 1604 intersection, and the Westside retail corridor. North Star Mall and La Cantera's luxury tenant mix makes them ORC targets. H-E-B's scale across San Antonio — dozens of stores in a city where H-E-B is the dominant grocery retailer — creates a network LP environment where cross-store pattern identification and coordination is part of the job. Officers who build ORC detection and case documentation skills in SA's retail LP market develop credentials that travel well across Texas retail LP.
Are there LP-adjacent roles in the SA medical corridor?+
Yes — the South Texas Medical Center on Floyd Curl Drive and the surrounding medical district have retail pharmacy, medical supply, and outpatient clinic security that overlaps with LP functions. Protecting controlled substances in hospital pharmacies and medical equipment from internal theft is a specialized LP-adjacent role that pays above standard retail LP. These roles are typically contracted through Allied Universal or hired directly by the health system and require IAHSS awareness in addition to standard LP competencies.

San Antonio Loss Prevention: H-E-B's Home Market

San Antonio is H-E-B's home. The company — founded in Kerrville in 1905 and headquartered in San Antonio for decades — is not just the dominant grocery retailer in the city but a civic institution with a relationship to San Antonians that no national retailer has replicated. H-E-B's LP team supporting its SA-area stores is a direct-employment operation with H-E-B's full benefits, institutional backing, and the career path that comes from working at a company committed to Texas for over a century. For LP candidates who want to build a career rather than fill a contracted shift, H-E-B San Antonio is the most locally distinctive option in the market.

The upscale retail corridor adds the organized retail crime dimension. North Star Mall and La Cantera attract ORC teams that target national luxury retailers — the LP skill set required at these accounts (behavioral observation, covert surveillance, multi-person coordination, case documentation) is substantially more sophisticated than standard grocery LP. Officers who develop ORC competencies at SA's mall accounts build credentials that transfer to LP leadership roles across the Texas retail market.

Role Overview

What Loss Prevention Officers Do in San Antonio

H-E-B LP is grocery-specific — floor observation in high-transaction environments, CCTV console monitoring across multiple departments, merchandise protection in the prepared foods, pharmacy, and general merchandise sections most targeted by organized theft, and active ORC surveillance. The customer service dimension is significant in H-E-B's culture — officers interact with the community constantly and represent a brand that San Antonians feel genuine loyalty toward. LP officers at H-E-B who do the job right are protecting a store their neighbors depend on.

Mall LP at North Star and La Cantera is observation-first, action-last. Plain-clothes coverage, multi-camera CCTV coordination, and floor communication between LP team members characterizes the environment. The customer population is higher-income and the average transaction value is higher — individual shoplifting is less frequent than at grocery LP, but ORC incidents are more complex and the case documentation required is more thorough.
