How does San Antonio's military population affect unarmed security hiring?+
San Antonio's veteran pipeline is the most active of any Texas city. Joint Base San Antonio — comprising JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, and JBSA-Randolph — employs 82,000+ people directly and generates a constant flow of transitioning service members into the civilian workforce. Kerberos International explicitly states they 'encourage prior military and law enforcement personnel to apply' for their Level II positions. Allied Universal runs a formal SkillBridge program partnering with the DoD to give transitioning service members up to 180 days of on-the-job training during their final months of active duty. Bilingual Spanish capability is a meaningful differentiator in San Antonio's majority-Hispanic market — officers who are bilingual are demonstrably more placeable across all account types.

Is a Level 2 license required before applying?+
Not always before applying. GardaWorld's weekly walk-in event hires on the spot and can support licensing. Allied Universal explicitly states licensing requirements are 'subject to state and/or local laws and may be required prior to employment' — meaning some roles allow you to start and complete licensing within a window. Kerberos International notes they train hires free of charge to obtain their license. However, Sunstates' active San Antonio postings specifically note 'Level II Texas Security License' as a requirement — having your license in hand before applying to Sunstates positions is the stronger approach. Processing through the Texas DPS TOPS system typically adds 2–3 weeks to your start date without a license already active.

San Antonio's economy runs on four pillars — military, healthcare, tourism, and education — and each generates distinct unarmed security demand year-round. What Unarmed Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Corporate campus access control is the anchor work type in the USAA, Valero, and 1604 corridor — visitor badging, lobby presence, contractor credentialing, and the professional demeanor that Fortune 500 campus environments require. Hospital patrol at Methodist, University Health, and Baptist campuses is the other dominant environment — round-the-clock coverage, de-escalation in ER settings, and IAHSS-aligned procedures that healthcare institutions expect from their security teams.

The River Walk adds San Antonio's hospitality dimension — hotel lobby security, parking structure patrol, and the mixed family-and-nightlife environment that the 15-mile linear park creates. Officers who build experience across corporate, hospital, and hospitality accounts in San Antonio develop the breadth that supervisory and multi-site roles reward.
