What are the major event security venues in San Antonio?+
The Alamodome is San Antonio's primary large-venue event security employer — a City of San Antonio facility at 100 Montana Street hosting UTSA football, Valero Alamo Bowl, San Antonio Sports events, NCAA tournaments, and major concerts. Its bowl seating holds around 65,000 for football and concert configurations. Allied Universal holds the event day security contract for entrance screening and crowd control per alamodome.com's own employment page. The City of San Antonio directly posts Security Guard I (Alamodome) for full-time patrol positions — the most recent posting ran June 8–22, 2026. The AT&T Center (18,500 capacity) is home to the San Antonio Spurs (NBA) and San Antonio FC (USL). The Henry B. González Convention Center, one of the largest convention facilities in Texas, generates year-round conference and convention security demand downtown.
How does Fiesta San Antonio affect event security employment?+
Fiesta San Antonio runs 11 days in April across 100-plus events citywide. It is consistently cited among the largest annual festivals in the United States and generates one of the highest event security employment surges in any Texas city during the run. Officers who want year-round event security work in San Antonio should target Fiesta in April, the full Spurs home schedule from October through April (41 home games at AT&T Center), UTSA football September through November, the Valero Alamo Bowl in late December, and the Alamodome's convention and concert calendar to build a schedule that stays active through all four quarters.

What does event security pay in San Antonio?+
Allied Universal event security positions are posted as flexible part-time with pay rates varying by site and role — the SA contracted market for event floor and entry positions typically runs $15–$18/hr. Pritchard Sports and Entertainment Group posts event supervisors specifically at $17/hr at the Alamodome. City of San Antonio Security Guard I (Alamodome) direct employment is classified as non-exempt hourly with city benefits — the city's pay schedule for this classification runs above the contracted rate. For officers who build an event security track record in San Antonio — Alamodome, AT&T Center, Fiesta, and convention center accounts — the combination of flexible hours and high event volume makes it possible to work close to full-time hours through event scheduling alone.
What is the River Walk's security employment profile?+
The San Antonio River Walk is 15 miles of linear park through downtown — a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy designation and one of the most visited tourist attractions in the United States. The River Walk's concentration of hotels (Grand Hyatt, JW Marriott, Marriott Rivercenter, Hilton Palacio del Rio), restaurants, bars, and retail generates year-round security demand. The entertainment security environment on the River Walk is most active Thursday through Saturday evenings with peaks during Fiesta, holiday seasons, and major convention weekends when the downtown hotel occupancy drives significant late-night foot traffic. Hotel security accounts on the River Walk are primarily contracted through Allied Universal or direct hotel security employment.

Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in San Antonio

Alamodome assignments are the anchor. Allied Universal officers staff entrance screening gates — bag checks, magnetometer operation, ticket verification, and concourse access control for events up to 65,000 capacity. UTSA football games running September through November are the highest-volume recurring assignments. The Valero Alamo Bowl in December and NCAA tournament events add major one-off assignments throughout the year. City of San Antonio Security Guard I officers handle ongoing facility patrol between events — a distinctly different role focused on the building's daily security rather than event-day crowd management.

The River Walk is the contrast environment — smaller scale, more interpersonal, and more incident-dynamic than stadium security. Hotel and venue security on the River Walk manages guest access, coordinates with SAPD in the river corridor, and handles the mixed family daytime / nightlife evening profile that the 15-mile park creates. Officers who build both large-venue and hospitality event experience in San Antonio cover the broadest profile in the SA event security market.
