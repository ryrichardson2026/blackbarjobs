Overview

Houston Armed Security: Energy Sector, Port, Medical Center, and Critical Infrastructure

Houston's armed security market is defined by the energy sector, the Port of Houston, and the Texas Medical Center - three of the largest critical infrastructure and institutional environments in the US. Armed officers at energy company campuses, petrochemical facilities along the Ship Channel, and hospital systems at TMC command rates of $21-28/hr for Level 3 professionals with relevant backgrounds.

Allied Universal, GardaWorld, and Sunstates collectively cover the majority of Houston's contracted armed accounts. Sunstates posts armed positions at $23/hr+ with weekly pay and full benefits. The petrochemical and energy corridor along the Ship Channel creates specialized armed security demand that doesn't exist at the same scale in any other Texas market.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Armed Security Officers Do in Houston

Energy campus armed security in Houston means professional armed presence at Fortune 500 oil and gas headquarters - Shell's campus in the Energy Corridor, Chevron's Houston facilities, and the dozens of energy company campuses across the west Houston/Energy Corridor are the premium armed security accounts in the Texas market, paying $22-26/hr for Level 3 officers with professional corporate demeanor.

Port of Houston and Ship Channel armed security is a specialized environment - access control at petrochemical plants, terminal security, and perimeter patrol at industrial facilities handling hazardous materials. Officers at these accounts need armed discipline, industrial environment awareness, and background investigation compatibility with port security requirements.

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer license - minimum age 21, 45-hour course, MMPI, DPS range qualification
  • Own firearm and duty gear - required at most Houston armed positions
  • Clean background compatible with energy sector and port security requirements
  • Level 4 PPO for executive protection accounts
  • Prior military or LE experience preferred for Ship Channel and critical infrastructure roles
Schedules

Typical Schedules

  • All shifts including overnight - overnight armed is highest demand and best-paying in Houston
  • GardaWorld: walk-in hiring for armed positions, all shifts, DailyPay
  • Sunstates: all shifts all days, armed at $23/hr+ weekly pay
  • Ship Channel/Port: 24/7 industrial operations, overnight emphasis
  • Energy corridor corporate: M-F primary with 24/7 coverage rotations
