What types of security jobs are available in Pearland?+

Industrial gate and facility security — Pearland sits two miles south of Beltway 8 adjacent to the nation's largest petrochemical hub, and chemical service companies like Third Coast require gate operators and facility security. Healthcare security — Houston Methodist Pearland and affiliated clinics generate consistent security demand. Allied Universal, Marksman Security, and DSI Security are all active hirers in the Pearland market.

Overview

Pearland Security Market: Fast-Growing Suburb, Consistent Demand

Pearland is the third-largest city in the Houston metro and one of the fastest-growing communities in Brazoria County, driven by master-planned residential development, proximity to the Texas Medical Center, and a reputation as one of Texas's top-10 safest cities. That growth pattern creates a security employment market dominated by two distinct segments: residential HOA and gated community security serving the large planned neighborhoods, and industrial/chemical facility security serving the Beltway 8 and Ship Channel–adjacent facilities that have located in south Houston.

The residential segment is the larger employer. Shadow Creek Ranch, Silverlake, Westwood, and other Pearland communities use staffed gatehouse and patrol security. These posts are stable, full-time-friendly, and draw officers from Pearland itself as well as south Houston, Webster, League City, and Friendswood. IGS (International Guard Services) actively recruits Level 2 officers for residential HOA gated community posts in the area.

The industrial and chemical segment adds a higher-paying tier. Third Coast — described as a premier chemical service company with facilities two miles south of Beltway 8 and adjacent to ocean shipping facilities — hires Gate Operators for truck check-in, scale operations, and visitor management. This category of industrial gate work in Pearland sits at the intersection of the city's residential and petrochemical geographies and pays above standard residential security rates.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Officers Do in Pearland

Security work in Pearland mirrors the city's character — professional, community-oriented, and split between residential and industrial environments. HOA gatehouse officers at Pearland's planned communities greet residents by name, manage visitor and vendor access, enforce parking and guest policies, and serve as the community's first point of contact for security concerns. The role requires a professional, friendly demeanor as much as enforcement competence — residents in Pearland's planned communities have high expectations for the quality of their security presence.

Industrial gate and facility security in Pearland's chemical and logistics corridor is a different environment — more procedural, TWIC-credential-focused, and higher-paying. Gate operators at chemical service facilities manage truck weigh-ins, verify driver credentials, stage vehicles, and coordinate between departments. It's structured gate work that rewards attention to detail and documentation discipline over interpersonal flexibility.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control access at HOA community gates — verify residents, log visitors, manage deliveries and contractors
  • Staff industrial facility gates — check driver credentials, manage truck weigh-in and staging, coordinate with operations
  • Conduct patrol of residential communities and commercial properties
  • Monitor CCTV and access control systems at assigned post
  • Respond to resident concerns, disturbances, and parking violations
  • Write accurate daily logs and incident reports
  • Coordinate with HPD and Pearland PD as needed for security incidents
  • Provide hospital campus security at Houston Methodist Pearland — visitor management and patrol
