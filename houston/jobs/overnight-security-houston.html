Overview

Overnight Security Jobs in Houston

Overnight security is the most consistently available and entry-accessible security employment category in Houston. The Houston market's enormous industrial footprint — distribution centers along Pasadena I-10, warehouse corridors in Pasadena and Baytown, logistics facilities in the Alliance north Houston zone, and light manufacturing operations across both counties — generates year-round overnight patrol demand that exceeds daytime security volume at most account types.

Overnight positions carry a $1–3/hr shift differential above daytime equivalents across virtually all Houston account types. At full-time hours (40/week), a $2/hr overnight differential adds approximately $4,160/yr to annual compensation on top of the base hourly rate — a meaningful income difference that most candidates underestimate when comparing daytime and overnight offers.

Overnight Role Types in Houston

  • Industrial and warehouse patrol — vehicle patrol of distribution, warehouse, and manufacturing facilities along Pasadena I-10, Pasadena Beltway 8, and the Stemmons corridor; the highest-volume overnight category in Houston
  • Residential gate coverage — apartment community and HOA overnight gate staffing across Houston's dense residential corridors; accessible to entry-level candidates
  • Mobile patrol routes — vehicle patrol covering multiple properties per shift overnight; consistent demand for officers with clean driving records
  • Armed overnight patrol — Level 3 overnight patrol at banking, government, and industrial accounts; highest-paying overnight category
  • Corporate campus overnight — building protection and access control at office towers and corporate campuses during off-hours
  • Construction site watch — overnight protection of active build sites across Houston's ongoing construction corridor

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — Level 3 required for armed overnight positions
  • Valid driver's license and clean MVR — overnight industrial patrol and mobile patrol routes require vehicle operation
  • Overnight availability — typically 10pm–6am or 11pm–7am; consistent availability across weekends is the most valuable combination
  • Physical stamina — outdoor overnight patrol in varying weather conditions; steel-toe boots often required at industrial sites
  • Reliable transportation to the site — overnight transit is limited in Houston; most overnight positions require personal vehicle access
