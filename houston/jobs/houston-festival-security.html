Frequently Asked Questions

What are the major festivals that hire security officers in Houston?+
Freedom Over Texas (Eleanor Tinsley Park, late June–early July, one of Houston's largest annual celebrations — active right now for the 2026 season), the Houston Livestock Show & Rodeo (NRG Park, 20 days in February–March, 2.5 million attendees), outdoor concert series at White Oak Music Hall and Discovery Green, and neighborhood festivals across the Heights, Midtown, and Montrose. The 2026 FIFA World Cup Fan Fest is the largest one-time festival event in recent Houston history.
Who are the main festival security staffing firms in Houston?+
5 STAR Event Services is the dominant festival staffing firm — women-owned, in business since 2000, with over 42,000 events across Houston, Austin, and San Antonio. They cover Freedom Over Texas, Shell Energy Stadium, and a wide range of outdoor events. S.A.F.E. Management covers NRG Park-based festivals including the Livestock Show & Rodeo.
Does 5 STAR provide Level 2 training for free?+
Yes. 5 STAR provides Level 2 Security License training and exam at no cost to new hires — one of the clearest paths to getting your guard card without paying out of pocket. Officers still need to pass the state exam, submit to fingerprinting, and pass a criminal background check, but the training cost is covered.
What is festival security like in Houston's summer heat?+
It's a genuine physical challenge. Houston's summer heat index regularly exceeds 100°F during outdoor events in June and July. Freedom Over Texas runs in late June into early July. Festival security officers work outdoor posts for 6–8 hour shifts. 5 STAR provides water, tents, and rain ponchos, but officers need to be genuinely heat-tolerant for summer Houston festival work.
Is festival security flexible on schedule?+
Yes — one of the most schedule-flexible segments in Houston security. 5 STAR and similar firms work on an event-availability basis: officers indicate which events they can work, get assigned from there. No fixed weekly commitment. Officers who want consistent work can fill their schedule across festivals, concerts, and sporting events; officers who want occasional shifts can pick up individual events.
Overview

Houston's Festival Security Market: Outdoor Events in Every Season

Houston's festival and outdoor event market runs year-round despite what the summer heat might suggest. The calendar starts with the Houston Livestock Show & Rodeo in February — 20 days at NRG Park with 2.5 million attendees, the single largest annual event mobilization in Texas and the biggest festival security deployment in the city outside a Super Bowl year. Spring and fall bring neighborhood festivals across the Heights, Midtown, Montrose, and EaDo, plus outdoor concert series at White Oak Music Hall, Discovery Green, and amphitheater venues. Summer is anchored by Freedom Over Texas at Eleanor Tinsley Park (June 27–July 6, 2026 — active right now), plus outdoor music festivals and the high-volume period for informal outdoor events citywide.

In 2026, the FIFA World Cup Fan Fest adds a category of international outdoor event security that Houston hasn't seen before. Fan Fest venues bring large crowds of international visitors into outdoor settings for watch parties and fan activations — the crowd management profile differs from domestic festivals, with higher density, greater language diversity, and elevated international visibility for any security incident.

5 STAR Event Services is the defining firm in this segment — founded in 2000, women-owned, DPS-licensed, with a portfolio spanning MLS and NWSL soccer, NASCAR, Formula 1, MotoGP, and AHL hockey across Texas. Their Houston calendar stays active across all four seasons, and their availability-based scheduling model lets officers build consistent income from festival and event work without committing to a fixed employer schedule.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does a Festival Security Officer Do in Houston?

Festival security is outdoor, mobile, and multi-dimensional in ways that fixed-venue security isn't. Officers at Freedom Over Texas or a White Oak Music Hall outdoor show may cover entry gate screening in the first hour, transition to crowd management on the festival grounds during peak attendance, and end the shift managing egress and parking lot clearance. The footprint changes throughout the event, and officers who adapt to changing post assignments without losing situational awareness are the ones who get called back.

The outdoor environment is a real operational factor in Houston. Summer festivals mean genuine heat management — officers need to hydrate aggressively, identify heat exhaustion in themselves and in guests, and communicate with medical staff when guests show signs of distress. The Livestock Show & Rodeo runs February–March, which is more manageable, but Freedom Over Texas and summer concerts run in genuine summer heat. Rain, lightning, and severe weather protocols are standard briefing items for any outdoor Houston festival.

TABC compliance at outdoor events is a daily function — almost every paid festival in Houston serves alcohol. Officers verify age at bar areas, monitor for over-service, and manage the interaction between high-density crowds and alcohol service. Experience working TABC in an outdoor setting is a meaningful differentiator for Houston festival security officers.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Screen guests at festival entry gates — ticket/wristband verification, bag checks, prohibited item screening per event security plan
  • Manage crowd flow and capacity at entry points, stage areas, and high-density zones throughout the festival footprint
  • Staff perimeter security around the event boundary — monitor for unauthorized entry and manage egress during peak and post-event periods
  • Verify age at alcohol service areas — enforce TABC compliance including last-call and service cutoff
  • Monitor crowd for heat exhaustion, medical emergencies, and over-intoxication — coordinate with medical staff and EMS
  • Respond to disturbances, altercations, and unauthorized area access — de-escalate and document per event protocol
  • Manage parking lot security during arrival and departure — highest incident concentration is at close
  • Conduct post-event sweep of the festival footprint — verify venue is clear before release to facility staff

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license — required; 5 STAR Event Services provides training and exam for free for new hires
  • Heat tolerance — summer Houston festivals run in 90–100°F+ heat index; outdoor post work in these conditions is a genuine physical requirement
  • Physical stamina — festival security involves extended standing and walking over large outdoor footprints for 6–8 hour shifts
  • TABC familiarity — outdoor festivals with alcohol service require age verification, over-service monitoring, and last-call management
  • Crowd management comfort — festivals concentrate high-density crowds outdoors; officers need situational awareness and composure at scale
  • Flexible availability — event scheduling is calendar-driven; consistent event commitment leads to more bookings
