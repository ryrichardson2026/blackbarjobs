FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of security jobs are in Pasadena TX?+
Pasadena's security market is dominated by industrial and petrochemical facility security — the city sits at the heart of the Houston Ship Channel corridor, surrounded by refineries, chemical plants, and manufacturing facilities that require 24/7 gate and perimeter staffing. Many Pasadena industrial accounts require a TWIC card for access. Beyond industrial, San Jacinto College (multi-campus community college) has campus security demand, and commercial retail and residential accounts in the Pasadena proper area draw from Allied Universal, Securitas, and Denali Universal Services, all active in the Humble/Pasadena market.
Does Pasadena industrial security pay more?+
Yes — industrial and Ship Channel–adjacent facility security in Pasadena runs $17–23/hr for TWIC-required posts, above standard commercial rates. Gate operator roles at chemical and refinery facilities require stricter screening and site-specific training, which commands a pay premium. Standard commercial and residential security in Pasadena runs $15–18/hr. TWIC-required assignments add $1–3/hr above base.
Do I need a TWIC card for Pasadena security jobs?+
Not for all of them, but TWIC opens the highest-paying accounts. Many of the Ship Channel–adjacent industrial facilities require a Transportation Worker Identification Credential for access to secured areas. Apply through TSA — allow 8–10 weeks. Once you have it, you're eligible for Pasadena's top-tier plant gate and port security assignments that most officers can't access without it.
Which employers hire security in Pasadena?+
Allied Universal (active Pasadena area postings), Denali Universal Services (listed as top Humble/Pasadena hirer on Glassdoor), Securitas, industrial gate operators at Ship Channel facilities, and contracted security firms serving the chemical plant corridor along the channel's south bank. San Jacinto College hires campus security officers directly for its Central, South, and North campuses.
Is Pasadena close enough to Houston for a commute?+
Pasadena is 15–20 minutes southeast of downtown Houston via SH-225 and about 20 minutes from the Texas Medical Center via Loop 610. For officers in southeast Houston, Deer Park, La Porte, Baytown, and League City, Pasadena accounts are often the closest well-paying industrial security market available without crossing the Ship Channel.
Overview

Pasadena Security Market: Gateway to the Ship Channel

Pasadena sits on the south bank of the Houston Ship Channel, flanked by refineries, chemical plants, and manufacturing facilities that represent some of the most demanding — and best-paying — industrial security work in Texas. The city's position in the petrochemical corridor means that Pasadena security isn't primarily a retail-and-HOA market like Sugar Land or a hospital-anchor market like the Medical Center. It's an industrial security market, and the pay, requirements, and working conditions reflect that.

TWIC-required gate and perimeter posts at Pasadena's industrial facilities are among the higher-paying contracted security positions in the greater Houston area. Officers who invest in the TWIC process — about 8–10 weeks and a federal background check — unlock access to facility security accounts that pay $18–23/hr and maintain steady long-term demand regardless of commodity prices. The plants don't stop needing gate officers when oil prices move.

San Jacinto College adds a stable non-industrial anchor. The college operates multiple campuses in Pasadena and the surrounding area with consistent campus security demand. For officers who prefer an educational environment over plant gate work, San Jacinto provides a professional campus setting with more predictable incident profiles.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Officers Do in Pasadena

Industrial gate security in Pasadena is procedural, documentation-heavy work. Officers verify driver credentials and TWIC cards at facility entrances, log truck arrivals and departures, coordinate with plant operations on access windows, and enforce strict visitor control at facilities where unauthorized access carries serious regulatory consequences. It's less about incident response and more about airtight access control — the kind of environment where a missed entry in the log matters.

Campus security at San Jacinto College operates on a completely different cadence — student-facing, community-oriented, and shift-variable with academic-year demand peaks. Officers patrol campus grounds, manage parking enforcement, respond to student safety concerns, and staff event coverage during graduation and campus events.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control access at industrial facility gates — verify TWIC cards, driver credentials, and contractor badges
  • Log all truck arrivals, departures, and visitor entries per facility-specific post orders
  • Conduct perimeter patrol of Ship Channel industrial accounts on foot or vehicle
  • Staff San Jacinto College campus security — patrol, parking enforcement, student escort
  • Monitor CCTV and alarm systems at assigned post
  • Coordinate with plant operations, dispatch, and HPD/Pasadena PD on incidents
  • Write accurate gate logs and incident reports — industrial documentation standards are strict
  • Conduct commercial and residential patrol for Pasadena-area contracted accounts

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license
  • TWIC card for Ship Channel and industrial facility accounts — apply through TSA, allow 8–10 weeks; opens highest-paying tier
  • Valid Texas driver's license — industrial perimeter patrol and multi-site coverage require a vehicle
  • Clean background — industrial facility accounts run federal-level screening in addition to DPS
  • Reliable transportation — Pasadena industrial facilities have limited transit access
  • Weather tolerance — outdoor gate and perimeter posts in Gulf Coast heat and humidity year-round
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All three shifts — industrial facilities run continuous 24/7 operations; no off-days
  • Overnight gate posts are most consistently available — third shift at ship channel facilities
  • Academic calendar affects campus security scheduling — San Jacinto peak during fall and spring semesters
  • Weekend coverage required for all industrial accounts — facilities don't reduce staffing on weekends
