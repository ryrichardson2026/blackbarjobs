Frequently Asked Questions

What types of security jobs are available in The Woodlands?+
The Woodlands security employment concentrates in four categories. Residential HOA security — The Woodlands is a 28,000-acre master-planned community with multiple controlled-access villages, extensive trail and amenity infrastructure, and HOA-managed security. IGS specifically recruits Level 2 officers for residential HOA gated community posts in The Woodlands. Corporate campus — The Woodlands has major corporate tenants including Chevron's north campus, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, and numerous energy sector firms; ESPN/The Walt Disney Company has a Woodlands facility with a security specialist role. Healthcare — Texas Children's Hospital The Woodlands campus and St. Luke's Health The Woodlands both hire security officers.
What does security pay in The Woodlands?+
Security in The Woodlands averages $17.00/hr with most positions running $13.75–$16.73/hr. Priceless Protection Resources LLC (The Woodlands-based) posts weekend Level 3 Security Officer roles at above-standard rates. Sunstates Security posts full-time and flex unarmed officer roles at $16/hr in the Kingwood/north Houston area adjacent to The Woodlands. Texas Children's The Woodlands campus follows the hospital system's pay structure. The Woodlands Resort posts overnight security officer roles for the resort property. Six Flags Over Texas (adjacent market via I-45) also notes that security officers receive free park entry as a perk.
Is The Woodlands corporate campus security work available?+
Yes — The Woodlands is a significant corporate campus market. Chevron's north campus, Hewlett Packard Enterprise's operations, and numerous energy sector firms have maintained substantial presences in The Woodlands Town Center and surrounding office parks. The ESPN/Disney facility in The Woodlands hires Security Specialists who manage the full range of corporate campus security functions. These corporate accounts offer professional indoor security work at competitive rates, often with the added stability of long-term corporate contracts.
How does The Woodlands compare to Sugar Land or Katy for security work?+
The Woodlands sits north of Houston along I-45, making it accessible from Spring, Conroe, Kingwood, and north Harris County rather than the west and southwest corridors where Katy and Sugar Land draw their officer pools. The Woodlands has a stronger corporate campus segment than Pearland or Sugar Land, and its HOA community security demand is among the largest of Houston's suburban markets given the community's 28,000-acre scale. For officers in Spring, Tomball, or north Houston, The Woodlands is the natural suburban security market — closer and less competitive than central Houston accounts.
Are there hospital security jobs in The Woodlands?+
Yes — two significant campuses. Texas Children's Hospital The Woodlands is a full-service pediatric campus serving north Houston and Montgomery County; it staffs security officers per the hospital system's requirements including IAHSS certification. St. Luke's Health The Woodlands campus employs security officers with Level 2 or Level 3 licensing depending on the post. Both campuses hire directly as system employees with institutional benefits. Hospital security in The Woodlands offers the same stability and benefit quality as the TMC campuses without the central-Houston commute for north Harris County and Montgomery County residents.
Overview

The Woodlands Security Market: Planned Community Scale Meets Corporate Campus

The Woodlands is one of the largest and most successful master-planned communities in the United States — 28,000 acres, developed since 1974, with over 120,000 residents across multiple villages, a 1,700-acre town center, and a corporate campus footprint that includes Chevron and Hewlett Packard Enterprise as anchor tenants. The scale of The Woodlands HOA community creates security employment that isn't really comparable to smaller suburban markets — it's closer in scope to managing security for a small city than a typical HOA subdivision.

IGS (International Guard Services) actively recruits Level 2 officers specifically for The Woodlands HOA gated community posts. Green City Security — which emphasizes eco-friendly operations through remote camera monitoring and hybrid vehicles — has Woodlands/Conroe area accounts. Allied Universal, Securitas, and Marksman Security all maintain presences in the north Houston corridor. The combination of HOA community demand, corporate campus demand, and two hospital campuses makes The Woodlands one of the more diverse suburban security markets in greater Houston.

The corporate campus segment is what distinguishes The Woodlands from purely residential suburban markets like Pearland. Chevron's north campus, HP Enterprise's operations, and the ESPN/Disney facility all represent institutional corporate security accounts that run at a professional standard comparable to the Energy Corridor. Officers who build experience at Woodlands corporate accounts develop credentials that transfer directly to Energy Corridor or downtown Houston corporate security roles.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Officers Do in The Woodlands

The Woodlands HOA security operates at a community management level that reflects the development's planning sophistication. Gate officers at The Woodlands village entries are the first point of contact for residents, their guests, contractors, and the delivery ecosystem that serves a 120,000-person community. The role is relationship-oriented — long-tenured officers who know the community become its de facto community liaison as much as its security presence. The Woodlands Township takes the quality of its security presence seriously.

Corporate campus security in The Woodlands Town Center and surrounding office parks mirrors Energy Corridor professional standards. Visitor management, contractor credentialing, badge access control, and camera console monitoring are the primary functions. The ESPN/Disney Woodlands facility adds a media company corporate security dimension — the security specialist role specifically manages the full range of corporate campus protection for a recognizable branded employer.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control access at The Woodlands village HOA gates — verify residents, manage visitor and contractor access across the community
  • Staff corporate campus security at Chevron, HPE, ESPN, and other Town Center tenants
  • Provide hospital security at Texas Children's and St. Luke's The Woodlands campuses
  • Conduct patrol of The Woodlands community grounds, trails, amenity centers, and parking areas
  • Monitor CCTV and access control systems at commercial and campus accounts
  • Staff event security for The Woodlands Pavilion concerts and Town Center events
  • Enforce community policies and parking regulations across HOA-managed areas
  • Write accurate daily activity logs and incident reports

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license
  • Valid Texas driver's license — community patrol across The Woodlands' 28,000 acres requires a vehicle
  • Professional demeanor and community orientation — Woodlands HOA accounts expect high-quality resident interaction
  • IAHSS certification preferred for hospital campus roles at Texas Children's and St. Luke's
  • Reliable transportation — The Woodlands is 30 miles north of central Houston; personal vehicle essential
  • Level 3 for armed weekend Woodlands accounts — Priceless Protection Resources and some corporate campus accounts require commissioned status

  • Weekend HOA peaks — The Woodlands community is most active on weekends; gatehouse and patrol demand concentrated
  • Overnight Woodlands Resort posts — hotel property security covers all overnight hours
