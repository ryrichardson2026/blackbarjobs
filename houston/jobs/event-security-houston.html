Overview

Houston Event Security: NFL, NBA, MLB, MLS, and the Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo

Houston hosts Texans (NFL) at NRG Stadium, Rockets (NBA) at Toyota Center, Astros (MLB) at Minute Maid Park, and Dynamo (MLS) at Shell Energy Stadium - four major professional sports franchises generating year-round event security employment across the metro. The Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo at NRG Park is one of the largest annual events in the US, running three weeks in March with daily security staffing needs.

BEST Crowd Management holds contracts at multiple major Houston venues. Andy Frain Services covers additional stadium and arena accounts. Getting on rosters at two or three event security firms gives officers access to the full Houston event calendar year-round at $16-22/hr depending on the venue and event type.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in Houston

Entry screening and magnetometer operation at NRG Stadium for Texans games involves managing entry flows for 70,000+ attendees - the largest single event security deployment in Houston. Officers conduct bag checks, operate walk-through magnetometers at multiple entry gates, and manage crowd flow through the pre-event window at one of the largest NFL stadiums in the country.

Toyota Center event security for Rockets NBA and Rockets-era concerts combines in-venue crowd management with access control across 18,000-seat arena configurations. The Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo - 3 weeks, 75,000+ daily attendees at peak, livestock areas, carnival, and concert programming simultaneously - is the most operationally complex annual event security deployment in the Texas market.

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license for unarmed event positions
  • Physical stamina - event security requires standing 8-10 hours at high-capacity venues
  • Professional crowd management composure - Houston venues expect hospitality-grade interaction
  • Flexible evening and weekend availability - events run primarily Thursday through Sunday
