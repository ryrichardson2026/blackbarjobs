Overview

Hospital Security Jobs in Houston

The Texas Medical Center is the largest medical complex in the world — 60+ institutions, 106,000 employees, and over 10 million patient visits annually. Security operations at TMC are continuous, substantial, and span multiple direct-hire institutions. Memorial Hermann, Houston Methodist, MD Anderson Cancer Center, Texas Children's Hospital, and Harris Health System all maintain dedicated security departments with their own HR pipelines, separate from contract guard firms.

Healthcare security is meaningfully different from standard guard work. Officers routinely manage psychiatric patient situations, de-escalate distressed family members, support clinical staff during emergencies, and patrol complex multi-building campuses. Direct-hire TMC positions include full hospital employee benefits — health insurance, paid time off, and retirement matching — that substantially increase total compensation above the hourly rate.

Healthcare Security Role Types in Houston

  • Emergency department security — highest-demand role; front-line de-escalation at Memorial Hermann Level I Trauma, Ben Taub (Harris Health), and Houston Methodist; ED premium pay
  • TMC campus patrol — foot and vehicle patrol across TMC's 1,345-acre campus spanning dozens of connected buildings and parking structures
  • Behavioral health security — specialized training required; patient management in locked psychiatric units at Menninger Clinic and Harris Health Psychiatric Center
  • Harris Health System (direct hire) — county employment through LBJ Hospital, Ben Taub, and Quentin Mease; government employee benefits; competitive pay
  • MD Anderson Cancer Center — specialized oncology hospital environment; direct-hire through UTMDACC HR; full UT System employee benefits
  • Access control and credentialing — visitor management and badge verification at entrances and restricted areas; high volume at major TMC visitor entrances

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — required for all hospital security positions
  • Emotional steadiness under pressure — healthcare environments involve patient crises, active medical emergencies, and distressed families; composure is the primary screening criterion
  • Thorough background check — TMC institutions run detailed background and reference checks; healthcare standards are stricter than commercial accounts
  • De-escalation aptitude — TMC employers provide internal training; demonstrating baseline composure in the interview process matters significantly
  • Shift flexibility — TMC facilities run 24/7/365; overnight and weekend availability significantly increases your options and pay

CPI certification is a plus and sometimes required for behavioral health positions. Military, corrections, and mental health backgrounds are particularly valued. Full guide: How To Become A Security Guard in Texas.
