FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes downtown Houston a distinct security market?+
Density. No other Houston submarket concentrates this many major venues within walking distance: the George R. Brown Convention Center (1.9 million sq ft), Toyota Center (Rockets), Minute Maid Park (Astros), Discovery Green (outdoor events), the Theater District (17 blocks, 12,948 theater seats — second in the US), and the Hilton Americas (1,200 rooms) and Marriott Marquis (1,000 rooms) — both connected to the GRB via skybridge. Security demand in downtown is genuinely multi-source.
What types of security jobs exist in downtown Houston?+
Convention center security (GRB — year-round event schedule), hotel security (Hilton Americas, Marriott Marquis, Omni, AC Marriott), high-rise commercial lobby security (downtown towers, Earle Cabell Federal Courthouse), event security at Toyota Center and Minute Maid Park, Theater District performing arts security, and outdoor venue security at Discovery Green and Avenida Houston plaza.
How does the FIFA World Cup 2026 affect downtown security?+
Significantly. FIFA Fan Fest venues downtown, the influx of international visitors at downtown hotels, and proximity to Houston Stadium (converted NRG, about 3 miles away) have expanded security demand in the downtown core. International visitor volume is driving bilingual Spanish preference across hotel and venue security postings throughout summer 2026.
Is downtown Houston security full-time or event-based?+
Both. Hotel security and high-rise lobby posts are full-time fixed positions. Convention center and venue security is event-based — volume follows the GRB event calendar and the sports season. Officers who want full-time income combine hotel or commercial lobby positions with event security to maintain consistent hours across the week.
What does downtown Houston security pay?+
High-rise lobby and commercial building security runs $15–19/hr. Hotel security (Hilton Americas, Marriott Marquis) runs $15–20/hr with benefits. Convention center and event security runs $16–21/hr depending on event and firm. Supervisor and lead roles across downtown properties run $20–26/hr.
Overview

Downtown Houston: The Most Concentrated Security Employment Zone in the City

Downtown Houston contains the highest density of major security accounts in the metro. The George R. Brown Convention Center anchors the cluster at 1.9 million square feet connected via skybridge to the Hilton Americas (1,200 rooms) and Marriott Marquis (1,000 rooms) — together forming the largest convention campus in Texas. The GRB hosts 250-plus events annually including 30-plus major conventions, generating security staffing needs that run every day the calendar has an event. A 700,000 sq ft expansion (GRB South, opening 2028) will increase event capacity by 30% and expand the security footprint further.

Toyota Center and Minute Maid Park sit within blocks of the GRB, making downtown the only Houston submarket where an officer can work a convention center day shift and a Rockets game evening shift on the same day without leaving the neighborhood. The Theater District's 17-block footprint — housing the Houston Symphony, Ballet, Grand Opera, and Alley Theatre — generates evening security demand across weekday and weekend performances. Discovery Green's 12-acre park and the Avenida Houston plaza adjacent to the GRB add outdoor event and public space security to the downtown mix.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 has elevated downtown's security profile considerably. International visitors arriving at IAH and staying at downtown hotels, FIFA Fan Fest activations, and the general influx of global visitors have combined to make summer 2026 the highest-volume period downtown Houston has seen since Super Bowl LI in 2017.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does a Downtown Houston Security Officer Do?

Downtown Houston security varies significantly by account type but the common thread is professional guest interaction at scale. High-rise commercial lobby security manages the daily flow of hundreds of employees, contractors, and visitors through access control in a setting where the officer represents building ownership to tenants and their clients. Convention center security manages a constantly rotating population of convention delegates, exhibitors, and vendors who need orientation as much as security management. Hotel security in the Hilton Americas or Marriott Marquis combines overnight patrol, loss prevention, and guest service across a thousand-room property connected to an active convention campus.

The proximity of multiple account types creates professional opportunities that don't exist in other Houston submarkets. Officers who build relationships with the downtown security ecosystem — GRB, the convention hotels, the Toyota Center event team — can maintain consistent income across all three without changing employers, working different accounts on different shifts across the same week.

The 2026 context adds practical dimension: downtown is currently the most internationally exposed security environment in Houston. Officers interacting with FIFA visitors, convention delegates from global organizations, and foreign government representatives at the Hilton Americas are practicing professional guest service at a level that builds resume value well beyond the current year.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage lobby access at high-rise commercial buildings and convention hotels — badge verification, visitor management, contractor escort
  • Staff convention center security at the GRB — exhibitor credentialing, load-in/load-out access, conference room checkpoints, perimeter management
  • Conduct overnight patrol of hotel corridors, parking structures, and exterior areas at convention campus properties
  • Manage event security at Toyota Center and Minute Maid Park on game and event nights — access control, crowd management, section security
  • Monitor public spaces at Discovery Green and Avenida Houston during events and routine public use
  • Respond to disturbances, medical situations, and security incidents — coordinate with HPD Central Division and EMS
  • Monitor CCTV systems and alarm panels at commercial and hotel properties during fixed-post assignments
  • Write accurate shift logs and incident reports — downtown properties maintain detailed records for liability and client reporting

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 or Level 3 Security License — Level 2 for most downtown commercial and hotel posts; Level 3 for armed assignments
  • Professional appearance and communication — downtown properties host executive and international guests; presentation standards are enforced
  • Convention center or hospitality experience preferred — GRB and hotel accounts value familiarity with large-group guest management
  • Bilingual Spanish — significant advantage for FIFA World Cup-period assignments and international convention delegate management in 2026
  • Reliable transit or transportation downtown — MetroRail Red Line serves downtown well; more transit access than most Houston submarkets
  • Flexibility across account types — downtown's multi-account environment rewards officers who can work convention center, hotel, and event posts across the week
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All three shifts available — downtown commercial and hotel security runs 24/7 across day, swing, and overnight
  • Convention schedule creates mid-week demand — GRB events run Monday through Friday as well as weekends; day shifts staffed consistently
  • Event nights add evening demand — Toyota Center Rockets games and Minute Maid Park Astros games create evening staffing surges
  • Theater District performances run Wednesday through Sunday — consistent evening security demand outside of sports events
  • FIFA World Cup summer 2026 adds intensive daytime and evening demand across downtown hotels, Fan Fest venues, and public spaces
  • Hotel security runs fixed full-time schedules — 12-hour shifts at Hilton Americas and Marriott Marquis standard for overnight officers
