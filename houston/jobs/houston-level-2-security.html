Overview

Level 2 Security Officer Jobs in Houston

The Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license is the foundation of every security career in Texas. It's the entry credential for unarmed security work and the prerequisite for the Level 3 Armed license. In Houston, the overwhelming majority of open security positions are Level 2 roles — corporate lobbies, residential gate coverage, healthcare campuses, retail floors, warehouses, event venues, and school campuses all operate on Level 2 staffing.

Getting your Level 2 license in Texas requires completing a 6-hour DPS-approved training course, passing a background check, and submitting your application through the Texas DPS online portal. The total cost runs $130–$220 depending on the training provider. Most candidates complete the process in 2–4 weeks. Houston has dozens of DPS-approved training providers, with courses offered on weekdays and weekends at locations across Harris County.

Where Level 2 Officers Work in Houston

  • Energy Corridor corporate campuses — the highest-volume Level 2 employer corridor in Houston; BP, Shell, ConocoPhillips, and ExxonMobil campus complexes all require large Level 2 security workforces
  • Texas Medical Center — Level 2 security at all TMC institutions; direct-hire positions at Memorial Hermann, Houston Methodist, and Harris Health come with full hospital employee benefits
  • Residential HOA and apartment gate coverage — Level 2 is all that's required for most Houston gate positions; high availability, accessible to new license holders
  • Galleria and major retail — all Houston mall and retail floor security positions are Level 2 minimum; many LP investigator positions begin at Level 2
  • NRG Stadium and event venues — Level 2 is sufficient for all standard event floor and gate positions at Houston's major venues
  • Warehouse and industrial overnight patrol — Level 2 covers the majority of overnight warehouse and distribution center positions across Beltway 8 and I-10 east

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • 6-hour DPS-approved training course — available from dozens of Houston-area providers on weekdays and weekends; cost runs $50–$120 for the course itself
  • Background check clearance — DPS reviews criminal history as part of the license application; felonies are disqualifying; the full disqualification list is published on the DPS website
  • DPS license application — submitted online through the Texas DPS portal; processing typically takes 1–2 weeks after background clearance
  • 18 years or older — minimum age for Texas security licensure
  • Reliable transportation — most Houston security accounts are not transit-accessible; a vehicle is the practical requirement for most positions

The Level 2 license is the starting point — not the ceiling. Most experienced Houston security officers upgrade to Level 3 Armed within 1–2 years for the pay premium. Full guide: How To Become A Security Guard in Texas.
