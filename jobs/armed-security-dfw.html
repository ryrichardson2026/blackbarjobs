Overview

Armed Security Jobs in DFW

Armed (Level 3) security is the highest-paying and most in-demand security specialty across Dallas–Fort Worth. The DFW market concentrates a significant density of accounts requiring Level 3 commissioned officers — downtown banking corridors, hyperscale data centers in Richardson and Allen, government buildings in the Earle Cabell Federal Complex, major corporate campuses, and a growing cannabis retail security market across both counties.

The Level 3 commission is the single highest-return credential move in Texas security. At specialized accounts — banks, data centers, government facilities — armed officers earn $5–15/hr above comparable unarmed positions. The DFW market's concentration of these premium account types makes it one of the strongest armed security markets in Texas.

Armed Security Role Types in DFW

  • Bank and financial security — branch and vault security at major banking corridors across the CBD, Uptown, and suburban commercial zones
  • Data center security — access control and armed patrol at hyperscale and colocation facilities in the Richardson/Allen/Garland corridor
  • Government building security — armed posts at federal and state facilities; FPS-contracted positions in downtown Dallas
  • Armed corporate campus — Level 3 posts at Fortune 500 headquarters requiring armed presence for executive and sensitive area access
  • Cannabis dispensary security — one of the fastest-growing armed security categories in DFW; consistent year-round demand
  • Armed overnight patrol — vehicle patrol covering multiple armed accounts on overnight routes across both counties

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security License — active Level 2 required first; 45-hour course, MMPI exam, firearms qualification
  • Must own or obtain approved duty firearm — most armed accounts specify caliber and holster requirements
  • Clean background — armed accounts conduct deeper checks than standard DPS requirements; federal account positions require additional screening
  • Firearms proficiency — regular qualification requirements at most armed accounts; military or law enforcement firearms background is competitive
  • Professional presentation — armed posts at banks and corporate campuses hold officers to the highest standards in the market
  • Reliable transportation — armed patrol routes cover multiple properties per shift
