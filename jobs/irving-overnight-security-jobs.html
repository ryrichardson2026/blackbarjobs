What Does an Overnight Security Officer Do?

Overnight security officers in Irving work across a wide range of environments — from high-rise corporate campuses in Las Colinas to cargo-adjacent warehouses near DFW Airport. Shifts typically run 10pm to 6am, covering the period when most commercial properties face their greatest vulnerability.

The mix of assignment types in Irving is broader than most DFW suburbs. On any given week, overnight openings may include hotel lobby and perimeter posts along the Airport Freeway, access control at Las Colinas office towers, vehicle patrol of distribution yards near I-635, and construction site security across the rapidly developing Urban Center.

Overnight roles in Irving — especially those tied to airport-adjacent operations — often come with competitive shift differentials and consistent scheduling, making them a reliable option for officers seeking steady overnight income.

Do I need a guard card to work overnight security in Texas? +
Yes. Texas requires a valid Security Officer Commission (Level II) through the Department of Public Safety for most paid security work. Armed positions require a Level III Commission. Entry-level candidates can typically complete state-approved training and obtain their guard card in 2–4 weeks.
