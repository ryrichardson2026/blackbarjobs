Overview

AT&T Stadium Security Jobs — What You Need To Know

AT&T Stadium in Arlington is one of the largest domed stadiums in the world — a 100,000+ capacity venue that is home to the Dallas Cowboys and hosts some of the largest events in Texas including Super Bowls, Big 12 Championship games, college football playoffs, major concerts, and international soccer matches. Security at AT&T Stadium is contracted through Allied Universal under a multi-year stadium security agreement.

Cowboys home game security is the primary ongoing deployment — 8 regular season home games plus preseason (typically 2 home games in August) and any home playoff games. Each game day requires a large security deployment across all entry gates, parking perimeters, concourses, field level, and suite corridors. The deployment runs from approximately 4 hours before kickoff through 1 hour post-game.

How To Get On The AT&T Stadium Security Roster

  • Apply to Allied Universal — DFW office — AT&T Stadium is an Allied Universal account. Apply through aus.com and specify "AT&T Stadium" and "Cowboys game day" interest in your application. The DFW office manages stadium staffing directly.
  • Apply in summer — roster spots for the NFL season are filled before the September opener. June and July are the best months to apply for Cowboys season coverage. Late applications may still get added to the roster mid-season for playoff coverage.
  • Get your Level 2 guard card first — a valid Texas Level 2 Security License is required for all paid security positions at AT&T Stadium. No license means no placement. See our full Texas licensing guide.
  • Attend orientation — Allied Universal runs pre-season orientation for new stadium security additions covering post assignments, radio protocols, and emergency procedures specific to AT&T Stadium.
  • Be available for all home games — roster officers are expected to be available for all scheduled home games. Consistent unavailability results in removal from the active roster.

AT&T Stadium Security Posts & Assignments

  • Parking perimeter — outdoor patrol of the stadium parking lots and approaches; first arrival point for fans; physically demanding outdoor post in Texas heat/cold
  • Gate entry screening — managing fan entry, bag check coordination, and ticket verification at stadium gates; high fan interaction volume
  • Concourse patrol — interior stadium concourse coverage during the game; managing crowd flow, incident response, and medical coordination
  • Field level — field perimeter security covering access control to playing surface, sidelines, and team areas; highest-profile post assignment
  • Suite corridor — VIP and suite level access control; professional presentation standard is highest at this post
  • Post-game egress — parking lot and egress management as fans exit; shift extends approximately 1 hour after final whistle
Pay & Schedule

AT&T Stadium Security Pay

Per-diem pay — no commitment between games. Rates vary by post assignment.

  • Standard game day posts (gate, concourse, parking): $17–21/hr
  • Suite and VIP corridor posts: $19–24/hr
  • Cowboys regular season games (8 home games): per-diem per game
  • Playoff games and major events (Super Bowl, concerts): above-standard rates
  • Non-Cowboys events (concerts, soccer, college football): $18–23/hr

AT&T Stadium also hosts approximately 20–30 additional major events per year outside the Cowboys schedule — concerts, international soccer, college football — providing additional per-diem income beyond the NFL calendar for officers on the active roster.

Requirements

What You Need For AT&T Stadium Security

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — required for all positions; no exceptions at major venue accounts
  • Professional presentation — Allied Universal sets uniform and appearance standards for AT&T Stadium; clean shave or neatly groomed facial hair, uniform pressed
  • Physical fitness — gate and parking posts involve extended standing and walking in outdoor Texas conditions; preseason August games are extremely hot
  • Customer service composure — high fan interaction volume including intoxicated fans; composure and de-escalation skills are essential
  • Game day availability — primarily Sunday home games during the NFL season; August preseason adds Saturday games
  • Clean background — Allied Universal and AT&T Stadium conduct thorough background screening for all stadium security placements
