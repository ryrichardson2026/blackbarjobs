Overview

Unarmed Security Jobs in Houston

Unarmed security is the largest security employment category in Houston and the entry point for every security career in Texas. Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officers work corporate lobbies, residential gate coverage, retail floors, healthcare facilities, event venues, school campuses, warehouses, and overnight industrial patrol across both Houston and Tarrant counties.

The Houston unarmed market is active year-round. Guard firms including Allied Universal and Securitas hold hundreds of active accounts — corporate campuses in Midtown and Energy Corridor, apartment communities across The Woodlands and Houston, retail centers from Galleria Houston to Houston Premium Outlets (Cypress), hospitals from Memorial Hermann to Houston Methodist, and industrial corridors from Pasadena I-10 to Pasadena's Beltway 8 zone.

Role Types

  • Corporate lobby security — access control, visitor management, professional presence at office towers and campuses across the CBD, Energy Corridor, and The Woodlands/Sugar Land corridors
  • Residential gate coverage — apartment and HOA gate security across Houston; the most accessible entry point for new candidates
  • Healthcare security — patient and visitor management at hospitals and clinics; Memorial Hermann and Houston Methodist offer direct county employment with government benefits
  • Retail and loss prevention — floor security at shopping centers, big-box, and outlet centers across both counties
  • Overnight industrial patrol — vehicle patrol at warehouses and distribution centers along Pasadena I-10, Pasadena Beltway 8, and the Alliance corridor
  • Event security — flexible gate and floor security at Toyota Center, NRG Stadium, Globe Life Field, and other Houston venues

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — the only non-negotiable. 2–4 weeks, ~$130–$220 total.
  • Clean background check — no felonies; most misdemeanors outside DPS disqualification windows are acceptable.
  • Reliable transportation — Houston is a large metro; most positions require you to reach the site without public transit.
  • Professional presentation — especially for corporate lobby and healthcare accounts.
  • Overnight and weekend availability — the single most effective way to increase your options.

No prior security experience required for most entry-level positions. Customer service, retail, military, and hospitality backgrounds are all considered relevant. Full guide: How To Become A Security Guard in Texas.
