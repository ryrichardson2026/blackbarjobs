How do I get hired fast in DFW security?+
Apply with your Texas Level 2 license active in TOPS to Sunstates, GardaWorld, and Allied Universal simultaneously — all three have continuous DFW hiring and fast application processes. What is the difference between Dallas and Fort Worth security markets?+
Dallas and Fort Worth have distinct security employment profiles despite being part of the same metro. Dallas's market centers on corporate high-rise security in the CBD and Uptown, medical district hospital security along Harry Hines, the northeast corridor tech campus security in Plano/Frisco/Allen, and the NorthPark/Galleria retail corridor. Fort Worth's market is more industrial and institutional — the Alliance Corridor's logistics and manufacturing security, the Medical District along Interstate 820, Dickies Arena and Globe Life Field event security, and the TCU campus and Cultural District accounts. ZipRecruiter shows Dallas averaging $16.97/hr and Fort Worth averaging $15.61/hr for standard contracted positions, reflecting Fort Worth's heavier industrial mix relative to Dallas's higher-paying corporate and tech campus accounts.
What makes DFW the largest security market in Texas?+
Scale and economic diversity. With approximately 7.8 million people across 13 counties, the DFW Metroplex is the third-largest metro area in the United States and the largest inland port in the world. The concentration of Fortune 500 headquarters — AT&T, American Airlines, ExxonMobil, Southwest Airlines, Toyota USA — creates corporate campus security demand at a scale that Texas's other metros don't approach. DFW International Airport's 75 million-plus annual passengers make it one of the busiest airports in the world, with corresponding aviation security employment. The industrial footprint along I-20 South Dallas and the Alliance Corridor in Fort Worth generates overnight industrial security volume that exceeds Houston's comparable corridors. And the continued population growth — DFW added more residents than any metro in the country in recent years — means construction security, new development patrol, and residential HOA security are all growing simultaneously.
What does it take to work armed security in DFW?+
Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer license — requires your Level 2 first, then 45 hours of in-person training from a DPS-licensed Level III school (HB 3424, January 2024 requires in-person firearms and defensive tactics), DPS range qualification at 70% minimum, and MMPI psychological evaluation. Minimum age 21. Lockheed Martin Grand Prairie posts armed security requiring Level III. GardaWorld Federal Services Fort Worth posts armed DoD-adjacent roles. Sunstates has armed supervisor positions in DFW at $26/hr. Armed positions at Parkland and JPS require documented behavioral health de-escalation experience in addition to Level 3. The armed DFW market runs $19–$28/hr for standard accounts, with DoD-adjacent cleared positions paying above market.
Overview

DFW Security Market: Third Largest US Metro, Largest Security Market in Texas

The Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex is the largest security employment market in Texas by volume, variety, and compensation ceiling. With approximately 7.8 million people, 13 counties, and an economy that spans Fortune 500 headquarters, the world's busiest inland airport, Level I trauma centers in both major cities, one of the country's largest industrial distribution networks, and three major professional sports franchises — the DFW security market offers account type diversity that no other Texas market replicates.

The three-firm contracted market (Sunstates, GardaWorld, Allied Universal) anchors the accessible employment tier. Sunstates' DFW operation offers the metro's most comprehensive benefits package for contracted security — weekly pay, 401k match, medical/dental/vision, tuition reimbursement, and premium holiday pay across armed and unarmed DFW accounts. GardaWorld's flex model at $13.50–$23.50/hr with DailyPay and Great Place to Work certification is the highest-flexibility DFW employer. Allied Universal's scale — 800,000 employees globally — means the deepest DFW account portfolio and the broadest Claim a Shift platform for schedule building.

Above the contracted tier, DFW's direct employer security market is anchored by Parkland Health and JPS Health Network — both county government employers with pension access, government benefits, and the highest-acuity hospital security environments in North Texas. AT&T's global headquarters in downtown Dallas represents the Fortune 500 corporate security tier. Lockheed Martin Grand Prairie and the Alliance Corridor's defense and aviation tenants add the defense-adjacent dimension to a market that is already North Texas's largest security employer.
