Frisco Market

Why Frisco Is One of the Fastest-Growing Security Markets in DFW

Frisco has been one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States for over a decade, and its security job market reflects that growth directly. The city's mix of corporate relocations, world-class sports infrastructure, master-planned retail developments, and a booming residential population creates layered and continuous demand for unarmed security staffing across every shift.

The Star in Frisco — the Dallas Cowboys' 91-acre world headquarters and practice facility — anchors a broader mixed-use development along the Dallas North Tollway that includes hotels, restaurants, retail, and event space requiring consistent security coverage. Toyota Stadium, home of FC Dallas and a major concert and event venue, adds sports and live event security demand on top of the corporate campus work. The Fields development and the broader Frisco Station area are bringing additional corporate office tenants and retail traffic northward along the tollway corridor.

Frisco's medical footprint is expanding rapidly — Medical City Frisco, Texas Health Frisco, and a growing cluster of specialty clinics and surgical centers along the Preston Road and Lebanon Road corridors all require ongoing security staffing. And Frisco's massive residential growth — with new apartment communities, master-planned neighborhoods, and mixed-use developments opening continuously — creates steady demand for residential and HOA security. Role Overview

What Does an Unarmed Security Officer Do in Frisco?

Unarmed security officers in Frisco work across one of the most varied assignment landscapes in the DFW metro. On any given week, openings may span lobby access control at a Frisco Station corporate tower, crowd management at a Toyota Stadium FC Dallas match, healthcare security at Medical City Frisco, and overnight patrol at a construction site in one of the city's many active development zones.

Frisco's corporate campus assignments — particularly at The Star and along the tollway corridor — emphasize professional presentation and customer-facing interaction alongside traditional security functions. The city's relatively affluent demographic and high-visibility properties mean employers consistently prioritize communication skills and composure over tactical experience for unarmed roles.

The city's growth also means a higher-than-average volume of construction site and temporary security assignments, making Frisco a strong market for officers who want consistent work across a mix of environments and shift types.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage lobby access, visitor sign-in, and badge issuance at corporate facilities
  • Conduct scheduled interior and exterior patrols throughout the shift
  • Monitor CCTV systems and access control panels
  • Staff entrance gates and conduct bag checks at Toyota Stadium and event venues
  • Respond to disturbances, medical situations, and alarm activations
  • Write accurate shift logs and incident reports for each assignment
  • Enforce parking and trespassing policies on corporate and retail properties
  • Provide visible deterrence across residential communities and HOA-managed properties

Schedules

Do I need a guard card for unarmed security work in Frisco? +
Yes. Texas requires a valid Security Officer Commission (Level II) through the Department of Public Safety for all paid unarmed security positions. The Level II commission covers unarmed officers without a firearm qualification requirement. Most candidates can complete the required training and obtain their commission within 2–4 weeks through a state-approved training provider.
Are there security jobs at The Star in Frisco? +
Yes. The Star — the Dallas Cowboys' 91-acre world headquarters and mixed-use development — requires ongoing security staffing across its campus, hotel, retail, and event space components. These roles tend to be among the more competitive unarmed positions in Frisco due to the high-profile environment and professional standards required. Guard firms contracted to the Cowboys organization and the broader development are the primary hiring path.
