Healthcare Security Officer

Just Posted  ·  Jun 22, 2026
Dallas, TX
Full-Time / On-Site
Healthcare
6a – 6p
About the Role

A major Dallas healthcare facility is hiring Security Officers to maintain a safe environment for patients, families, visitors, and staff. This is a front-line role — you'll be a trained responder for high-risk situations and a core part of the facility's safety operations.

This is not a passive post. Expect a fast-moving environment where your judgment, de-escalation skills, and ability to act under pressure matter every shift.

Responsibilities

What You'll Do

  • Respond to workplace violence, medical emergencies, fire, bomb threats, flooding, hazmat situations, and other critical incidents
  • Conduct weapons screening at facility entry points
  • Provide de-escalation support for patients, families, and staff during crisis situations
  • Patrol assigned areas — internal and external — to identify signs of crime, hazards, or suspicious activity
  • Write detailed incident reports accurate enough for legal proceedings and personnel reviews
Requirements

What They're Looking For

Experience

  • 1+ year in security, healthcare, hospitality, law enforcement, or military (required)
  • 2+ years in security, law enforcement, or military (preferred)

Education

  • High school diploma or GED (required)
  • Associate's degree in Criminal Justice, Law Enforcement, or Security (preferred)
Licenses & Certifications

Preferred Qualifications

  • Valid Texas Driver's License
  • Basic Life Support (BLS) certification
  • Texas DPS Private Security Bureau — Level 2 or Level 3
  • CHSO Basic Level Certification (IAHSS)
Compensation & Benefits

What You Get

  • Competitive pay based on experience
  • Medical, dental, and vision coverage
  • 401(k) with employer match
  • Paid parental and caregiver leave
  • Career growth opportunities within a major DFW health system
