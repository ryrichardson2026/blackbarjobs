Overview

Loss Prevention Jobs in DFW

Loss prevention is one of the most accessible and consistently available security employment categories in Dallas–Fort Worth — and one of the few with a clear internal career track that can reach $80,000+/yr at the director level without ever leaving retail security. The DFW market's massive retail footprint — NorthPark Center, Galleria Dallas, Allen Premium Outlets, North East Mall, and hundreds of major big-box and grocery anchor locations across both counties — generates year-round LP employment demand that peaks dramatically during the November–January holiday season.

Loss prevention comes in two distinct roles. LP investigators work plainclothes — blending in with shoppers to observe, document, and apprehend shoplifters and organized retail crime groups. Uniformed retail security officers work visible deterrence on the retail floor. LP investigator roles pay $2–5/hr above uniformed floor equivalents and require more judgment and surveillance system proficiency.

LP Role Types in DFW

  • LP investigator — plainclothes — observe, document, and apprehend shoplifters; CCTV and EAS monitoring; highest-paying entry-level LP category
  • Assets Protection Specialist (Target) — Target's direct-hire LP program with Target employee benefits; structured career advancement within Target's AP organization
  • Asset Protection Associate (Walmart) — Walmart's direct-hire LP program; high-volume environment with consistent scheduling and Walmart employee benefits
  • Mall LP — uniformed and plainclothes — NorthPark, Galleria, Allen Premium Outlets, North East Mall; multi-tenant retail environments with high foot traffic
  • Grocery LP — high shrink, high volume; accessible to entry-level candidates with guard card
  • Big-box retail security — Home Depot, Lowe's, Best Buy; floor security with LP investigator support structure

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — required for all paid LP roles in Texas
  • Observation skills and CCTV proficiency — for LP investigator roles; ability to monitor multiple camera feeds and document incidents accurately
  • Judgment under pressure — lawful detention decisions require composure; prior retail, security, or law enforcement experience is valued
  • Professional presentation — retailer brands hold uniformed LP staff to customer-facing appearance standards
  • Evening and weekend availability — peak LP demand is evenings, weekends, and the November–January holiday season
