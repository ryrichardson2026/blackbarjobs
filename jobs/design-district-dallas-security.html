Design District

Security Demand in the Dallas Design District

The Dallas Design District — the mixed-use commercial corridor west of downtown along the Stemmons Freeway between Oak Lawn Avenue and Motor Street — has evolved from a pure trade showroom district into one of Dallas's most dynamic commercial neighborhoods. The area now houses luxury home and interior design showrooms, contemporary art galleries, high-end restaurants, event venues, boutique hotels, and a growing concentration of tech and creative office tenants alongside its traditional trade showroom base.

The Design District's security profile is distinctive. Luxury showrooms containing high-value furniture, art, and design inventory require overnight and weekend armed coverage for after-hours protection. The district's art galleries — including several significant contemporary art spaces — require sophisticated access control and security monitoring during exhibitions. The area's growing data center presence along the Stemmons corridor adds technology infrastructure security demand on top of the commercial and retail profile.

Design District security positions skew toward officers who can present professionally in an upscale commercial environment while also managing the more industrial overnight security demands of the district's warehouse and data center properties. The combination creates a varied assignment landscape with above-average compensation for candidates who can navigate both environments. Active hiring zip codes include 75207 and 75219.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Security Jobs Are Available in the Design District?

Design District security spans several distinct environments within a compact geographic area. Luxury showroom security during business hours is professional and client-facing — officers manage access to trade-only showrooms, monitor showroom floors containing high-value inventory, and coordinate vendor and delivery access during market events. After-hours showroom security shifts to armed protection of high-value inventory during overnight hours.

Art gallery security in the Design District requires a combination of access control, visitor management, and the ability to monitor open exhibition spaces during events without being intrusive. Exhibition openings and private viewing events are the highest-demand periods — requiring officers comfortable in an upscale social environment alongside their security function. Gallery security during off-hours shifts to CCTV monitoring and perimeter protection.

Data center security along the Stemmons corridor adds a technology infrastructure protection dimension — access control for authorized technical personnel, after-hours monitoring, and armed overnight patrol at facilities where the physical infrastructure supports major cloud and enterprise computing operations. This is the highest-compensating category within the Design District security market.

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct after-hours armed patrol of showroom and gallery properties protecting high-value inventory
  • Control access to data center facilities — verify authorized personnel and log all after-hours entry
  • Monitor CCTV systems across mixed-use Design District properties
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD and property management on incidents in the district
  • Write detailed shift logs and incident reports for all Design District security events

Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required — Level 3 for armed showroom overnight and data center posts
  • Professional appearance and composure in upscale commercial and gallery environments
  • Prior security experience in luxury retail, hospitality, or corporate settings strongly valued for showroom and gallery assignments
  • Technical comfort with CCTV and access control systems for data center and monitoring assignments
  • Overnight availability significantly increases available positions — the district's overnight security demand is high
  • Valid driver's license for patrol roles covering the district's commercial campus footprint
Schedules

  • Overnight positions (10pm–6am) are the most consistently available — showroom, gallery, and data center overnight coverage

  • Weekend coverage available at event venues and galleries with Saturday and Sunday programming
