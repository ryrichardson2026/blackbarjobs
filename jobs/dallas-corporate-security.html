Role Overview

What Corporate Security Jobs Are Available in Dallas?

Entry and mid-level corporate security roles in Dallas include security operations center (SOC) analysts monitoring access control and CCTV systems across corporate campuses, shift supervisors overseeing contracted guard services on behalf of the corporate security department, and corporate security investigators handling internal investigations, employee threat assessments, and workplace violence prevention cases.

Senior corporate security roles in Dallas include corporate security managers overseeing multi-site security programs for regional or national operations, travel security specialists advising on international travel risk for executives and employees, and threat intelligence analysts monitoring for risks to corporate personnel, facilities, and intellectual property. These roles require significant prior security, law enforcement, or military experience and often carry six-figure compensation.

The distinction between corporate security and contracted guard security is important to understand. Contracted security officers work for guard firms deployed at corporate sites. Corporate security professionals work directly for the corporation — they manage the guard firm contracts, set security policy, conduct investigations, and advise corporate leadership on security strategy. Both categories are active in Dallas, but corporate direct-hire positions carry significantly higher compensation and career trajectory.
