How do I get a Level 3 security license in Texas? +
You must first hold or complete a Level 2 commission. Then complete a DPS-approved Level 3 firearms training course (minimum 40 hours) and pass a live-fire range qualification. Apply for the Level 3 commission through the Texas Online Private Security portal with your training certificate and range qualification documentation. Total additional cost is typically $240–$510 on top of Level 2 costs.

What is the difference between Level 2 and Level 3 security in Dallas? +
Level 2 is unarmed — officers hold a Non-Commissioned Security Officer Commission and cannot carry a firearm on duty. Level 3 is armed — officers hold a Commissioned Security Officer Commission and are authorized to carry a handgun. Level 3 requires additional firearms training and ongoing range qualification, and typically pays $5–10/hr more than comparable Level 2 positions in Dallas.
Can I carry any firearm with a Level 3 commission in Texas? +
Level 3 commissions authorize officers to carry the specific firearm they qualified with during their range qualification — typically a semi-automatic handgun. Officers must carry the same make and model they qualified with or complete a new qualification for a different firearm. Employer policies may further restrict firearm types and carry methods beyond the DPS minimum requirements.

What Is a Level 3 Security Officer in Texas?

A Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer Commission is issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety and authorizes the holder to carry a firearm while working as a paid security officer. It is required for all armed security roles in Texas — there are no exceptions. Officers must complete Level 2 training or hold an existing Level 2 commission before obtaining Level 3.

The Level 3 credential requires a minimum of 40 hours of DPS-approved firearms training plus a live-fire range qualification demonstrating proficiency with the approved handgun. This is in addition to the Level 2 training requirement. The commission renews every two years and requires both 6 hours of continuing education and a current range qualification at renewal.

Level 3 officers in Dallas are deployed at sites where the risk profile, asset value, or regulatory requirements justify an armed security presence. Common Dallas assignments include bank branches, armored car operations, pharmaceutical distribution, data centers, court security, and high-profile corporate campus protection. The visibility and responsibility of these assignments drives pay rates significantly above the Level 2 market.
