Do I need a guard card for unarmed security in Dallas? +
Yes. Texas requires a valid Security Officer Commission (Level II) for all paid unarmed security work. Most candidates complete the required training and obtain their commission within 2–4 weeks through a DPS-licensed training school. No firearm qualification is required for unarmed roles.
What companies hire unarmed security in Dallas? +
Allied Universal and Securitas hold major service contracts across downtown Dallas corporate towers and are among the most active unarmed hirers in the city. Healthcare systems including Parkland, UT Southwestern, and Baylor Scott & White hire both directly and through contracted guard services for unarmed hospital security roles.
Can I get an unarmed security job in Dallas with no experience? +
Yes. Many unarmed positions across Dallas — particularly residential communities, retail centers, and entry-level corporate campus posts — do not require prior security experience. A valid Level II guard card, clean background, and reliable transportation are typically the minimum requirements. Customer service, retail, and hospitality backgrounds are all considered relevant.
What is the difference between unarmed and armed security in Dallas? +
Unarmed security officers carry no firearm and require only a Texas Level II Security License. Armed officers carry a handgun and must hold a Level III Armed Commission, which requires additional firearms training and range qualification. Armed positions typically pay $5–10/hr more than comparable unarmed roles. Most Dallas corporate campus and retail assignments are unarmed — armed roles are concentrated at financial institutions, data centers, and high-value asset sites.
Are part-time unarmed security jobs available in Dallas? +
Yes. Part-time and flexible unarmed positions are available in Dallas, particularly for weekend coverage at retail centers and residential communities, and for supplemental staffing at corporate campuses. Many guard companies operating in Dallas offer part-time scheduling for candidates available on weekends or specific shift windows.

Role Overview

What Does an Unarmed Security Officer Do in Dallas?

Unarmed security officers provide visible deterrence, access control, and incident response without carrying a firearm. In Dallas's corporate-heavy environment, the role often blends traditional security with professional front-of-house responsibilities — greeting visitors, managing badge access, and serving as the first contact for anyone entering a secured facility.

Day shifts at CBD and Uptown corporate towers are higher-visibility and customer-facing. Evening and overnight shifts shift toward independent patrol, monitoring, and documentation. Healthcare assignments at Parkland Memorial, UT Southwestern, and Baylor Scott & White add a de-escalation component specific to medical environments.

Unarmed positions are a strong entry point for candidates new to security, and a preferred long-term option for officers who want professional, stable schedules without the additional licensing burden of armed roles.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage lobby access, visitor sign-in, and badge issuance at corporate facilities
  • Conduct scheduled interior and exterior patrols throughout the shift
  • Monitor CCTV systems and access control panels
  • Respond to disturbances, medical situations, and alarm activations
  • Write accurate shift logs and incident reports for each assignment
  • Coordinate with building management, tenants, and law enforcement as needed
  • Enforce parking and trespassing policies on corporate or retail properties
  • Provide visible security presence deterring theft, vandalism, and unauthorized access

Overview

Unarmed Security Guard Jobs in Dallas, TX

Dallas proper is one of the largest unarmed security markets in Texas — driven by the city's density of corporate towers, healthcare systems, entertainment districts, government buildings, and residential high-rises. Level 2 non-commissioned security officers work across every corner of the city, from Uptown lobbies to South Dallas industrial corridors to the Medical District around Parkland and UT Southwestern.

The Dallas unarmed security market breaks down by district — and each district has a different security profile, different employers, and different pay expectations. Understanding where the work is concentrated helps candidates target the right accounts and negotiate the right rate.

Dallas Districts and Their Security Markets

Uptown Dallas — the densest concentration of corporate lobby and residential tower security in the city. High-rise apartment communities, boutique hotels, and the office corridor along McKinney Ave and Cedar Springs generate consistent full-time unarmed demand. Professional presentation is the standard here — these are client-facing posts. Pay: $18–24/hr.

Downtown Dallas / CBD — government buildings, the Dallas Arts District, Reunion Tower, hotel towers, and the Earle Cabell Federal Complex. A mix of government-contracted and privately contracted security. Higher presentation standards, more structured post assignments. Some positions require background checks beyond standard DPS requirements for courthouse and municipal building posts. Pay: $18–24/hr.

Medical District — anchored by Parkland Memorial Hospital, UT Southwestern Medical Center, and Children's Medical Center. Healthcare security is one of the most active unarmed employment categories in Dallas — patient and visitor management, emergency department coverage, campus patrol. Parkland and UT Southwestern offer direct county and university employment with government benefits. Pay: $17–24/hr, with government benefit packages adding significant total compensation at direct-hire accounts.

Deep Ellum — Dallas's primary live music and entertainment district. Venue security, bar security, and event floor staff concentrate here Thursday through Sunday nights. flexible and part-time schedules dominate. Less corporate, more crowd management. Pay: $17–22/hr, primarily evenings and weekends.

Design District — showrooms, warehouses converted to event spaces, and boutique retail. A mix of daytime commercial security and evening event security as the district hosts private events and gallery openings. Pay: $16–20/hr.

South Dallas / I-20 Corridor — the largest concentration of overnight industrial and warehouse security in the city. Distribution centers, manufacturing facilities, and freight operations along the I-20 and I-35 corridors generate consistent overnight patrol demand. Less client-facing, more physical stamina required. Overnight differential typically adds $1–2/hr above base. Pay: $15–20/hr + overnight differential.

North Dallas / Preston Hollow / Park Cities — high-value residential security, HOA gate coverage, and private estate security. Residential gate posts are the most accessible entry point for new Level 2 officers in Dallas. Pay: $15–19/hr.
