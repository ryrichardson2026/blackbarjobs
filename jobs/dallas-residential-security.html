Is residential security a good entry-level job in Dallas?+
Yes — apartment community gate security is one of the most accessible entry points in the Dallas security market. Requirements are typically a valid guard card, clean background, and professional demeanor. Customer service, retail, and hospitality backgrounds are all competitive. Many Dallas security officers start in residential gate work before moving into higher-paying corporate or healthcare assignments.
What does residential security pay in Dallas?+
Apartment community gate security starts at $14–17/hr — at the lower end of the Dallas unarmed range. HOA patrol and gated neighborhood security runs $17–22/hr. Luxury high-rise residential security in Uptown reaches $22–30/hr. Overnight and weekend shifts carry $1–2/hr differentials on top of base pay.
Can I work residential security part-time in Dallas?+
Yes — residential gate security is one of the most part-time-friendly categories in Dallas. Many apartment communities specifically post for Saturday and Sunday gate coverage or evening shifts that work around a primary weekday job. Guard firms managing residential accounts also maintain per-diem rosters for supplemental coverage needs.
What is the difference between residential security and commercial security in Dallas?+
Residential security focuses specifically on protecting people in their living environments — apartment communities, gated neighborhoods, and residential towers. Commercial security covers office buildings, retail, and business properties. Residential security tends to have more consistent and predictable scheduling, higher customer service requirements, and slightly lower pay than comparable commercial positions. The daily interaction with residents also requires a different communication style than commercial guard work.
Do Dallas apartment communities hire security directly or through guard firms?+
Both. Larger apartment management companies in Dallas often contract through guard firms for flexibility and liability management. Some luxury residential towers and large Class A apartment communities hire security officers directly as building staff. Direct-hire residential positions often include building employee benefits. Guard firm residential positions offer more flexible scheduling across multiple accounts.

What Does Residential Security Look Like in Dallas?

Residential security in Dallas spans a wide range of environments. Gate security officers at apartment communities manage vehicle and pedestrian access, verify residents and guests, enforce community rules, and serve as the visible security presence that management uses to justify security fees to residents. The role is highly visible, customer-facing, and requires consistent professional conduct across every resident interaction.

HOA and gated neighborhood patrol involves vehicle patrol of residential streets, monitoring for unauthorized vehicles, enforcing community rules on noise, parking, and amenity use, and providing a visible deterrence against residential burglary. Officers in these roles typically work alone and must be self-directed during lower-activity overnight shifts.

Luxury high-rise residential security in Uptown and the CBD mirrors hotel security in its professional standards — officers represent the building brand, interact with affluent residents, and manage everything from package delivery coordination to after-hours disturbances with a discretion that standard apartment community security doesn't require. Pay rates at luxury towers correspondingly exceed apartment community baseline rates.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Manage vehicle and pedestrian access at apartment community gates and residential entrances
  • Verify residents and guests — enforce access policies and visitor log procedures
  • Conduct scheduled patrol of assigned residential community grounds and parking areas
  • Respond to resident complaints, disturbances, and security incidents professionally
  • Monitor community amenities — pool areas, fitness centers, and common spaces after hours
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD on trespassing incidents, theft, and disturbances requiring law enforcement
  • Write shift logs and incident reports for all residential security events
  • Enforce community rules on noise, parking, and after-hours amenity use
