How do I become a security officer in Dallas?+
Complete a DPS-approved Level 2 training course (available in weekend intensive formats in Dallas), pass the written exam, submit fingerprints through IdentoGO, and apply through the Texas Online Private Security portal. Total cost is $100–$260. The process takes 2–4 weeks. Once your commission is active, you're eligible for the majority of Dallas security officer positions.
What is the difference between a security guard and a security officer in Dallas?+
In Texas, the formal credential is Security Officer Commission — not guard card or security guard. The terms are used interchangeably in common usage. Legally, anyone providing paid security services in Texas must hold a valid Level 2 or Level 3 commission issued by the Texas DPS. The job title on your employment paperwork may say Security Officer, Security Guard, or Security Specialist — all refer to the same licensed role.
What security officer jobs pay the most in Dallas?+
Armed overnight positions at data centers and financial institutions are the highest-compensating standard security roles in Dallas — typically $26–42/hr for experienced officers. Armed assignments at government buildings and corporate headquarters are the next highest category. Unarmed top earners include hospital security supervisors, Class A office tower overnight leads, and experienced mobile patrol supervisors.
Do Dallas security officers need to carry a gun?+
Not necessarily. The majority of security officer positions in Dallas are unarmed (Level 2) roles. Armed positions require a Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer Commission and are concentrated at financial institutions, data centers, government buildings, and high-value industrial sites. Most corporate campus, retail, residential, and event security positions are unarmed.

Dallas security officer jobs span every category in the industry. Unarmed officers work corporate lobbies, healthcare facilities, retail centers, residential communities, and event venues.
