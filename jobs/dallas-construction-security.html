How long do construction security jobs last in Dallas?+
Construction security assignments are project-duration — typically ranging from a few weeks for small commercial builds to 12–24 months for major high-rise and campus development projects. Guard firms managing construction security accounts typically transfer officers directly to the next construction project when a current assignment wraps, providing continuity of employment for reliable officers.
What does construction security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level construction site security runs $15–18/hr with overnight differentials of $1–3/hr. Experienced construction patrol officers earn $18–24/hr. Senior officers managing multi-site construction patrol routes or armed construction posts reach $24–32/hr.
Is construction security dangerous in Dallas?+
Construction site security involves working alone overnight in an environment with uneven terrain, limited lighting, and the possibility of encountering trespassers or theft attempts. Serious confrontational incidents are uncommon — the primary deterrence value of construction security is the officer's presence discouraging theft before it occurs. Officers are expected to observe and report rather than physically intervene in active theft situations involving multiple individuals.
Do I need construction experience for construction security jobs in Dallas?+
No — prior construction industry knowledge is not required. A valid guard card, clean background, valid driver's license, and overnight availability are the standard requirements. Familiarity with construction sites is helpful for navigating assignments safely, but guard firms provide site-specific orientation before first shift.
What is the biggest theft risk at Dallas construction sites?+
Copper wire and copper plumbing are the most consistently targeted materials at Dallas construction sites — copper theft is a multi-million dollar annual problem across North Texas. Heavy equipment (skid steers, generators, compressors) and power tools are the next highest-value targets. Catalytic converter theft from construction vehicles parked on site overnight has also increased significantly across the Dallas metro in recent years.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Does Construction Security Look Like in Dallas?

Construction security officers in Dallas are assigned to a single active build site for the duration of their shift — typically overnight or weekend hours when the construction crew is absent. The primary mission is deterrence: a marked patrol vehicle and uniformed officer present on site dramatically reduces the likelihood of theft and vandalism compared to an unguarded site.

Active duties include perimeter vehicle patrol, periodic walkthroughs of the structure to check for unauthorized entry, monitoring and logging any vehicle or individual approaching the site during off-hours, and responding to alarms if the site has security system integration. Officers also document any observed damage, graffiti, or material discrepancies at shift start and end to establish accountability records for the construction company.

Construction security assignments in Dallas are typically contract-based — tied to the project timeline rather than a permanent facility. Officers who perform well on one project are often transferred directly to the guard firm's next construction contract. This creates a rolling assignment model that provides consistent work for reliable officers across multiple Dallas development projects over time.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Conduct vehicle and foot patrol of assigned construction site perimeter and interior
  • Monitor and log all vehicle and personnel access to the site during assigned shift
  • Deter and respond to theft attempts targeting equipment, copper, tools, and materials
  • Document site conditions at shift start and end — equipment inventory, structural status
  • Respond to perimeter alarms and unauthorized access alerts
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD on theft incidents and trespassing at construction sites
  • Write detailed shift logs and incident reports for construction company and guard firm review
  • Maintain visibility and deterrence presence throughout the shift

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required
  • Valid driver's license for vehicle patrol of construction site perimeters
  • Comfort with extended solo overnight assignments in outdoor construction environments
  • Physical fitness for walking uneven construction site terrain in all weather conditions
  • Prior security, military, or construction industry experience relevant but not required for entry-level sites
  • Overnight and weekend availability — the primary demand window for all Dallas construction security
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • Overnight shifts (10pm–6am or dusk-to-dawn) are the standard for construction site security in Dallas
  • Weekend coverage required — construction sites are highest-risk for theft on Saturday and Sunday when crews are absent
  • Project-based assignments — position duration tied to construction timeline (weeks to months)
  • 12-hour overnight shifts common at high-value construction sites to minimize handoff gaps
  • Shift differentials of $1–3/hr for overnight construction patrol
  • Rolling assignment model — reliable officers move between projects as guard firm construction contracts rotate
