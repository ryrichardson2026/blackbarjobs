Overview

Event Security Jobs in DFW

Dallas–Fort Worth has one of the most active event security markets in the United States — anchored by four major professional sports teams, two of the largest venues in Texas, and a year-round calendar of concerts, conventions, and major events that generates consistent flexible security demand across the metro.

American Airlines Center (home of the Dallas Mavericks and Dallas Stars) is the single largest ongoing event security employer in Dallas proper — running back-to-back NBA and NHL seasons from October through June, plus concerts and additional events. AT&T Stadium in Arlington is one of the largest domed stadiums in the world, generating massive security staffing deployments for Dallas Cowboys games and major events. Globe Life Field (Texas Rangers), Dickies Arena (Fort Worth), and the Dallas Convention Center round out the primary venue employment base.

Event Security Role Types in DFW

  • Arena security — Mavericks and Stars — American Airlines Center runs one of the most consistent event security employment programs in DFW; both NBA and NHL seasons run simultaneously October through June
  • Stadium security — Cowboys — AT&T Stadium game day deployments are among the largest single-event security staffing operations in the metro; Sunday game days are the primary schedule
  • Baseball security — Rangers — Globe Life Field's 162-game MLB season runs April through October; one of the most consistent seasonal event security programs in DFW
  • Concert and arena events — non-sports events at all major DFW venues; summer and fall concert seasons drive above-normal flexible demand
  • Convention center security — the Dallas Convention Center runs year-round events with above-normal demand during major convention weeks
  • Nightlife and venue security — Deep Ellum, Uptown, and the Fort Worth entertainment district generate consistent Thursday–Saturday flexible security demand

AT&T Stadium Security Jobs

AT&T Stadium Security Jobs — Arlington, TX

AT&T Stadium in Arlington is one of the largest domed stadiums in the world and home to the Dallas Cowboys — generating some of the largest single-event security deployments in the DFW metro. Cowboys home games run September through January with regular season Sundays as the primary deployment days, plus preseason games in August and playoff games when applicable.

Security officers at AT&T Stadium work game day deployments covering perimeter access control, parking lot security, gate entry screening, concourse patrol, and suite level security. Officers are contracted through Allied Universal and Securitas under the stadium's security program — apply specifying interest in AT&T Stadium and Cowboys game day assignments.

Cowboys Stadium Security — What To Expect

  • Schedule — Sunday home games are the primary commitment. 8 regular season home games plus preseason. Shift typically runs 4 hours before kickoff through 1 hour post-game.
  • Posts — parking perimeter, gate entry screening, concourse patrol, field level, suite corridors. Gate entry and perimeter are most available for new roster additions.
  • Pay — $17–22/hr for Cowboys game day security. Playoff games and major events (Super Bowl, Big 12 Championship, concerts) pay above standard game day rates.
  • Requirements — valid Texas Level 2 security license, professional uniform presentation per Allied Universal standards, pre-shift briefing attendance required.
  • Getting on the roster — apply to Allied Universal DFW office directly and specify Cowboys/AT&T Stadium interest. Rosters are filled before the season starts — apply in June/July for the September season opener.

Other Major DFW Venue Security

  • American Airlines Center (Dallas) — Mavericks (NBA) and Stars (NHL) run simultaneously October through June. One of the most consistent flexible event security programs in Dallas — nearly every week has a game.
  • Globe Life Field (Arlington) — Texas Rangers MLB season runs April through October. 81 home games per season make this one of the highest-volume seasonal event security programs in DFW.
  • Dickies Arena (Fort Worth) — year-round concerts and events, plus rodeo season. Fort Worth's primary large venue security account.
  • Toyota Music Factory (Irving) — outdoor amphitheater and entertainment complex. Summer and fall concert season drives active flexible security demand.
  • Dos Equis Pavilion (Dallas) — summer outdoor concert venue at Fair Park. Active May through September.
  • State Fair of Texas (Dallas) — 24-day annual fair run in October at Fair Park. One of the largest single-event security deployments in Texas — hundreds of officers across the full run.

Requirements

What You Need to Get Hired

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License — required for all paid event security roles
  • Evening and weekend availability — virtually all DFW event security occurs Thursday through Sunday evenings
  • Professional physical presentation — major venue accounts (AAC, AT&T Stadium) enforce dress and grooming standards
  • Customer service orientation — event security involves constant fan and guest interaction; composure in high-energy crowd environments is essential
  • Crowd management experience — prior experience in high-volume public environments (retail, hospitality, military) is valued by venue security managers
