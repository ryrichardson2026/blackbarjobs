What is event security work at AT&T Stadium like?+

Overview

DFW Event Security: NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, and the Busiest Entertainment Market in Texas

The Dallas–Fort Worth metro hosts more major professional sports teams than any other Texas market — Cowboys (NFL), Mavericks (NBA), Stars (NHL), Rangers (MLB), FC Dallas (MLS), and the Texas Legends (G League). Each franchise has a home venue running 40–82 home events per year, creating the most consistent year-round event security employment calendar in Texas. Add Dos Equis Pavilion's summer concert season, the State Fair of Texas in October, Dickies Arena's rodeo and concert calendar, and Toyota Music Factory's year-round programming, and DFW's event security market runs essentially every week of the year.

BEST Crowd Management, Andy Frain Services, and Contemporary Services Corporation are the dominant event security employers across DFW's major venues. Getting on the roster at two or three of these firms gives officers access to the full DFW event calendar — football, basketball, hockey, baseball, concerts, and rodeos — at rates running $16–$22/hr depending on the event and venue.

Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in DFW

Entry screening and magnetometer operation is the baseline event security function — verifying tickets, conducting bag checks, operating walk-through magnetometers at entry gates, and managing the flow of thousands of attendees into the venue within the pre-event window. Officers at AT&T Stadium manage entry flows for 80,000+ attendees on Cowboys game days — an operational scale that develops crowd management competency no other Texas venue replicates.

In-venue crowd management covers seating area monitoring, aisle and concourse crowd flow, incident de-escalation, and medical emergency coordination. Officers in premium seating areas and floor-level positions work directly with event staff and venue operations to maintain the guest experience while managing incidents professionally. The combination of large-scale entry management and in-venue incident response develops the full spectrum of event security competency within a single market.
