What is the difference between retail security and loss prevention in Dallas?+
Uniformed retail security focuses on visible deterrence and customer-facing security presence. Loss prevention (LP) is specifically focused on detecting and stopping theft — LP officers often work in plainclothes, conduct CCTV surveillance, and build apprehension cases. LP is a more investigative, specialized role than general retail security. Both require a Texas Level 2 commission. LP investigators typically earn more and have more structured career advancement within retail organizations.
What does retail security pay in Dallas?+
Entry-level uniformed retail security runs $15–18/hr. Experienced LP investigators at major Dallas retailers earn $18–25/hr. Senior LP, organized retail crime specialists, and district LP managers reach $25–34/hr. Luxury retail assignments at NorthPark and Highland Park Village pay at the upper end of the uniformed security range.
Can I get a retail security job in Dallas with no experience?+
Yes — entry-level uniformed floor security at standard Dallas retailers is accessible to candidates with a guard card, clean background, and retail or customer service experience. Plainclothes LP investigator positions require more background and are less accessible to first-time security candidates. Starting in uniformed retail security and building toward LP is the standard progression.

Is retail security seasonal in Dallas?+
Retail security is available year-round but has clear seasonal peaks. Black Friday through New Year's is the highest-demand period — retailers add significant supplemental security during the holiday shopping season. Back-to-school (July through September) is the second major peak. Summer and post-holiday periods are the lightest. Candidates with year-round availability have consistent options across all Dallas retail security categories.

Retail Security Demand in Dallas

Dallas is one of the strongest retail security markets in Texas. Role Overview

What Does Retail Security Look Like in Dallas?

Retail security officers in Dallas work in one of two modes. Uniformed floor security provides visible presence at store entrances and on the sales floor — the goal is deterrence. Officers greet customers, monitor floor activity, and coordinate with store management on any security concerns. The role is customer-facing and requires a professional, approachable demeanor alongside security function awareness.

Plainclothes LP investigators work differently — their goal is detection and apprehension. They monitor CCTV systems, conduct covert surveillance on suspects, build apprehension cases by observing the full act of concealment and exit, and detain shoplifters following strict company and legal protocols. Dallas LP investigators at major retailers regularly work organized retail crime cases involving repeat offenders and coordinated theft operations.

Retail security at Dallas's luxury tier — NorthPark's high-end anchors, Highland Park Village boutiques, and luxury jewelry counters — requires a higher standard of professional presentation and customer interaction than standard big-box retail security. Officers in luxury retail environments are expected to be indistinguishable from store associates in terms of professional conduct, while remaining fully capable of security response when required.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Provide uniformed floor security and visible deterrence at retail entrances and sales floors
  • Monitor customer behavior and identify theft indicators on the sales floor

  • Conduct plainclothes surveillance and apprehend shoplifters per company policy
  • Process apprehensions — documentation, police coordination, and trespass notices
  • Train store associates on theft awareness and prevention best practices
  • Conduct internal investigations involving employee theft and policy violations
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD on organized retail crime incidents and repeat offenders
