Does Parkland Hospital hire security directly?+
Yes. Parkland Health employs security officers as direct health system employees through its Security Services department. What is the hardest healthcare security job in Dallas?+
Parkland Memorial's emergency department security is widely considered the most demanding healthcare security assignment in Dallas. As a Level I trauma center serving the county's highest-acuity patient population, Parkland's ED security officers regularly manage combative patients, psychiatric crises, and high-tension family situations in a high-volume environment. Officers who excel in this environment are among the most experienced healthcare security professionals in the city.
Do Dallas healthcare security jobs include benefits?+

Contracted guard service positions at the same facilities may offer benefits through the guard firm, which are typically less comprehensive. When evaluating healthcare security positions in Dallas, always clarify whether the role is direct health system employment or through a contracted guard service.
What is the career path for healthcare security in Dallas?+

Healthcare security is one of the few Dallas security categories with a clear multi-level career ladder.

Role Overview

What Healthcare Security Jobs Are Available in Dallas?

Dallas healthcare security spans several distinct roles across its major health systems. Emergency department security at Level I trauma centers is the most demanding and best-compensated unarmed healthcare security role — requiring active de-escalation with psychiatric patients, combative individuals, and distressed family members in a high-volume clinical environment. Access control at research and pharmaceutical facilities adds a documentation-intensive, compliance-oriented dimension to the healthcare security profile.

Community hospital security across the Methodist, Medical City, and Texas Health networks provides more moderate-intensity environments where officers focus on patient safety, visitor management, and perimeter patrol. These assignments offer good stability, consistent scheduling, and advancement opportunities within large health system organizations. Pediatric hospital security at Children's Medical Center and Children's Health facilities requires a specialized approach for working with pediatric patients and their families.

Healthcare security is also one of the few security categories in Dallas where direct employment — rather than contracted guard services — is common. Parkland Health, UT Southwestern, and several other major Dallas health systems employ security officers directly as system employees with full benefits including health insurance, retirement contributions, and paid time off.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Control access to patient care units, emergency departments, and restricted clinical areas
  • Respond to Code Gray behavioral health incidents and assist clinical staff with patient management
  • Conduct de-escalation with patients, visitors, and family members in distress
  • Patrol hospital campuses, parking structures, and facility perimeters
  • Monitor CCTV and access control systems at hospital security operations centers
  • Escort staff, patients, and vendors safely throughout facility campuses
  • Coordinate with Dallas PD and EMS on incidents requiring law enforcement response
  • Write detailed incident reports reviewed by clinical, legal, and accreditation teams

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Security License required — CPR/First Aid certification required at most Dallas health systems
  • De-escalation training required or strongly preferred at emergency department and behavioral health assignments
  • Prior security, law enforcement, military, or healthcare background strongly preferred
  • Physical ability to respond quickly and assist clinical staff in demanding environments
  • Comfort working with high-acuity patient populations — psychiatric, trauma, substance-involved, and pediatric cases
  • Strong written documentation skills for reports reviewed by clinical and regulatory stakeholders
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All three shifts available across all Dallas health systems — 24/7/365 with no slowdown
  • 12-hour rotations are standard at Parkland, UT Southwestern, and large Baylor Scott & White facilities
  • Overnight and weekend healthcare shifts carry additional differentials on top of healthcare security base rates
  • Holiday coverage required — Dallas health systems maintain full security staffing on all holidays
  • Emergency department assignments are highest intensity and typically filled after a probationary period
  • Research and outpatient facility assignments offer more moderate intensity for officers preferring lower-acuity environments
