Frequently Asked Questions

Why is security work a good job for UT Austin and ACC students?+
Three reasons that separate security from typical student jobs. Pay: the verified Austin market rates for part-time security range from $19/hr (Signal of Austin mobile patrol, ACT Security rover) to $21/hr (Sunstates Part-Time 3rd shift overnight weekends, seasonal unarmed posts) to $22/hr (evening and weekend posts Thursday–Sunday). These rates are $3–$6/hr above most campus, retail, and food service student employment. Schedule: security employers explicitly design part-time and flex shifts around evening and weekend availability — the hours that don't compete with daytime classes. Sunstates posts specifically for Part-Time 3rd shift including weekends at $21/hr; ACT Security Group's Rover mid-shift runs Friday through Tuesday 1pm–10pm; GardaWorld's FLEX program lets you receive text notifications for available shifts and pick them up at your convenience. Resume: security experience builds the concrete professional skills — incident report documentation, access control, de-escalation, CCTV operation — that translate directly into applications for law enforcement, criminal justice careers, corporate security, and graduate programs.

Austin's security market has more student-compatible shift patterns than almost any other entry-level job category. Confirmed active Austin postings include: Security Officer posting explicitly Thursday 7pm–1am / Friday 7pm–7am / Saturday–Sunday 6pm–6am at $22 — ideal for students with Monday–Friday morning and afternoon classes. Sunstates Part-Time 3rd shift overnight weekends at $21/hr weekly pay — Friday and Saturday nights when most students are awake anyway. Seasonal post confirmed running through September 2026, Monday–Friday 12pm–8pm plus Saturday–Sunday 10am–8pm at $21 — a complete summer job. ACT Security Group's Unarmed Rover mid-shift runs Friday through Tuesday 1pm–10pm — workable with a Monday–Wednesday–Friday class schedule. GardaWorld's FLEX program sends text notifications when shifts open across Austin accounts — you pick up what fits your week. Event security at Moody Center (BEST Crowd Management, 150+ events/yr) means working a concert Thursday night doesn't affect Friday classes if you plan around the calendar.
How do I get a security license while enrolled as a student?+
The Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer course is a 6-hour online DPS-approved program you can complete in one sitting on any device, any time — literally during a study break if you have six hours of focus. The total cost is $117–$170 including the DPS-approved training ($47 at Defencify Training or securityguardschool.com), the TOPS application fee (approximately $40), and IdentoGO fingerprinting (approximately $30). After submitting your TOPS application and completing fingerprinting, DPS processes most Austin applications within 2–4 weeks. You can begin working once your status shows ACTIVE in the TOPS system — no need to wait for the physical pocket card. Apply at GardaWorld's Thursday walk-in at 11044 Research Blvd (Stratum Executive Center) with your TOPS ACTIVE status proof and you can start within days. No prior experience required. No physical classroom requirement. Complete it between semesters or during a light week.
What majors benefit most from security officer experience?+
Criminal justice and criminology — most directly. Security experience is the first entry on every criminal justice career path: incident report writing develops the documentation discipline that LE agencies test, de-escalation competency maps directly to the scenarios in police academy training, and CCTV and access control system familiarity is exactly what corporate security management roles require. Pre-law students develop the evidence-of-conduct documentation skills that legal careers require and exposure to how law enforcement interactions work in the field that no classroom replicates. Public policy and sociology students gain firsthand observation of the public safety systems they're studying and professional experience that differentiates them in graduate program applications. Psychology majors who work hospital or event security develop behavioral de-escalation competency in live settings. Business and management students who take supervisor or shift lead roles within 6–12 months of starting get documented leadership experience over a team — the entry that differentiates otherwise identical resumes at the graduate business school application stage.
What career paths does security open up from Austin?+
Three primary tracks. Law enforcement: security officer → TCOLE police academy → Austin PD, Travis County Sheriff, UT Police Department, or other Texas agency. Security experience on a law enforcement application signals that the candidate has already demonstrated patrol discipline, documentation habits, and composure in civilian security incidents — it's the most valued non-sworn experience by Texas law enforcement hiring panels. Corporate security: security officer → senior officer → shift supervisor → SOC operator → security manager or analyst. Allied Universal's own career example of Adriana Lopez (Security Officer → Account Manager) illustrates the internal track. Austin's tech economy specifically values corporate security experience at tech campus accounts — officers who work Domain-area tech campuses gain exposure to enterprise access control systems and security operations that tech companies' internal security teams actively recruit from. Event and venue management: security officer at Moody Center, SXSW, or Austin FC → venue operations → event management → production coordination. Austin's live music and events economy gives student security officers direct industry exposure that no classroom course replicates.
Can I work security part-time at UT Austin specifically?+
UT Austin employs 10,000+ students each semester across academic, non-academic, Federal Work-Study, and non-Work-Study positions. The UT Applied Research Laboratories has posted Security Guard positions directly. UT Austin's UTPD (University of Texas Police Department) employs both sworn peace officers and civilian security roles, and the part-time Police Communications Operator role — which includes voluntary vision, dental, life, and disability insurance options — is accessible to students who meet the requirements. Additionally, Allied Universal holds contracted security accounts at UT Austin and neighboring campuses, posting Security Officer – Campus Patrols roles specifically for the education campus environment. Austin Community College, which serves approximately 70,000 credit students and is one of the largest community college districts in Texas, has its own District Police operation with Cadet and Patrol Officer positions. Getting on campus-specific security accounts is competitive — the advantage of the contracted security market (GardaWorld, Sunstates, AU) is faster hiring and more flexible scheduling than direct university employment.
What does the pay look like compared to other student jobs?+
The comparison is straightforward. Typical Austin student jobs — food service, campus retail, library desk, tutoring center — pay $14–$17/hr. Part-time security in Austin's verified 2026 market pays $19–$22/hr at the positions designed for evening and weekend availability, with GardaWorld's FLEX model paying $19–$21.50/hr with DailyPay (get paid the same day you work) and Sunstates' Part-Time 3rd shift paying $21/hr weekly. A student working 15–20 hours per week of security at $21/hr earns $315–$420 per week. At a typical $15/hr retail job, the same hours produce $225–$300 per week — $90–$120 less per week, $1,800–$2,400 less per semester. That difference covers books, groceries, or monthly rent contributions. The schedule flexibility also means security hours don't have to displace study time the way fixed retail or food service shifts do — you can take a Thursday–Sunday evening security pattern and keep your weekday study schedule intact.
