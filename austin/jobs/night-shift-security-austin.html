FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the most night shift security demand in Austin?+
The bulk of Austin night shift demand comes from warehouse and distribution along the SH-130 corridor, the Tesla and Samsung plants, tech campuses at the Domain and Del Valle, hospital security in the medical district, and residential gatehouse coverage in suburban communities. These accounts run around the clock, and overnight is consistently the hardest segment to staff.
What is the difference between 2nd and 3rd shift in Austin?+
Second shift, or swing, typically runs 2pm to 10pm or 3pm to 11pm and covers the transition from business hours into evening, with more human interaction. Third shift, or graveyard, runs 10pm to 6am or 11pm to 7am, the window when warehouse theft risk peaks and plants run continuous operations. Third shift is quieter, independent patrol and monitoring work.
Does night shift security pay more in Austin?+
Yes. Overnight differentials of $1-3/hr are standard on top of base across Austin accounts, one of the most underused pay levers in Texas security. At 40 hours a week, a $2/hr differential adds about $4,160 a year over base. Tech-campus and armed overnight posts push the total higher.
Is night shift security in Austin safe?+
The question is about incident profile, not physical danger. Most Austin night shift accounts, warehouse, tech campus, and residential, are lower-incident environments than daytime retail or nightlife. Distribution and plant graveyard shifts are quiet patrol and monitoring work. The practical challenges are staying alert through the shift and the drive home afterward, not the post itself.
What do I need to get hired for night shift security in Austin?+
A valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license covers unarmed overnight posts, and a Level 3 commission is required for armed overnight accounts. Employers want genuine overnight availability, a valid driver's license for patrol and multi-site coverage, and reliable transportation since overnight transit is limited. Staying alert and documenting clearly through a graveyard shift matters most.
Overview

Austin Night Shift Security: Where the Overnight Demand Is

Austin runs a deep overnight security market driven by its logistics, tech, and manufacturing base. The SH-130 warehouse corridor, the Tesla and Samsung plants, tech campuses at the Domain and Del Valle, hospitals in the medical district, and suburban residential communities all need consistent 2nd and 3rd shift coverage, and overnight is the hardest segment to keep staffed.

Second shift, or swing, typically runs 2pm to 10pm and covers the transition from business hours into evening. Third shift, or graveyard, runs 10pm to 6am or 11pm to 7am, the window when warehouse theft risk peaks, plants run continuous operations, and commercial buildings sit empty. The two shifts are different environments, and officers usually settle into one.

Warehouse and distribution along SH-130, industrial and tech campuses, hospital security at St. David's and Ascension Seton, and residential gatehouse coverage generate the bulk of Austin night shift demand. Firms staff these accounts year round, and reliable overnight officers get steady work and consistent overtime.

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Run scheduled overnight patrol of the assigned warehouse, industrial, or commercial facility, all points logged
  • Staff 2nd and 3rd shift access control and gate posts at logistics, tech, and industrial accounts
  • Monitor CCTV and alarm systems from the overnight console
  • Complete gate logs and access records, the primary output of most overnight posts
  • Respond to overnight incidents independently, assess, secure, contact dispatch, document
  • Staff overnight residential gatehouse and patrol in suburban communities
  • Conduct perimeter checks across the facility footprint through the shift
  • Maintain radio check-ins with dispatch throughout the shift

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license, required for unarmed overnight posts
  • Level 3 commission for armed overnight accounts, which pay above unarmed rates
  • Genuine overnight availability, since night shift accounts need consistent 2nd or 3rd shift coverage
  • Valid Texas driver's license, since overnight patrol and multi-site coverage require vehicle operation
  • Reliable personal transportation, because overnight transit across Austin is limited
  • Ability to stay alert and keep clear documentation through a graveyard shift
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • 2nd shift: 2pm to 10pm or 3pm to 11pm, swing coverage for industrial, retail, and residential accounts
  • 3rd shift: 10pm to 6am or 11pm to 7am, graveyard at distribution, plant, and overnight patrol accounts
  • 4x10 patterns common at 24/7 operations to reduce shift handoffs
  • Weekend overnight is the highest-demand segment across all account types
  • Consistent voluntary overtime, since overnight understaffing is chronic
  • Weekly pay common at the larger security firms
