FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does armed overnight pay the most in Austin?+
Armed overnight combines two premiums. The Level 3 commission opens accounts, tech campus, high-value, cleared, and armed industrial, that a Level 2 officer cannot work at any rate, and the overnight differential of $1-3/hr stacks on top of base. Together they produce the highest per-hour rate available to non-supervisory line officers in the market.
What license do I need for armed overnight security in Austin?+
A valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer license is required for all armed posts, along with a DPS commission in good standing and a clean background. The Level 3 is the credential that separates armed overnight pay from standard unarmed overnight rates.
Where do armed overnight officers work in Austin?+
Armed overnight posts include patrol-driver routes across the Del Valle corridor, high-value and asset protection posts, tech campus and industrial plants that run armed coverage after dark, cleared and defense-adjacent accounts tied to Lockheed and Raytheon operations, and banking, ATM, and cash-handling overnight support. Most are observation and deterrence roles.
Is armed overnight security dangerous in Austin?+
Most armed overnight posts are observation and deterrence, not confrontation. The armed qualification is there because the account, high-value, cash, tech, or cleared, requires it, but the day-to-day work is patrol, access control, and documentation. Officers are trained to observe, document, and report, and to escalate to dispatch and law enforcement rather than intervene.
What do I need to get hired for armed overnight security in Austin?+
A valid Texas Level 3 Commissioned Security Officer license and a DPS commission in good standing are required. Employers look for a clean background, use-of-force and weapon-handling discipline, a valid driver's license and reliable transportation for patrol-driver routes, and cleared or government credentialing for defense-adjacent accounts.
Overview

Austin Armed Overnight Security: The Top of the Overnight Range

Armed overnight is the highest-paying non-supervisory segment in Austin security. A Level 3 commission plus an overnight differential produces the best per-hour rate available to line officers, and demand runs steady across tech campus, industrial, high-value, and cleared accounts that need an armed presence after dark.

The work spans armed patrol-driver routes across the Del Valle and tech corridor, high-value and asset protection posts, industrial plants requiring an armed overnight presence, and cleared or defense-adjacent sites tied to Austin's Lockheed and Raytheon operations. Most posts are observation and deterrence, with the armed qualification opening accounts a Level 2 officer cannot work at any rate.

The key is the Level 3 commission. It opens armed overnight, tech-campus, high-value, and cleared posts, and combined with the overnight differential it puts these among the best-paid line roles in the market. Firms staff these accounts year round and value reliable, well-documented armed officers.

  • High-value and asset protection posts requiring an armed overnight presence

Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Maintain an armed deterrent presence at the assigned overnight post or patrol route
  • Run armed patrol-driver routes across the Del Valle corridor and multi-site routes, logging each stop
  • Control access and verify credentials at gates and restricted entry points
  • Monitor CCTV and alarms and respond to overnight incidents per site protocol
  • Observe, document, and report, escalating to dispatch and law enforcement as needed
  • Maintain weapon-handling and use-of-force discipline to Level 3 and site standards
  • Complete post logs, patrol records, and incident reports through the shift
  • Maintain radio contact with dispatch throughout the overnight shift

What You Need
