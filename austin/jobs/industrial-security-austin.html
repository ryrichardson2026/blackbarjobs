Overview

Austin Industrial Security: Tesla, Samsung, and the Del Valle Corridor

Austin's industrial base is anchored by Tesla Gigafactory Texas in Del Valle, a 4.3 million square foot campus that is the largest single-site employer in the metro, along with Samsung's Austin and Taylor semiconductor fabs and suppliers like NXP and Applied Materials. These plants run all shifts, and their security programs need consistent gate and patrol coverage around the clock.

Industrial security here is gate-and-patrol work at a large scale, with an intellectual-property edge at the fabs. Officers control truck and employee gates, verify contractors, patrol the plant footprint, and enforce site safety and access rules. Tesla staffs direct security officers at Gigafactory, and firms like Allied Universal run armed patrol-driver posts across the Del Valle corridor.

Access control and documentation are the core output. Officers log every vehicle and contractor, check credentials at the gate, and maintain the records that keep a plant compliant and secure. Prior gate or plant experience and a Level 3 commission move officers toward the higher-paying armed and supervisory posts.

Roles & Duties
Role Types

Types of Industrial Security Roles

  • Gate and access control, processing trucks and contractors and verifying credentials at plant entrances
  • Plant and campus patrol, covering the facility footprint, production areas, and perimeter across the shift
  • Armed patrol-driver, covering the Del Valle corridor and multi-site industrial routes on a Level 3 commission
  • Contractor and vendor management, badging and escorting outside crews on site
  • Console and CCTV monitoring from the plant security center
Duties

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Staff the truck and employee gates, verifying credentials and logging every vehicle and contractor
  • Patrol the plant footprint, production areas, generator yards, and perimeter fencing
  • Enforce site safety, PPE, and access rules across the facility
  • Monitor CCTV, alarm, and fire systems from the plant security center
  • Screen and badge contractors and vendors, escorting outside crews where required
  • Support intellectual-property and access controls at semiconductor and EV plants
  • Complete gate logs, inspection records, and incident reports to plant standards
  • Maintain radio contact with the plant security team throughout the shift

Requirements & Schedules
Requirements

What You Need

  • Valid Texas Level 2 Non-Commissioned Security Officer license, required for unarmed plant posts
  • Level 3 commission for armed industrial and patrol-driver posts, which pay above unarmed rates
  • Valid Texas driver's license, since plant patrol and patrol-driver routes involve vehicle operation
  • Reliable personal transportation, because Del Valle and southeast Austin plant transit is limited
  • A safety mindset and willingness to follow PPE and plant access rules
  • Prior gate, plant, or tech-campus experience preferred for the higher-paying posts
Schedules

Typical Schedules & Environments

  • All-shift 24/7 plant operations, with security coverage across days, swings, and overnights
  • Third shift patrol at plants that run continuous manufacturing
  • 4x10 and 12-hour patterns common at continuous operations
  • Weekend coverage consistently in demand
  • Consistent voluntary overtime, since plant security understaffing is common
  • Weekly pay common at the larger security firms
