What are the major event security venues and events in Austin?+
Moody Center (2001 Robert Dedman Drive, Austin TX 78712) is Austin's primary large-venue event security employer — a 15,000-seat arena on the UT Austin campus that hosts 150+ nights of events per year including major concerts and UT Longhorns women's and men's basketball games. BEST Crowd Management holds the Moody Center security contract, with both part-time and full-time positions available. The venue grossed $89.8 million from October 2024 to September 2025 and was named the highest-grossing midsize venue (10,001–15,000 capacity) by Billboard for 2025. Q2 Stadium is home to Austin FC (MLS) — Andy Frain Services confirmed as the event security provider for Austin FC matches and stadium events. SXSW (South by Southwest), the annual March conference and festival, actively recruits flex unarmed security officers. Austin City Limits Music Festival at Zilker Park runs two October weekends. Formula 1 United States Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas (COTA) runs in October. Moontower Comedy Festival in April. Rodeo Austin in March.

Overview & Duties
Role Overview

What Event Security Officers Do in Austin

BEST Crowd Management at Moody Center is entry screening and crowd flow management at scale. Officers at the 15,000-capacity arena conduct thorough bag checks at entry gates, verify tickets, manage magnetometer lanes, maintain concourse crowd flow during peak periods, and enforce venue policies. On UT basketball game days, the environment is 10,000-seat collegiate sports; on major concert nights, the full 15,000-seat bowl configuration. The range of environments within a single venue — rock concerts, country shows, UT athletic events, family shows — develops the crowd management versatility that Austin's diverse event calendar demands.

SXSW security is distributed across dozens of venues simultaneously — officers work a single venue assignment for a specific SXSW event (a showcase at a club, a keynote session at a convention center, a film screening at a theater), managing venue-specific entry, crowd, and incident protocols in a compressed ten-day high-intensity window. The pace and variety of SXSW security is unlike any single-venue assignment and develops situational awareness and adaptability that standard venue security doesn't replicate.
