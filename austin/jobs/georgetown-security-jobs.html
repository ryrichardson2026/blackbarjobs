What is the Georgetown security market's defining characteristic?+
Georgetown is the county seat of Williamson County — a county with approximately 300,000 residents and one of the fastest-growing in Texas. As county seat, Georgetown hosts county government facilities (courthouse, jail, administrative offices), the Williamson County healthcare system including St. David's Georgetown hospital, and the county's law enforcement infrastructure. This institutional dimension — county corrections, government building security, court security — gives Georgetown a different security market profile than the purely residential and retail suburb markets of Cedar Park or Leander. Additionally, Georgetown sits in the corridor between Austin and Samsung's $17 billion Taylor semiconductor fab 18 miles northeast — the industrial workforce that fab draws will increasingly route through Georgetown's labor market and commercial development.
