Do I need a license before applying or can I get it after?+
Both pathways exist. Getting your Texas Level 2 before applying is faster overall because you become immediately deployable — employers don't have to wait for your license to be active before they put you on a post. The Level 2 is a 6-hour DPS-approved online course available from Defencify Training for $47, plus the TOPS application fee (approximately $40) and IdentoGO fingerprinting (approximately $30), for a total of $117–$170. DPS typically processes Austin applications within 2–4 weeks of fingerprinting. Some Austin employers — including those who explicitly train from zero — will hire you before your license is active and start you on administrative or supervised tasks while your TOPS application processes. GardaWorld's Thursday walk-in hires on the spot with or without a license in some cases. Either way: the sooner you start the TOPS application, the sooner you're working. The 6-hour course can be completed on any device in one sitting.
What do Austin employers actually look for if not experience?+
Availability — specifically overnight and weekend availability. ZipRecruiter Austin security average is $17/hr across all roles, but the fastest-placing candidates in the Austin market are those who express genuine willingness to work Thursday–Sunday evenings or overnight shifts. Professionalism — security employers review clean-cut appearance and basic communication competence before they review work history. A calm, professional phone call or walk-in impression matters more than your resume for entry-level Austin security. Clean background — no felonies, no Class A misdemeanors, no Class B within 5 years. This is non-negotiable across all Austin employers. Reliability indicators — stable address history, references who answer the phone. Austin's contracted security employers deal with high turnover at the entry level and actively want candidates who signal they'll show up consistently. None of these require prior security experience.

Role Overview

What Entry-Level Officers Do in Austin

Entry-level Austin security work is primarily access control, foot patrol, and observation and reporting. You stand or patrol at an assigned post, verify credentials of anyone entering restricted areas, observe and document anything unusual, and write activity reports at the end of your shift. The reporting piece — accurate, factual, time-stamped documentation — is the skill that matters most for career progression. Officers who write clean, professional incident reports move up faster than officers who rely on physical presence alone.

ACT Security Group's rover model — vehicle inspections, building patrols, responding to calls, and driving between accounts — is the most active version of entry-level security work in Austin. It builds mobile patrol competency that makes officers more versatile across account types. GardaWorld's flex model as a new officer means working different accounts each shift, which accelerates the learning curve for post orders, account environments, and client service standards across multiple Austin industry sectors simultaneously.

Common Duties & Responsibilities

  • Respond to alarms, disturbances, and safety concerns per post orders
