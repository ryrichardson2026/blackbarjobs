What makes Austin tech campus security different from standard contracted security?+
Four things. Scale: Tesla's 4.3 million square foot Gigafactory, Google's 804,000-square-foot downtown tower, Amazon's 330,000-square-foot Domain presence, and Apple's expanding Parmer Lane campus all operate at a physical footprint and employee density that generates security complexity standard commercial buildings don't have. Access control systems: tech campuses use enterprise-grade integrated access control — the Identipass, Genetec, and Lenel systems common to major tech employers require officers who are technology-comfortable and can operate and document issues with these platforms. Intellectual property context: tech campus security includes awareness of what is and isn't an authorized removal of materials from the facility — a dimension retail or office park security doesn't involve. Professional expectations: Amazon's 5-day in-office requirement, Apple's in-person culture, and Tesla's fully on-site workforce mean tech campus security officers interact with executives, engineers, and global visitors daily. The professional standard is calibrated to the tech corporate environment.
What is the SpaceX security role in the Austin metro?+
SpaceX has Starlink operations and Austin-area connections, with their Security Operator 3 position confirmed in Bastrop TX — approximately 35 miles east of Austin. The SpaceX Security Operator 3 requires 5 or more years of combined law enforcement/military/armed security experience, US government security clearance, and eligibility to obtain a valid state security officer license. The role covers physical security for critical aerospace and technology infrastructure — a specialized environment requiring the professional and clearance profile that standard contracted security doesn't involve. For Level 3 licensed officers with prior military, law enforcement, or government security backgrounds who live in the Austin metro, SpaceX Bastrop represents the highest-specification tech-adjacent security role in the region.
Which tech companies drive Austin's physical security market?+
Austin's tech campus physical security market is driven by three clusters. The Southeast Austin / Del Valle corridor centers on Tesla Gigafactory — the scale of a 20,000+ employee manufacturing campus generates gate security, perimeter patrol, and internal access control demand that is the largest single tech campus physical security employment environment in Austin. The Domain in North Austin concentrates Amazon (330,000 sqft, 5,000+ employees, 2,000+ being added), IBM (moving into former Meta's 320,000 sqft building July 2026), and smaller tech tenants — AU and GardaWorld both have active Domain-area tech account postings. Downtown Austin brings Google (804,000 sqft Block 185 finally occupied), TikTok (300 Colorado St, 6 floors, 5-day in-office 2026), Oracle (Riverside campus), and Canva. Each cluster has distinct security employment: Domain is access control and lobby professional; downtown is corporate high-rise security; Tesla/Del Valle is industrial campus and perimeter patrol.

What Tech Campus Security Officers Do in Austin

Tesla Security Officer duties at Gigafactory Texas: monitor and patrol a 4.3 million square foot manufacturing campus, resolve or report vehicle, building, security, and safety issues to management, and maintain access control across the facility's multiple entry points and production zones. The scale and industrial character of Gigafactory — Model Y and Cybertruck production running around the clock — means security officers work in an active manufacturing environment with vehicle and pedestrian traffic flows that standard corporate campus security doesn't encounter.

AU Tech Campus Patrol duties at Domain and downtown Austin accounts: monitor and patrol assigned tech office areas, conduct routine patrols, maintain visible presence to deter incidents, support access points and visitor processing, respond to incidents calmly, and document observations in written reports. The tech corporate environment requires customer-service-grade interaction with engineers, executives, and international visitors alongside standard security patrol and access control functions. Digital literacy — operating access control software, filing electronic reports, and using AU's Claim a Shift mobile platform — is the baseline technology competency for all AU Austin tech campus accounts.
